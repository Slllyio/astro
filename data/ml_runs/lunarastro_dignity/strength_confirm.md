## Stage 2 — confirmation (held-out half B), spec = `S4_full_strength_benefic_AD`

- n = 1764 · hit-rate 7.5% vs expected 7.0% · lift **1.082** · z 0.99 · permutation p₁ = **0.175** → **does not replicate** at p<0.05.

## Stage 3 — full corpus + power

- Full-corpus lift **1.111** (n=3530, permutation p₁ = 0.033).
- Power: at the confirmation n=1764 and base exposure 7.2%, detecting an effect at 80% power needs lift ≥ **1.212**; the full corpus needs ≥ **1.15**. Observed full lift 1.111.

## Reading

- If the confirmation half replicates, the dignity/strength effect is real (if tiny) — the one piece of classical timing that carries signal.
- If it does not, note the **power**: a half-corpus cannot resolve a lift near 1.212-and-below, so a null here is *inconclusive*, not disconfirming — the full-corpus permutation p is the more informative read, and it too falls short of significance.
