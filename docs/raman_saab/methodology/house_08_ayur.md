## Main Considerations — cited

1. Judge House + Lord + Occupants + Karaka(Saturn), then the yogas. `HTJAH-II:2890–2893`
2. Repeat the entire judgment in **Navamsa**, and additionally "from the Moon"
   (Chandra-Lagna) — every worked chart does both. `HTJAH-II:2894`, `HTJAH-II:4703`
3. House-placement results of the 8th lord "are general… must be modified on the
   strength of the Ascendant, the Moon and Dasas in operation." `HTJAH-II:3071–3072`
4. For criminal/unrighteous-act predictions, additionally weigh the Ascendant, the
   Moon AND the 10th house (Karmasthana); if those are strong the temptation is
   "nipped at the state of mind and not allowed to manifest." `HTJAH-II:3073–3080`
5. Longevity cannot be pronounced before **12 years** of age (the new-born's life is
   regulated by parents' karma). `HTJAH-II:3916–3923`
6. Do not declare the span from positions alone — "The relative strengths of the
   planets must be balanced before a correct assessment." `HTJAH-II:3477–3479`

### Longevity span-class combinations (large rule bank — pointer)
Raman lists, from "ancient authoritative classical works," explicit yoga banks:
**Balarishta** 29 combinations + an Antidotes block `HTJAH-II:3109–3249`;
**Alpayu** 21 combinations `HTJAH-II:3257–3335`; **Madhyayu** 15 combinations
`HTJAH-II:3343–3398`; **Purnayu** 15 combinations `HTJAH-II:3404–3467`. The
summarising master-rule: span is short / medium / long as the **Lagna-lord & all
benefics** fall in kendras / panaparas / apoklimas respectively, and conversely the
**8th lord & malefics** in kendras / panaparas / apoklimas give short / medium /
long. `HTJAH-II:3469–3474`

## Timing of Fructification

**Governing factors.** Maraka (death-inflicting) planets are graded in three tiers:

- **Primary**: 3rd & 8th are houses of life; their 12ths (the **2nd and 7th**) are
  houses of death. (a) 2nd & 7th lords; (b) malefic occupants of 2nd/7th; (c) planets
  associating with 2nd/7th lords — "planets in conjunction with the 2nd and 7th lords
  are the most powerful in causing death." `HTJAH-II:4494–4509`
- **Secondary**: benefics with 2nd/7th lords (less powerful); lords of 3rd & 8th;
  3rd/8th lord associating with 2nd/7th lord. `HTJAH-II:4510–4519`
- **Tertiary**: Saturn in conjunction/aspect/association with any primary/secondary
  maraka; lord of 6th or 8th; the weakest planet in the chart. `HTJAH-II:4520–4529`

**Dasha rule (8th lord in 6/8/12).** Death can occur (1) in the Dasa & Bhukti of the
8th lord; (2) in the Dasa of the lord of the sign Saturn occupies, Bhukti of the 8th
lord; (3) Dasa of the 8th lord, Bhukti of the 9th lord. `HTJAH-II:4548–4558`.
Strength duel: "If the 8th lord is strong, the lord of Lagna can kill in his period.
If the Lagna lord is strong, the 8th lord can kill in his Dasa." `HTJAH-II:4576–4578`

**Seershodaya / Prushtodaya rule.** For Seershodaya Lagnas (Gemini, Leo, Virgo,
Libra, Scorpio, Aquarius) death falls in periods of the 2nd-lord / Lagna-lord / Rahu
as the Lagna is movable / fixed / common; for Prushtodaya Lagnas (Aries, Taurus,
Cancer, Sagittarius, Capricorn) the Lagna-Drekkana-lord chain is used instead.
`HTJAH-II:4584–4592`

**Transit (gochara) trigger — last signal.** Transits are "catalytic agents…
secondary in importance" and apply only after the maraka Dasa is fixed.
`HTJAH-II:4679–4687`. Saturn the Ayushkaraka/Mrutyukaraka gives the final signal:
death when Saturn transits the houses of the 8th-lord or 12th-lord, or the sign (& its
trines) of the 22nd-drekkana lord / Mandi, or his own natal Rasi+Navamsa or their
trines. `HTJAH-II:4606–4624`, `HTJAH-II:4846–4853`. Jupiter, Sun and Moon transit
triggers are also given (sums of longitudes / trines of the 8th-lord). `HTJAH-II:4626–4659`
