# Fourth House — Sukha / Matru / Bandhu Bhava

> Faithful extraction of B.V. Raman, *How to Judge a Horoscope*, Vol. I,
> Chapter VII "Concerning the Fourth House." Quotes are ≤1 sentence and cited
> as `HTJAH-I:<line>`. Read-only of the actual text; nothing invented.

## Significations (karyas) — cited

Raman opens by listing the fourth's references and warns the significations do
not move in lockstep (a man may be educated yet lose his mother early).

Key framing sentence: "The fourth has reference to mother, immovable property,
education, vehicles and general happiness." `HTJAH-I:4125`

Structural importance: "The fourth, like the tenth, is a very important house and
is in fact the pivot around which revolve some of the special events affecting
the individual's life." `HTJAH-I:4142–4143`

Raman's method note — he deliberately **separates each signification** and judges
them one at a time: "I have endeavoured to deal with each signification of a Bhava
separately." `HTJAH-I:4130–4131`. He then literally partitions the chapter into
**Mother / Education and Learning / Conveyances and Possessions** (`HTJAH-I:4382–4384`).

## Three Pillars — House / Lord / Karaka / Secondary

Raman's Primary Considerations: study the general strength of (a) the Bhava,
(b) the Karaka, (c) its lord and the occupants; then note yogas bearing on the
fourth. `HTJAH-I:4137–4139`. Order of work: "We shall first consider the general
combinations and then the effects of planets severally." `HTJAH-I:4141`

**CRITICAL — the 4th has MULTIPLE karakas, and the matter judged selects the karaka.**
Raman adopts Vaidyanatha Deekshitar's routing explicitly:

> "Vaidyanatha Deekshitar wants us to look to the fourth house and Jupiter for
> judging happiness; the Moon and the fourth for mother; for vehicles and
> ornaments Venus and the fourth Bhava." `HTJAH-I:4386–4388`

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **House** | 4th | 4th (Matru-Sthana) | 4th (Vidya-Sthana) | 4th (Vahana-Sthana) | 4th |
| **Lord** | lord of 4th | lord of 4th | lord of 4th | lord of 4th | lord of 4th |
| **Karaka** | **Jupiter** `HTJAH-I:4386` | **Moon** (Matru-Karaka) `HTJAH-I:4387` | **Jupiter** (Vidya-Karaka) `HTJAH-I:4701–4702, 4761` | **Venus** (Vahana-Karaka) `HTJAH-I:4934` | **Mars** (Griha-Karaka) `HTJAH-I:4969–4970` |

Secondary / supporting indicators Raman actually uses:
- **Mercury** as planet-of-intelligence; "Mercury should be in the fourth" for
  proficiency in astrology `HTJAH-I:4715–4717, 4917`.
- **Venus** as Karaka for music (distinct from his Vahana-Karaka role)
  `HTJAH-I:4768–4769, 4915`.
- **Lord of the fourth from the Moon** (Chandra-Lagna fourth) — listed as a
  governing factor `HTJAH-I:4310` and computed in every worked chart.
- **Matru-Sthana-as-Lagna technique**: treat the 4th house (or Matru-Karaka) as
  the mother's ascendant and judge the **8th therefrom** for her longevity
  `HTJAH-I:4395–4397`.

NOTE for the engine: the project's stated D4/D12/D24 routing is **not** what Raman
invokes here. Raman uses **Rashi + Navamsha (D9) only**. D4/D12/D24 do not appear.

## Main Considerations — cited

1. **Strength triad first.** Study Bhava, Karaka, lord+occupants; flag relevant
   yogas. `HTJAH-I:4137–4139`
2. **General combinations before single planets.** `HTJAH-I:4141`
3. **Do not apply lord-placement results verbatim** — scale them by the 4th
   lord's strength/weakness. "It must be clearly noted that these combinations
   are very general and they should not be applied verbatim." `HTJAH-I:4190–4191`
4. **Satyacharya's gradation rule**: strongly-afflicted 4th lord → intensified
   evil; moderately afflicted → tempered. `HTJAH-I:4193–4198`
5. **Interpretation must be intelligent and skilled, not literal.**
   "In judging any Bhava, one's interpretation must be intelligent and skilled
   and not literal." `HTJAH-I:4200`
6. **Collective, not isolated, judgment.** "Each Bhava must be studied not in an
   isolated manner but as having a bearing on every other part of the horoscope."
   `HTJAH-I:4303–4305`
7. **The dominant planet rule for education**: "Here the planet having to do most
   with the fourth house is to be considered and not merely the house or the
   lord." `HTJAH-I:4721–4722`

## Timing of Fructification

**Governing factors of the 4th house** (`HTJAH-I:4307–4310`):
(a) the lord; (b) planet(s) aspecting the 4th; (c) planet(s) posited in the 4th;
(d) planet(s) aspecting the lord of the 4th; (e) planet(s) in association with
the lord of the 4th; (f) the lord of the 4th from the Moon; (g) the Karaka of the
4th Bhava.

**Dasha rule — strength of the channel** (`HTJAH-I:4312–4322`):
- A planet influences via Dasha (major), Bhukti (sub), or finer divisions.
- **Strongest fructification**: sub-period of a 4th-influencing planet **inside**
  the major period of another 4th-influencing planet → 4th-house results
  "par excellence."
- **Limited fructification**: when only ONE of the major/sub lords touches the
  4th (the other does not), the 4th results appear "only to a limited or even
  nominal extent."
- **"Association" here = aspect AND conjunction.** `HTJAH-I:4321–4322`

**Yoga-carrying rule** (`HTJAH-I:4324–4328`): where a 4th-related lord is in a Raja
yoga or Arishta yoga, it delivers those yoga results (for 4th matters) in its
Dasha/Bhukti. A Rajabhanga-Neecha 4th lord can destroy the 4th's Raja-yoga gains
in its own period — houses/cars lost, properties auctioned.

**Navamsha overlay (D9) — the recurring 6/8/12 test.** In multiple rules the
Rashi promise is checked against the Navamsha: if the 4th lord falls in the
6th/8th/12th **in Navamsha** from the relevant co-lord, the good result reverses
(or, for the 8th-lord case, the evil is mitigated). This Navamsha-6/8/12 test is
Raman's single most-used overlay in the chapter. `HTJAH-I:4354–4373`

**Constellation (nakshatra-lord) rule — Satyacharya.** The constellation a planet
occupies is "of paramount importance especially in the matter of predicting
significant events." `HTJAH-I:4518–4520`. In Chart 64 the Moon in Mars's
constellation lets Mars (a Maraka) kill the mother in his Dasha; in Chart 66 the
Moon in his own constellation could not kill the mother in his own Dasha.

## Engine Notes (for `house_04.py`)

1. **Multi-karaka dispatch is mandatory.** Unlike single-karaka houses, the 4th
   routes the Karaka pillar by the sub-matter under judgment:
   - happiness → **Jupiter**; mother → **Moon (Matru-Karaka)**; education →
     **Jupiter (Vidya-Karaka)**; vehicles → **Venus (Vahana-Karaka)**; houses →
     **Mars (Griha-Karaka)**. (`HTJAH-I:4386–4388, 4701–4702, 4934, 4969`)
   - Also keep **Mercury** as an auxiliary education indicator and **Venus** as a
     distinct *music* karaka. Implement `karaka_for(submatter)`; do not hardcode
     a single `karaka_planet = MOON`.

2. **Varga routing — Raman uses RASHI + NAVAMSHA (D9) ONLY here.** Do NOT route
   D4/D12/D24 in `house_04.py` on the authority of this chapter; the
   project-card's D4/D12/D24 mapping is NOT what Raman invokes. The single
   reusable overlay is the **Navamsha 6/8/12 test**: after a Rashi promise from a
   co-lord conjunction, check the 4th lord's Navamsha position 6/8/12 from that
   co-lord → reversal (or mitigation for the 8th-lord case).
   (`HTJAH-I:4354–4373`)

3. **Reference frames — compute three.** Every worked chart evaluates the 4th
   from (i) **Lagna**, (ii) **Chandra-Lagna** ("lord of the fourth from the
   Moon", `HTJAH-I:4310`), and (iii) **Navamsha**. The `from_moon` 4th-lord and
   Navamsha must be first-class inputs, not afterthoughts.

4. **Matru-Sthana-as-Lagna for mother longevity.** Implement the derived-chart
   trick: treat the 4th house OR Matru-Karaka as the mother's ascendant and judge
   the **8th therefrom**, plus Maraka-from-karaka logic for death timing.
   (`HTJAH-I:4395–4397`)

5. **Maraka + constellation timing for death events.** Mother's-death Dasha/Bhukti
   selection used **Maraka houses (2nd/7th) reckoned from the 4th AND from the
   Matru-Karaka/Moon**, and the **nakshatra-lord (constellation) of the timing
   planet** (Satyacharya: constellation is paramount; a Moon in its own star
   cannot kill in its own Dasha). Encode both the Maraka-from-karaka set and the
   nakshatra-dispositor gate. (`HTJAH-I:4459–4463, 4516–4520, 4621–4624`)
   - **HAZARD — Chart 68 vs 69 death-timing fixture.** Chart 69's outcome line
     ("16th year, Moon Dasha Saturn Bhukti", `HTJAH-I:4572`) is printed immediately
     after the Chart 69 header but **above** the Chart 69 analysis block — a
     page-break artifact that sits adjacent to Chart 68's material. Do NOT mis-wire
     it: Chart 69's *own* conclusion (`HTJAH-I:4584–4586`) gives the death as **age 9,
     Rahu Dasha Rahu Bhukti** (Rahu in the 12th from Matru-Sthana). The 16th-year /
     Moon-Saturn datum belongs to the preceding chart's flow, not to Chart 69's
     fixture. Pin Chart 68 and Chart 69 death events independently.

6. **Dasha-channel strength tiers.** Tier-A (both MD and AD lords touch the 4th by
   aspect OR conjunction) → full results; Tier-B (only one touches) → nominal.
   "Association" must include BOTH aspect and conjunction in the touch-test.
   (`HTJAH-I:4314–4322`)

7. **Satyacharya scaling, not verbatim lookup.** The 12-house lord-placement table
   is a baseline; multiply by 4th-lord strength (strong→augment good,
   afflicted→augment evil; moderate→temper). Never emit the row text raw.
   (`HTJAH-I:4190–4198`)

8. **Saturn-in-4th special case.** Saturn in the 4th is unfavourable for domestic
   life **except when Saturn is Lagnadhipati** (e.g., Aquarius/Capricorn Lagna) or
   when beneficially aspected/associated. Guard against flagging Saturn-4th as
   pure malefic for those Lagnas. (`HTJAH-I:4293–4294`)

   - Jupiter is double-duty (happiness-Karaka AND Vidya-Karaka) — both, not one.
   - The "dominant planet" rule: for education, weight the planet "having to do
