> Faithful extraction of B.V. Raman, *How to Judge a Horoscope*, Vol. II,
> Chapter XI "Concerning the Seventh House." Quotes are ≤1 sentence and cited

marital happiness." `HTJAH-II:200-201`. The classical scope of marriage spans

The 7th is a **maraka (death-dealing) house**: "since the 2nd and 7th are maraka
places…" `HTJAH-II:743-744`; the 7th-lord period "can be a maraka" when the
Lagna lord is feeble (`HTJAH-II:733-734`). For loss-of-partner judgment one must
ALSO read the 8th (mangalya — strength of marital bond) and 9th (sowbhagya — good
fortune incl. a long-lived partner). `HTJAH-II:2050-2052, 2077-2079`

## Three Pillars — House / Lord / Karaka (Venus) / Secondary

Raman's stated method: "due consideration must be given to three important
factors, viz., (a) the House, (b) the Lord, and (c) the Karaka, who in this case
would be Venus." `HTJAH-II:223-225`. **Secondary**: the occupants of the 7th and
the planets associating with the 7th lord. `HTJAH-II:229-230`

| Pillar | What to judge | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| **House** | 7th Bhava from Lagna AND from the Moon; occupants; aspects; benefic/malefic sign | `HTJAH-II:223-224, 353-354, 658-660` |
| **Lord** | 7th lord's placement, strength, aspects, associations; same from the Moon | `HTJAH-II:224, 671-675` |
| **Karaka — VENUS, judged AS A LAGNA** | Venus's dignity (exaltation/own/Gopura/Vaiseshikamsa), AND rules read **FROM Venus**: malefics in 4/8/12 from Venus → wife dies soon; malefics in 7th from Venus → unhappy marriage; Saturn in 6th/8th from Venus → impotency | `HTJAH-II:225, 368-378, 430` |
| **Secondary** | Occupants of the 7th; planets associating with the 7th lord | `HTJAH-II:229-230, 672-675` |

**Karaka-as-Lagna (from-Venus) frame — locked technique.** Raman repeatedly
reckons houses *from Venus* as if Venus were a Lagna:
- "If there are malefics in the 4th, 8th and 12th houses from Venus, or Venus is
  hemmed in between malefics, the wife will die soon after marriage."
  `HTJAH-II:374-376`
- "If there are malefics in the 7th from Venus, the marriage will be unhappy."
  `HTJAH-II:376-377`
- "Saturn in the 6th or the 8th house from Venus" → impotency. `HTJAH-II:430`

**Karako-bhava-nasaya caveat (Venus in 7th).** Ancient writers held Venus (karaka)
in the 7th inhibits the 7th's indications; Raman explicitly rejects this:
"In fact Venus in the 7th is one of the finest combinations for a fairly happy
marriage." `HTJAH-II:2406-2408` (cf. the textual dictum at `HTJAH-II:2402-2406`).

**Note the maraka role.** Because the 7th is a maraka, the SAME placements that
promise marriage can, on affliction + longevity, instead time the death of the
native or the partner. The pillar judgment must carry a maraka flag (see Timing
and Nature-of-Results sections).

## Main Considerations — cited (`HTJAH-II:221`)

1. **Three primary factors + two secondary.** House, Lord, Karaka (Venus);
   secondary = 7th occupants and 7th-lord associates. `HTJAH-II:223-230`
2. **Never apply lord-placement results literally.** "But they must not be applied
   literally to charts without considering the entire chart as a whole after
   assessing the strengths and weaknesses of the several planets."
   `HTJAH-II:343-346`
3. **Judge from BOTH Lagna and the Moon.** The strength/associations of karaka,
   7th house and 7th lord "from the Moon must also be duly weighed."
   `HTJAH-II:658-660`
4. **Combinations are not conclusive in isolation** — weigh natural AND functional
   benefic/malefic status of every aspecting planet. `HTJAH-II:654-658`
5. **Chastity/faithfulness questions demand both horoscopes** be scrutinised
   carefully. `HTJAH-II:663-666`
6. **Background/period/society matter** — "early" vs. "delayed" marriage is
   relative to the times and society of the native. `HTJAH-II:1100-1107`

## Timing of Fructification

**The SEVEN governing factors** (`HTJAH-II:671-676`):
(a) the lord; (b) planet(s) aspecting the 7th house; (c) planet(s) occupying the
7th house; (d) planet(s) aspecting the lord of the 7th; (e) planet(s) in
association with the lord of the 7th; (f) the lord of the 7th from the Moon; and
(g) the karaka of the 7th Bhava (Venus).

These act as lords of Dasas (major), Bhuktis (sub) or Antaras (sub-sub) and the
still-minor periods. `HTJAH-II:680-683`

**TWO-LEVEL MD×AD activation rule** (`HTJAH-II:685-694`):
- **Par excellence / great intensity**: sub-period of a 7th-influencing planet
  INSIDE the major period of another 7th-influencing planet. `HTJAH-II:685-688`
- **Limited extent**: sub-period of a 7th-related planet in the MD of a planet
  NOT related to the 7th — and conversely, an unrelated Bhukti in a 7th-related MD
  — produces 7th-house results "only to a limited extent." `HTJAH-II:688-694`

**Yoga-carrying rule** (`HTJAH-II:696-702`): a 7th lord in a powerful yoga delivers
that yoga's 7th-house results in his Dasa/Bhukti. Severely afflicted 7th → the
7th lord's period can DESTROY the marriage or make it miserable; well fortified →
successful, distinguished, happy marriage.

**Timing-of-marriage selection** (`HTJAH-II:847-867`): rank the candidate lords
(rule records 81-86); the STRONGEST of these gives marriage in his Dasa. Natal
positions/Dasas get **primary** importance; transiting Jupiter (rules 87-88) only
**secondary**. `HTJAH-II:882-883`

## Kuja-Dosha (Mangal-Dosha) numeric grid — `HTJAH-II:2613-2632`

Per-house × per-dignity **dosha-units** matrix used for chart matching. Malefic
ranking: **Mars (worst) > Saturn / Rahu / Ketu > Sun (least)**; maleficence is
highest in debilitation, lowest in exaltation (`HTJAH-II:2607-2611`). The columns
group the malefic-house positions by their base severity: **{8th & 7th}**,
**{4th}**, **{12th & 2nd}**.

| Dignity of the malefic | 8th & 7th: Mars | 8th & 7th: Saturn/Rahu/Ketu | 8th & 7th: Sun | 4th: Mars | 12th & 2nd: Saturn/Rahu/Ketu | 12th & 2nd: Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Debility | 100 | 75 | 50 | 50 | 37.50 | 25 |
| Inimical | 90 | 67.50 | 45 | 45 | 33.75 | 22.50 |
| Neutral | 80 | 60.00 | 40 | 40 | 30.00 | 20.00 |
| Friendly | 70 | 52.50 | 35 | 35 | 26.25 | 17.50 |
| Own | 60 | 45.00 | 30 | 30 | 22.50 | 15.00 |
| Exaltation | 50 | 37.50 | 25 | 25 | 18.75 | 12.50 |

> Source layout note: the printed table (`HTJAH-II:2613-2618`) compresses the
> header into two physical rows — "8th and 7th / 4th / 12th and 2nd" over the
> malefic names "Mars Saturn Sun Mars Saturn Sun" with "Rahu/Ketu" stacked under
> "Saturn". Each grouped position (8th/7th, 4th, 12th/2nd) shares one severity
> column, so the 7th & 8th carry identical units, as do the 2nd & 12th.

**Matching rule** (`HTJAH-II:2626-2632`): sum each chart's total dosha units; if
the two totals are equal or nearly so, the matching is good. If the **male** chart
exceeds the **female** chart's dosha by **≤25 %**, it is passable. If the male
exceeds that percentage, OR the **female chart has more dosha**, the charts cannot
be matched.

## Engine Notes (for `house_07.py`)

1. **Karaka-as-Lagna (from-Venus) frame is mandatory.** Venus is a single karaka
   here (unlike the 4th), but several rules are read FROM VENUS as if Venus were a
   Lagna: malefics in **4/8/12 from Venus** → wife dies soon (`HTJAH-II:374-376`);
   malefics in **7th from Venus** → unhappy marriage (`HTJAH-II:376-377`); Saturn
   in **6th/8th from Venus** → impotency (`HTJAH-II:430`); benefics in **2/7/11
   from the 7th lord** → spouse happiness (`HTJAH-II:359-361`). Implement a
   `houses_from(planet)` helper and run the malefic/benefic-placement tests with
   Venus (and the 7th lord) as alternate reference points, not only the Lagna.

2. **Three reference frames — Lagna, Chandra-Lagna, Navamsa.** Every worked chart
   computes the 7th from (i) **Lagna**, (ii) **the Moon** ("7th lord from the
   Moon" is governing factor (f), `HTJAH-II:675`), and (iii) **Navamsa (D9)**.
   The `from_moon` 7th-house/lord and the D9 Venus/7th-lord dignity are first-class
   inputs.

3. **D9/D7 routing.** D9 (Navamsa) is the spouse-varga Raman actually invokes
   (every chart): Venus/7th-lord Navamsa dignity, malefic-Navamsa-of-dispositor
   test, even/odd-Navamsa impotency tests, and the **6/8/12-from-Navamsa-Lagna
   intensifier**. **D7 (Saptamsa)** is the project's progeny-via-spouse varga but
   is NOT separately invoked in this chapter's text — route it for progeny
   sub-judgments only, citing other sources, not HTJAH-II.

4. **Two-level MD×AD timing.** Tier-A = AD of a 7th-influencing planet INSIDE the
   MD of another 7th-influencing planet → results "par excellence"; Tier-B = only
   one of MD/AD touches the 7th → "limited extent." Encode the seven governing
   factors (`HTJAH-II:671-676`) as the membership set for "7th-influencing,"
   including the **lord-of-7th-from-the-Moon** and **Venus**.

5. **Marriage-timing candidate ranking.** Build the candidate-lord list (rule
   records 81-86): {Rasi-dispositor of 7th lord, Navamsa-dispositor of 7th lord,
   Venus, Moon, Navamsa-dispositor of 2nd lord, 9th lord, 10th lord, 7th lord,
   7th occupant, 7th aspecter}. Pick the **strongest by aspect/position/lordship**;
   apply DELAY gates (Saturn aspect on 7th/7th-lord/Venus; 6/8/12-lord touch).
   Natal Dasa primary; transit-Jupiter (rules 87-88) secondary only.

6. **Maraka flag — branch on longevity before emitting death.** The 7th is a
   maraka. The SAME 7th-lord/2nd-lord period that could give wealth-via-marriage
   can instead time death OR, **if longevity is good, deliver "separation +
   mental agony" instead of death** (`HTJAH-II:743-744`). For loss-of-partner,
   also evaluate the **8th (mangalya) and 9th (sowbhagya)**, and Maraka-from-7th
   reckoning (e.g., 2nd-from-7th planet kills the spouse, Chart 24).

7. **Even/odd-sign & even/odd-Navamsa parity engine.** The impotency cluster
   (records 30-33, `HTJAH-II:432-443`) and the even-sign "good wife" rule (record
   5) require a **sign-parity (odd/even) and Navamsa-parity** predicate over
   specific planet pairs (Sun-Moon, Mars-Sun, Saturn-Mercury) WITH mutual-aspect.
   Build a `parity(planet)` + `mutual_aspect(a,b)` pair.

8. **Dual-sign = multiple marriages signature.** "7th house or Venus in dual
   (common) signs gives more than one marriage" (`HTJAH-II:996-997`). The
   strongest single discriminator across Charts 6, 7, 17, 18, 19, 20: **7th lord
   AND/OR Venus in common signs (Gemini/Virgo/Sag/Pisces) or common Navamsas** →
   ≥2 marriages — UNLESS a holding factor (Jupiter↔Venus mutual aspect, Chart 1)
   collapses it to one. Encode the exception, or you will over-predict remarriage.

9. **Venus-in-7th is NOT karako-bhava-nasaya.** Do NOT penalise Venus in the 7th;
   Raman calls it "one of the finest combinations for a fairly happy marriage"
   (`HTJAH-II:2406-2408`). Charts 8 & 21 show it can still be redeemed (Yogakaraka,
   fixed sign) or afflicted (papakartari) — judge by Venus's own affliction state,
   not by the bare placement.

10. **Gotchas:**
    - **Lagna-vs-Moon frame disagreement is informative, not an error** — Chart 5
      stays married only because Jupiter aspects the 7th-from-Moon though the
      7th-from-Lagna and from-Venus are afflicted. Surface BOTH frames.
    - **Kuja Dosha must be checked from THREE points** (Asc, Moon, Venus) and by
      **Bhava not Rasi**, with the sign-exception table and Mars+Jupiter / Mars+Moon
      neutraliser (`HTJAH-II:2583-2601`). Do not hardcode "Mars in 7th = dosha."
    - **Maraka reckoned from the 7th-as-spouse-Lagna**: in Chart 24 Saturn is the
      2nd-from-7th maraka that kills the husband — implement spouse-maraka via
      2nd/7th counted FROM the 7th house.
    - **Women's vs men's charts differ** for some rules (record 52: Moon+Saturn in
      7th → remarriage for a woman, denial for a man; records 69-70 split by sex).
      Carry the native's sex as an input.
    - **Subhakartari (benefic hem) can soften a malefic-occupied 7th** (Chart 23) —
      the papakartari/subhakartari hem test must run on the 7th house AND the 7th
      lord AND Venus.
    - **neechabhanga / parivartana / Vargottama** repeatedly rescue or weaken the
      pillars (Charts 11, 12, 13, 22, 23, 25, 32) — these dignity modifiers must
      gate the raw lord-placement verdicts (cf. the `HTJAH-II:343-346` literal-
      application warning).
    - **Background/era scaling for "delay"** (`HTJAH-II:1100-1107`) — "delayed
      marriage" is relative; do not emit an absolute age threshold.
