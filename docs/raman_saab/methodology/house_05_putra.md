## Three Pillars — House / Lord / Karaka (Jupiter / Putra-Karaka) / Secondary

Raman names the triad explicitly: "In analysing the fifth Bhava, due
consideration must be given to the three important factors, viz., (a) the house,
(b) the lord, and (c) the Karaka, which in this case would be Jupiter."
`HTJAH-I:5018–5019`

| Pillar | What to judge | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| **House** | The 5th Bhava itself — sign on Bhava-madhya, its tatwa, occupants, aspects | `HTJAH-I:5018`, `5026–5027` |
| **Lord** | Lord of the 5th — its placement, strength, association, Navamsha | `HTJAH-I:5019`, `5108–5111` |
| **Karaka** | **Jupiter** = Putra-Karaka; judge his sign, aspects, Papakarthari, own-house | `HTJAH-I:5019`, `5619–5620`, `5727` |
| **Secondary** | Occupants of the 5th; associates joining the 5th lord and the Karaka; the Yoga-Karakas bearing on the 5th | `HTJAH-I:5020–5022` |

**How to judge the Karaka Jupiter specifically** — the recurring tests Raman
applies to Jupiter in the worked charts:
- Is Jupiter in his **own house** (Sagittarius/Pisces)? → strong Karaka
  (`HTJAH-I:5727`, `5777`, `5826`).
- Is Jupiter under **Papakarthari Yoga** (hemmed between malefics)? → blemished
  (`HTJAH-I:5620`, `5658`, `5734`).
- Does Jupiter occupy a **malefic sign** (Scorpio etc.)? → blemished
  (`HTJAH-I:5620`, `5641`, `5813`).
- Is Jupiter in a **trikona / 9th / Kendra** from the 5th? → favourable
  (`HTJAH-I:5681`, `5701`).
- Is Jupiter **aspected by / conjoined Mars or Saturn or Rahu**? → rendered
  malefic (`HTJAH-I:5701–5702`, `5778`).
- **Dual-role caution** for Jupiter: he is a natural benefic but may be a
  *malefic lord* by ownership — balance both. "He may become a malefic lord by
  virtue of ownership of certain houses." `HTJAH-I:5790–5791`. The astrologer
  must weigh the natural-benefic effect against the malefic-lord effect
  (`HTJAH-I:5780–5793`).

**Direct karaka-quality rules** (not just chart-derived):
- "Jupiter in the 5th gives good intellect and memory." `HTJAH-I:5226`
- Jupiter in the 5th: "Learned in logic and law, mantrasastra, highly
  intelligent… great discriminating power… A number of children." `HTJAH-I:5248–5250`

## Main Considerations — cited

1. **Three pillars + secondaries first.** House, lord, Karaka (Jupiter); then
   occupants, associates of the 5th lord and Karaka, and the relevant
   Yoga-Karakas. `HTJAH-I:5018–5022`
2. **Read the emotional nature from the sign's tatwa** on the Bhava-madhya
   before the children-combinations (a distinctive 5th-house pre-pass).
   `HTJAH-I:5024–5027`
3. **General combinations are general** — "they have to be used with great care
   paying particular attention to the benefic, malefic, or moderating nature of
   the lord." `HTJAH-I:5173–5175`. Likewise the culled combinations require
   "the skill and intelligence of the astrologer." `HTJAH-I:5180–5181`
4. **Fertility (Beeja / Kshetra) MUST be checked before the house** — "Before
   the 5th house is examined, it is very essential to find out the sterility or
   otherwise of the marrying couple." `HTJAH-I:5497–5498`. Even a strong 5th
   gives no issues without Beeja/Kshetra strength. `HTJAH-I:5499–5500`
5. **Judge BOTH spouses' horoscopes** — "it is essential that the horoscopes of
   both the husband and wife are considered from the point of view of fertility
   or sterility." `HTJAH-I:5436–5437`; the husband's afflicted 5th "is made good
   by a favourable disposition in the case of the wife and vice versa."
   `HTJAH-I:5968–5970`
6. **Analysis then synthesis** — split each combination to find each
   conjunction/aspect's effect, then build a co-ordinated whole.
   `HTJAH-I:5871–5874`
7. **Judge from Lagna AND Chandra-Lagna AND Navamsha** — every worked chart
   re-reads the 5th "from Chandra Lagna" and in the Navamsha.
   `HTJAH-I:5322`, `5630`, `5648`.

## Timing of Fructification

**Whether the 5th-lord Dasha will even occur** — first ask if the native will
enjoy the Dasha of the 5th lord in his lifetime; if not, fall back to the
planets influencing the 5th by occupation/aspect and whether *their* Dashas
occur. `HTJAH-I:5310–5313`

**Governing factors of the 5th house** (`HTJAH-I:5315–5323`):
(a) the lord; (b) planet(s) aspecting the 5th; (c) planet(s) posited in the 5th;
(d) planet(s) aspecting the lord of the 5th; (e) planet(s) in association with
the lord of the 5th; (f) **the lord of the 5th from the Moon AND the Karaka
(Jupiter)** of the 5th Bhava.

**Dasha-channel strength rule** (`HTJAH-I:5325–5333`) — intensity is a function
of *both* period lords:
- Sub-period of a 5th-influencing planet **inside** the major period of another
  5th-influencing planet → 5th-house results **"par excellence."**
- Sub-period of a 5th-associated planet inside the major period of a
  *non*-associated planet → results **"to a limited extent only."**
- Sub-period of a non-associated lord inside the major period of an associated
  lord → results **"only to a limited or nominal extent."**

These factors operate as lords of main periods (Dasha), sub-periods (Bhuktis),
or still-minor periods. `HTJAH-I:5325–5326`

**Navamsha overlay — the recurring 6/8/12 test.** As in House 4, a Rashi promise
from a co-lord conjunction is checked against the Navamsha: if the 5th lord is
6/8/12 **in Navamsha** from the relevant co-lord, the good reverses (or, for the
8th-lord case, the evil is minimised). `HTJAH-I:5345–5347, 5358–5359, 5411`

**Navamsha for COUNTING children.** "The number of children to be born is
guessed by the number of Navamsha gained by the 5th house or 5th lord. If the
5th lord is aspected by Jupiter or Venus, the number is doubled. Malefics
occupying the intervening Navamsha denote possible losses." `HTJAH-I:5902–5904`.
Vargottama / Bhava-midpoint-in-same-Navamsha can **treble** the count
(`HTJAH-I:5934–5936`).

**Beeja / Kshetra (fertility) special computation** (`HTJAH-I:5517–5527`):
- **Beeja-sphuta (male seed)** = (Sun + Venus + Jupiter) longitudes, mod 360°;
  must fall in an **odd sign AND odd Navamsha**, aspected/associated by benefics.
- **Kshetra-sphuta (female bed)** = (Mars + Moon + Jupiter) longitudes, mod 360°;
  must fall in an **even sign AND even Navamsha**, aspected/associated by benefics.
- Evil/impotent planets must not aspect these points; no evil planet in the 5th
  *from* Beeja/Kshetra; the point must not fall in a malefic sign even if
  friendly; **Rahu must never join** them. `HTJAH-I:5524–5527`
