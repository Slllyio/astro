## Three Pillars — House / Lord / Karaka / Secondary

Primary Considerations: study the general strength of (a) the Bhava, (b) its lord,
(c) the karaka, and (d) the occupants; "Yogas in the chart bearing on the 11th
house must also be taken into account." `HTJAH-II:14246–14250`. Order of work:
"We shall first study the general combinations and the effects of planets …
severally." `HTJAH-II:14252–14253`

| Pillar | For GAINS / WEALTH | For ELDER BROTHER / CO-BORNS |
|---|---|---|
| **House** | 11th (Labha-Sthana) | 11th |
| **Lord** | lord of 11th | lord of 11th |
| **Karaka** | **Jupiter** (Dhanakaraka, "can also be taken as the karaka for labha or gains") `HTJAH-II:15155` | **Mars** ("karaka for 'brothers' according to Vaidyanatha Dikshitar") `HTJAH-II:14742–14744` |
| **Secondary** | occupants of 11th; planet associating with the 11th lord (source-of-gains); 2nd-house factors (see below) | benefic/malefic aspects & associations on the 11th house and 11th lord |

**CRITICAL — 11th-lord is the WORST functional malefic in Raman's scheme, but
this chapter treats it doctrinally as the matter's own lord.** The chapter never
softens the lord's natural-malefic-of-lordship status: it repeatedly calls the
11th lord's lordship "malefic." Examples in-text: Jupiter's "malefic lordship is
turned into a benefic yoga" only by Vipareeta-Raja association `HTJAH-II:15278–15281`;
Saturn's "influence as a malefic lord on the 11th house must give a limited number
of elder brothers" `HTJAH-II:15034–15035`; and a combust 11th-lord Jupiter whose
"malefic lordship can gain an upper hand during adverse planetary periods"
crashed the native's empire in Ketu Dasa `HTJAH-II:16104–16111`. → The pillar is
judged for the 11th's significations, but the engine must carry the lord's
functional-malefic flag because it explains the reversals (loss in the lord's own
adverse period).

**No karaka for elder brother as such.** "Although there is no karaka as such for
elder brothers Mars … can also be considered for the 11th house." `HTJAH-II:14742–14744`

**Secondary frames actually used in every chart:** the 11th reckoned from the
**Moon** (Chandra-Lagna), and the **Navamsa** (Navamsa-Lagna 6/8/12 test; Navamsa
co-born count). The "11th lord from the Moon" is an explicit timing factor
`HTJAH-II:14504, 14536`.

## Main Considerations — cited (Primary Considerations)

1. **Strength of four factors + yogas.** Study Bhava, lord, karaka, occupants;
   weigh yogas bearing on the 11th. `HTJAH-II:14246–14250`
2. **General combinations before single planets.** `HTJAH-II:14252–14253`
3. **Do not apply lord-placement results verbatim.** "These results are very
   general and must not be applied directly without weighing other factors."
   `HTJAH-II:14344–14345`
4. **Satyacharya penury rule.** If the 11th lord is in an evil constellation —
   the 3rd, 5th or 7th from that occupied by the Moon — the native suffers penury
   and misery. `HTJAH-II:14345–14348`
5. **Benefics augment, malefics diminish.** "Benefics in the 11th house augment
   the significations of the Bhava while malefics diminish them both qualitatively
   and quantitatively." `HTJAH-II:14351–14353`
6. **The 11th necessarily comprises the 2nd.** "The 11th house necessarily
   comprises the 2nd also so that timing … will have to be made taking into
   account the 2nd house factors as well." `HTJAH-II:15159–15162` — gains are
   anticipated in periods of 2nd-house planets/lord/aspecters too `HTJAH-II:15164–15169`.
7. **Source of gains = nature of planet in the 11th** (or the planet associating
   with the 11th lord). `HTJAH-II:14373, 14604–14606`
8. **Financial gains depend on more than the 11th** — also a strong Lagna, the
   9th (fortune), the 10th (profession) and the 2nd (wealth). `HTJAH-II:15392–15394`

## Timing of Fructification

**Governing factors of the 11th house** (`HTJAH-II:14496–14504`):
(a) lord of the 11th; (b) planet(s) aspecting the 11th; (c) planet(s) occupying
the 11th; (d) planet(s) aspecting the 11th lord; (e) planet(s) in association
with the 11th lord; (f) the 11th lord from the Moon.

**Dasha rule — strength of the channel** (`HTJAH-II:14506–14518`):
- These factors operate as lords of the main period (MD) or of sub-periods (AD).
- **Par excellence**: sub-period of an 11th-influencer **inside** the major period
  of another 11th-influencer → 11th-house results "par excellence."
- **Limited extent**: AD of a planet associated with the 11th in the MD of a
  planet NOT associated → results "only to a limited extent."
- **Feeble degree**: AD of a planet not associated, in the MD of a planet
  associated with the 11th lord → results "only to a feeble degree."

**Gains-timing (with the 2nd-house overlay)** (`HTJAH-II:15164–15169`): gains in
the MD/AD of any planet in the 11th, the 11th lord, or a planet aspecting the 11th
house or 11th lord — AND in the MD/AD of any planet occupying/aspecting/owning the
**2nd house** (because "the 11th house necessarily comprises the 2nd also"
`HTJAH-II:15159–15162`).

**Dasa of the 11th lord** is treated as its own block (rules 41–54 above, in
"Fulfilment of ambitions") — the lord's house-placement keys the period's events.
`HTJAH-II:14612–14736`

## Nature of Results (dasha-phala) — fortified vs afflicted

General principle: "The exact nature of results produced by a planet who is
capable of influencing the 11th house in its Dasa … or Bhukti is qualified by its
ownership/aspect and position." `HTJAH-II:14568–14570`. Predictions on acquisition
of money should be made after assessing the strength of the 11th house and 11th
lord, with the source read from the planet in the 11th or associating with the
11th lord. `HTJAH-II:14602–14606`

**Fortified vs afflicted gating** (`HTJAH-II:15164–15174`): in the periods of the
11th/2nd-influencers, gains accrue when those planets are well-disposed; the SAME
planets, when afflicted/combust/depressed/eclipsed/with inimical planets, produce
**loss** of the 11th-Bhava significations instead.

**Navamsa overlays (D9):**
- The recurring **6/8/12-from-Navamsa-Lagna test**: 11th lord in the 6th/8th/12th
  from Navamsa Lagna → moderate results (rule 44), or runs into debts (rule 54).
  `HTJAH-II:14628–14629, 14735–14736`
- **Navamsa fortification mitigates malefic outcomes**: child-affliction "troubles
  will be minimised if the 11th lord is well fortified in the Navamsa."
  `HTJAH-II:14657–14658`

## Engine Notes (for `house_11.py`)

1. **Two karakas, dispatched by sub-matter.** Route the Karaka pillar:
   - **gains / wealth / fulfilment of ambitions → Jupiter** (Dhanakaraka, explicit
     karaka for labha). `HTJAH-II:15155`
   - **elder brother / co-borns → Mars** (Vaidyanatha Dikshitar's karaka for
     "brothers"; there is *no* dedicated elder-brother karaka). `HTJAH-II:14742–14744`
   Implement `karaka_for(submatter)`; do not hardcode a single karaka. Note Chart
   209 labels Jupiter as the karaka *because there Jupiter is itself the 11th lord*
   — that is a coincidence of that chart, not a general rule.

2. **Carry the 11th-lord-as-worst-functional-malefic flag.** The project scheme
   makes the 11th lord the worst functional malefic; Raman's text agrees ("malefic
   lordship," `HTJAH-II:15278, 15035, 16104`). The flag is what explains the
   *reversal* pattern: a chart strong for gains can still **lose everything in the
   11th lord's own adverse Dasa** when that lord is combust/eclipsed/in a malefic
   constellation (Chart 232: combust Jupiter → crash in Ketu Dasa; Chart 230: Mars
   eclipsed by Rahu → abdication). Encode: even after a positive gains verdict,
   emit a "non-durability / reversal risk" sub-flag keyed on the 11th lord's
   combustion/eclipse/constellation affliction.

3. **The 2nd-house overlay is mandatory for gains timing.** "The 11th house
   necessarily comprises the 2nd also." Gains timing must scan 2nd-house
   planets/lord/aspecters in addition to the six 11th-factors.
   (`HTJAH-II:15159–15169`)

4. **Reference frames — compute three.** Every worked chart evaluates the 11th
   from (i) **Lagna**, (ii) **Chandra-Lagna** (the explicit "11th lord from the
   Moon" timing factor, `HTJAH-II:14504, 14536`), and (iii) **Navamsa**. The
   `from_moon` 11th-lord and the Navamsa must be first-class inputs.

5. **Navamsa is the only varga — implement four distinct D9 tests:**
   - **6/8/12-from-Navamsa-Lagna**: 11th lord there → moderate results / debts.
     (`HTJAH-II:14628–14629, 14735–14736`)
   - **Navamsa fortification mitigates** malefic outcomes (child-loss minimised).
     (`HTJAH-II:14657–14658`)
   - **Navamsa-number of the strongest 11th-planet** = count of elder co-borns;
     malefic aspect on that planet subtracts co-borns. (`HTJAH-II:14946–14950`)
   - **Navamsas-gained** by the 11th lord governs co-born *survival* (7 amsas →
     survival; 1 amsa → denial). (`HTJAH-II:15088–15089, 15143–15145`)
   Do NOT route D4/D12/D24 here — Raman invokes Rasi + Navamsa only.

6. **Co-born count algorithm (with caveat).** "One theory": #(planets in 11th +
   12th) ≈ #elder co-borns; Raman explicitly says it "cannot be applied verbatim"
   and must be qualified. In practice he **averages the Lagna count and the Moon
   count**, ignores Rahu/Ketu as shadowy, then adjusts by Navamsa
   number/strength and malefic aspects. Encode as: `avg(count_from_lagna,
   count_from_moon)` (nodes excluded) → adjust by Navamsa survival/strength.
   (`HTJAH-II:14755–14759, 14870–14886, 14944–14950, 15083–15093`)

7. **Source-of-gains keyed to the 11th occupant (and to the 11th lord's house).**
   Two distinct lookup tables exist: (a) planet *in the 11th* → income source
   (rules 24–30); (b) when the **11th lord is in the 11th**, the *lord's* nature →
   business type (rule 53). Keep them separate; both are planet-keyed.
   (`HTJAH-II:14373–14386, 14714–14729`)

8. **Death-timing (co-borns) uses Maraka-from-11th and Maraka-from-11th-lord.**
   Every "brother died" verdict picks the Dasa/Bhukti via maraka houses (2nd/7th)
   reckoned from BOTH the 11th house and the 11th lord, plus the constellation of
   the timing planet. (Chart 210: Saturn 7th-lord-from-11th; Chart 211: Mercury 2nd
   from 11th & 12th from 11th lord; Chart 212: papakartari from both lords.)
   (`HTJAH-II:14811–14814, 14863–14868, 14937–14942`)

9. **Gains depend on the wider wealth-quartet, not the 11th alone.** Weight Lagna,
   9th (fortune), 10th (profession) and 2nd (wealth) alongside the 11th when
   scoring magnitude of gains — this is what separates Chart 220/221 (one of the
   wealthiest) from Chart 222 (rich but middling). (`HTJAH-II:15392–15394, 15510–15514`)

    - **Satyacharya penury rule** is constellation-based (11th lord in 3rd/5th/7th

    - The **deafness/ear-affliction cluster** (rules 32–36) is a domain-specific

    - **Vipareeta-Raja / yoga turning malefic-lordship benefic** (Chart 218) — the

    - **Parivartana sets** that signal gains: 2nd↔5th, 2nd↔11th, 5th↔9th — distinct

    - **Chart 209 is a method-fixture (timing-factor identification), not a

    - Charts **230–232 are reversal fixtures** ("Loss or Gain"): strong gains-yoga
