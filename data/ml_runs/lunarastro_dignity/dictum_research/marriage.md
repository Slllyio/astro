## ⚑ Headline scholarly finding
**In BPHS itself, the 7th lord's dasha is a MARAKA, not a marriage dasha**: "There will be distress to wife and the possibility of death of the native in the Dasha of the Lord of Yuvati" (**BPHS 48.5–8**); 2L & 7L "are all known as Marakas" (**BPHS 44.2–5**). The "marriage in 7L dasha" dictum enters with **Phaladeepika 10.13/20.8** and **Jataka Parijata 14.29**. Parashara's own marriage timing = fixed-age yogas (Ch. 18) + conditional benefic antardashas (Ch. 52–60). Any test suite should split "7L dasha → marriage" (Phaladeepika-stream) from "benefic AD in good shape → marriage" (BPHS-stream).

## A. Dasha selection rules (which period gives marriage)

- **A1. Phaladeepika 10.13**: "Marriage is likely during the dasha of the planet (1) occupying the 7th, (2) aspecting the 7th, or (3) the lord of the 7th."
- **A2. Phaladeepika 20.8**: 7H endowed with full strength → in 7L's dasa "auspicious functions like marriage will be celebrated in his house."
- **A3/A4. The navamsa-dispositor rule — Phaladeepika 10.14 = Jataka Parijata 14.28** (*kalatra-nātha-sthita-bhāṁśakeśayoḥ sita-kṣapānāyakayor balīyasaḥ daśāgame…*): marriage in the dasha of the **strongest of {rāśi-dispositor(7L), navamsa-dispositor(7L), Venus, Moon}** — with Jupiter transiting trine to 7L's sign/navamsa (C2). ⚑ Popular attribution to Uttara Kalamrita NOT located — the rule is JP→Phaladeepika.
- **A5. Jataka Parijata 14.29**: marriage in the dasha/bhukti of: dispositor of Venus-joined-7th-sign; dispositor of the 2L's sign; 10L and 8L respectively; **the planet conjoined the 7th lord or placed in the 7th house**.
- **A6. Phaladeepika 19.26**: Venus MD → "acquisition of wife, jewels, clothes…"
- **A7. BPHS 47.79–82**: Venus in mūlatrikoṇa → in Venus's dasha "celebration of marriage in the family."
- **A8.** Venus = marriage karaka: Phaladeepika 2.6; UK Kanda 5 karaka list ("(4) Marriage… (11) Wife or husband").
- **A9. Jataka Parijata 14.30** (within-dasha tempo): marriage-giving benefic in benefic sign → event at dasha **beginning**; benefic in malefic sign → **middle**; malefic in malefic sign → **end**.
- **A10. Jataka Tattva 7H sutras 125–127**: early marriage if 1L near 7L; benefic near 1L/7L; strong 7L in 2H or in Pārāvata-amsa.
- **A11. Prasna Marga Ch. XVII** (vivāha praśna): marriage by kāryeśa (7L) + kāraka (Venus) dasha/bhukti/gochara. [flagged: translation summaries]
- **A12. Sarvartha Chintamani** kalatra section, early marriage ślokas 753–755. [flagged: attributed]

## C. Transit triggers — classical vs modern

- **C1. Phaladeepika 10.12**: marriage when **Venus or 7L transits a sign trine to the sign/navamsa of the Lagna lord**.
- **C2. Phaladeepika 10.14 / JP 14.28**: …when **Jupiter transits trine to the sign or navamsa occupied by the 7th lord** — the Jupiter-transit trigger IS classical, in this specific form.
- **C3. Phaladeepika 10.13b**: …when the **Lagna lord transits the 7H sign**.
- **C4. JP 14.31**: Jupiter transiting the house of the 7L or of Venus → obtainment of wife.
- **C5. Saturn+Jupiter "double transit" on 1H/7H/7L → modern (K.N. Rao**, by his own statement). FLAG.
- **C6.** Ashtakavarga-bindu marriage timing: no classical verse located. FLAG.

## E. Jaimini stream (Darakaraka, Upapada)

- **E1.** Chara-karaka scheme; **DK** = lowest-degree planet (BPHS 32.13–21; JS 1.2). Constant karaka: "From Ketu note wife."
- **E2.** **UL** = ārūḍha of the 12th (JS 1.4.1 *upapadaṁ padaṁ pitranucarāt*; BPHS 30.1–6; odd/even variant).
- **E3.** UL with/aspected by benefic → full spouse happiness; afflicted without relief → "ascetic, goes without a wife" (BPHS 30.1–6 = JS 1.4.2–4).
- **E4.** **2nd from UL**: debilitated occupant → destruction of wife; exalted → many wives; Gemini there → many wives; own-lord → wife dies only at advanced age (BPHS 30.7–12 = JS 1.4.5–8).
- **E5.** **Saturn+Rahu in 2nd-from-UL → loses wife through calumny or death** (BPHS 30.16); Rangacharya's gloss: divorce probable. 
- **E6.** Dignity of UL-lord/Venus/7L → spouse family quality & loss-timing (BPHS 30.13–15).
- **E7.** Read spouse from Lagna, Lagna-pada, **7th from UL** & lords — "so say Narada and others" (BPHS 30.23).
- **E8.** 7th from **Karakamsa**: Rahu → "widow in marriage"; Moon+Jup → very beautiful wife; Venus → sensuous (BPHS 33.46–48).
- **E9.** Chara-dasha marriage timing: rasi-dasha of sign holding DK/Ketu/Venus, 7th from Lagna/AL, **UL**, 2nd-from-UL, **A7** — ⚑ commentary tradition (Rangacharya), not a located sūtra.
- **E10.** JS 1.3.30 *svasthe dārāḥ* — disputed interpretation, low confidence.
- **E11.** 2nd-from-UL planet pairs → spouse ailments (BPHS 30.17–22 = JS 1.4.9–15).

## F. Delay / denial

- **F1.** BPHS 18.2: 7L in 6/8/12 → wife sickly. ⚑ The popular "7L in 6/8/12 **delays** marriage" is an inference (this + PD 20.14); no classical verse says "delay" verbatim.
- **F2.** PD 20.14: dasha lord in 6/8/12 gives no good results — gates A2.
- **F3.** **PD 10.4**: deprived of wife if 7H/7L malefic-associated, hemmed, debilitated, inimical, **or combust** (the Venus/7L-combust denial rule); reinforced 10.15.
- **F4.** **Brihat Jataka 24.8**: female — Saturn in 7H aspected by malefics → "becomes old without marriage" (the classical root of "Saturn delays marriage"); echoed Saravali 46.15–17.
- **F5.** PD 10.2: weak Moon in 5H + malefics in 1/7/12 → without wife and children.
- **F6.** **PD 10.5**: "Mars and Saturn in the 7th from Venus and Moon → wifeless and childless" (the Venus/Moon-relative frames).
- **F7.** PD 10.2: 7L in 5H → loses wife/sonless; 5L/8L in 7H → wife does not survive.
- **F8.** UL afflicted (E3) → denial/asceticism.
- **F9.** Empty + unaspected weak 7H → mean husband / no marriage (BJ 24.8a; Saravali 46.15).
- **F10.** UK 2nd Kanda v.19 region: malefics in 4th & 8th **from Venus** (or 7L), Venus hemmed → destructive for the match. [Sastri flags the verse an interpolation]

## G. Plurality / second marriage / divorce

- **G1.** BPHS 18.19–21: 7L fallen w/ malefic + eunuch-planet 7H/navamsa → two wives; Mars+Venus in 7H or Saturn in 7H + 1L in 8H → three; Venus in dual rasi + strong 7L → many. Also 18.6.
- **G2.** PD 10.5: 7L and Venus both in dual sign/amsa → two wives; count of wives = planets conjunct 7L & Venus.
- **G3.** Jataka Tattva 7H sutras 64–67: number of marriages = strong planets in 7H or aspected by 7L; monogamy yoga (7L & 2L debilitated + benefics in kendras).
- **G4.** **BJ 24.9 = PD 11.3 = Saravali 46.15–17**: female — benefics AND malefics in 7H → second husband.
- **G5.** PD 10.8: Moon+Saturn in 7H → female remarries; male → wifeless.
- **G6.** UK (arudha section, Sastri comm.): exalted **A7 (Darapada) lord → second marriage bound**. [verse-vs-commentary boundary blurry]
- **G7.** Jataka Tattva sutras ~117–124: second-wife triggers (combust weak 1L/7L; malefics 7&8 + Mars in 12).
- **G8.** **PD 20.18 — the divorce-timing rule**: "If the lord of the 7th be weak, during his dasa the native will make separation from his wife."
- **G9.** Saturn+Rahu on UL/2nd-from-UL → divorce probable (E5, Jaimini tradition per commentary).
- **G10.** Discord matrix: BPHS 24.19 (2L in 7H w/ malefic → wife of questionable character), 24.76, 24.82; full 7L-in-houses set BPHS 24.73–84.

## H. Widowhood / spouse death — incl. the timing rules

- **H1. Brihat Jataka 24.14 — the widowhood-timing dictum**: malefic in 8H → "widowhood at the age indicated by the **lord of the navamsa occupied by the 8th lord**"; benefics in 2H → she predeceases.
- **H2. Saravali 46.27** converts H1 into a **dasha rule**: "widowhood will occur in the dasa periods of the lord of the navamsa occupied by the 8th lord."
- **H3.** BJ 24.8 = PD 11.3 = Saravali 46.15–17: Mars in female 7H (afflicted) → early widowhood.
- **H4.** PD 10.8: debilitated malefics in 2/7/8 → widow (female) / widower (male).
- **H5.** **BPHS 18.35–39** (Sanskrit verified): spouse-death age table — 7L debilitated + Venus in 8H → wife dies in 18th/33rd year; 7L in 8H + 12L in 7H → 19th; Rahu in 2H + Mars in 7H → within 3 days of marriage; Venus in 8H w/ dispositor in Saturn's rasi → 12th/21st; 1L debilitated + 2L in 8H → 13th.
- **H6.** **BPHS 18.42** (*ṣaṣṭhe ca bhavane bhaumaḥ…*): Mars in 6H + Rahu in 7H + Saturn in 8H → "his wife will not live."
- **H7.** BPHS 18.3: Venus yuti malefic in any bhava → loss of wife.
- **H8.** BPHS 80.1–4 (female horoscopy): widowhood judged from the **8th** (from stronger of Lagna/Moon).
- **H9.** Jataka Tattva strī-block: widowed in **7th year of marriage** if malefic in 1H/7H; 8th year if Moon in 6/8; soon after marriage if 8L in 7H + 7L in 8H w/ malefic aspect (elapsed-years-after-marriage rules).
- **H10.** Maraka machinery (BPHS 44.2–5; PD 20.40) shared with death dictums.

## I. Mangal / Kuja dosha — attribution verdict

- The quoted verse (*lagne vyaye ca pātāle jāmitre cāṣṭame kuje…* and the *dhane…* variant) is **NOT in BPHS, Phaladeepika, Saravali, Brihat Jataka, or Jataka Parijata** (complete Sanskrit e-texts searched for *pātāle/jāmitre*). Attributions: **Agastya Saṁhitā** (first form), **Mānasāgarī** (second); the named "Kuja dosha" concept first appears in **Deva Keralam (Chandra Kala Nadi)**. ⇒ late/secondary, not a primary classic.
- What IS classical: Mars in female 7H → spouse harm (BJ 24.8, PD 11.3, Saravali 46.15–17); Mars/Saturn-on-7th chains (BPHS 18.42; PD mūla *jāmitre bhujage śanau…*). The 1/2/4/7/8/12 panchaka + "from Moon and Venus also" extension + cancellation lore = later compendia (Muhurta texts, B.V. Raman).
