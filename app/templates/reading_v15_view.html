How these conclusions are reached — method & validation

Every verdict here is produced by B. V. Raman's own doctrine, encoded rule-by-rule — not opinion. This is the derivation chain, and how faithfully it reproduces Raman's own printed verdicts when tested on charts it was never tuned on.

The derivation chain
  1. The chart is cast in the Lahiri sidereal frame with whole-sign houses (the Vedic frame Raman uses).
  2. Each house is graded on its lord, occupants, aspects and kāraka, judged in both the Rāśi (D1, promise) and the Navāṁśa (D9, fruition) — per How to Judge a Horoscope.
  3. Those combine into a 9-grade verdict (afflicted → very powerful); whether the three independent checks (house · lord · kāraka) agree gives the confidence band.
  4. A planet activates a house by Raman's six HTJAH influence factors (owns / occupies / aspects the house / aspects its lord / conjoins its lord / lord-from-the-Moon); the mahādaśā × antardaśā running now sets the fructification tier (par excellence → predominant → limited → dormant).
  5. Domain scores roll up the relevant houses, keeping potential (natal promise) separate from timing (what the current daśā activates) — never blended into one misleading number.

{# ============== Glossary of terms (#15) ============== #}

Glossary — the terms in this reading
Rāśi (D1)
The main birth chart — the "promise" of the horoscope.
Navāṁśa (D9)
The ninth-division chart — the "fruition", how the promise actually plays out.
Mahādaśā
A major planetary period (years to decades) in the Vimśottarī system that colours a whole life-phase.
Antardaśā (bhukti)
A sub-period within a mahādaśā — the more immediate texture of the time.
Lord
The planet that rules a house's sign; its condition is the house's chief testimony.
Kāraka
The natural significator of a matter (e.g. Venus for marriage), judged alongside the house.
Vimsopaka
A 0–20 composite of a planet's strength across sixteen divisional charts.
Ṣaḍbala
The classical "six-fold strength"; shown as a fraction of the required minimum (≥1.0 meets it).
Sarvāṣṭakavarga
A points (bindu) system rating each house's overall support (≥28 strong, ≤25 weak).
Combustion
A planet too close to the Sun, which weakens its ability to give results.
Sade-Sati
The ~7½-year phase while Saturn transits the signs around the natal Moon.
Papakartari
"Scissors" affliction — a planet or house hemmed between two malefics.
Doctrine grade
A verdict on B. V. Raman's 9-step strength scale — a faithful reading of the method, not a probability.
