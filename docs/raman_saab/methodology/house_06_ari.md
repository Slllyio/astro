# Sixth House — Ari / Roga / Shatru / Rina Bhava

> Faithful extraction of B.V. Raman, *How to Judge a Horoscope*, Vol. I,
> Chapter IX "Concerning the Sixth House." Quotes are ≤1 sentence and cited
> as `HTJAH-I:<line>`. Read-only of the actual text; nothing invented.

## Significations (karyas) — cited

Raman opens by enumerating the 6th's references and immediately warns that the
events comprehended by a single house do **not** co-occur: a man may have disease
yet no debts, or be healthy yet deeply indebted.

| Karya | Cite |
|---|---|
| Accidents | `HTJAH-I:5984` |
| Diseases / illness | `HTJAH-I:5984` |
| Enemies | `HTJAH-I:5984` |
| Mental affliction | `HTJAH-I:5984` |
| Mother's brother (maternal uncle) | `HTJAH-I:5984–5985` |
| Misfortunes / miseries | `HTJAH-I:5985` |
| Debts (incurring debts) | `HTJAH-I:5986` |
| Litigation | `HTJAH-I:6333` (father's litigation), `HTJAH-I:6428` (enemies/debts theme) |
| Service / subordinate employment | `HTJAH-I:6122` (Moon → success in subordinate positions) |

Key framing sentence: "The sixth house rules or signifies accidents, diseases,
enemies, mental affliction, mother's brother and misfortunes." `HTJAH-I:5984–5985`

**The three principal arishtas.** Raman crystallises the chapter's spine: "the 6th
has mainly to do with the three important arishtas or misfortunes, viz-, diseases,
debts and enemies." `HTJAH-I:6187–6188`. He restates this at `HTJAH-I:6428` before
the worked charts and partitions the illustrations into disease, debts and enemies.

Discrimination warning (the chapter's opening methodological caution): a single
house comprehends multiple distinct events and "the difficulty arises in
differentiating the different events comprehended by a single house." `HTJAH-I:5988`
A rash predictor without theory and practical experience "will draw quite wrong
conclusions." `HTJAH-I:5990`

## Three Pillars — House / Lord / Karaka (Mars & Saturn) / Secondary

Raman's **Main Considerations** for the 6th: study the strength of (a) the Bhava,
(b) the Karaka, (c) the Lord, and (d) the Occupants; planets aspecting the house
and the lord are also important; and several arishta yogas bearing on the 6th must
be studied. `HTJAH-I:5994–5997`

| Pillar | For DISEASE | For ENEMIES | For DEBTS |
|---|---|---|---|
| **House** | 6th (Roga-Bhava) + reinforced by 8th & 12th & Lagna | 6th (Shatru-Bhava) | 6th (Rina-Bhava) + 2nd/12th |
| **Lord** | lord of 6th | lord of 6th | lord of 6th |
| **Karaka** | **Mars (RogaKaraka)** `HTJAH-I:6667, 6682` | **Mars & Saturn** (natural malefics of the dusthana) | **Saturn** (sorrow-lord, "duhkham dinesatmajaha") `HTJAH-I:6790` |
| **Secondary** | Moon (mind), Sun (soul/vitality), Lagna (body), Mercury (nerves), 8th house + Prasna-Marga | benefics in/aspecting 6th = MORE enemies; malefics = FEWER | 2nd & 12th lords, Parivarthana 6th↔12th |

**CRITICAL — the 6th is a dusthana: malefics here are PROTECTIVE, benefics are HARMFUL.**
This is Raman's central inversion for the enemy-signification:

> "If evil planets aspect or occupy the 6th, the native will have few enemies. If
> good planets aspect or occupy it, the number of enemies will increase." `HTJAH-I:6072–6073`

The inversion is reinforced repeatedly in the Planets-in-6th section: the Sun
fortified gives "few enemies" `HTJAH-I:6116`; Mars fortified is "victorious";
Jupiter is "feared by enemies"; Venus gives "No enemies"; Saturn is "foeless,
courageous" `HTJAH-I:6145`; and **Ketu in the 6th is called "The best position for
Ketu to occupy in a horoscope … He will be foeless."** `HTJAH-I:6155–6156`. The
preference ordering within nodes is explicit: "Between Rahu and Ketu, the latter's
position is to be preferred in the 6th." `HTJAH-I:6180–6181`

**Two natural karakas, not one.** Mars is named "RogaKaraka" / "Karaka for 6th
Bhava" (`HTJAH-I:6621, 6648, 6667, 6682`); Saturn is the co-malefic of the
dusthana and is separately named "AyushKaraka" in the longevity reading of Chart
110 (`HTJAH-I:6641`). For disease the engine must weigh BOTH Mars and Saturn.

**Secondary frame — "lord of the 6th from the Moon."** The 6th from Chandra-Lagna
and its lord is a listed governing factor (`HTJAH-I:6275`) and is computed in
almost every worked chart (Charts 109, 110, 111, 116, 121, 123).

## Main Considerations — cited

1. **Strength quartet first.** Study Bhava, Karaka, Lord, Occupants; then aspecters
   of house and lord; then relevant arishta yogas. `HTJAH-I:5994–5997`
2. **Differentiate the events.** Do not assume disease + debts + enemies co-occur;
   discriminate which of the three a given affliction produces. `HTJAH-I:5986–5990`
3. **Modifications before application.** The lord-placement results are "general"; in
   real charts, weigh benefic/malefic associations and aspects: "Unless the
   modifications due to benefice and malefic associations, aspects, etc., are borne
   in mind, it will be difficult to use these indications successfully."
   `HTJAH-I:6063–6065`
4. **Do not apply verbatim.** Twice-stated. "Over-enthusiastic students … should not
   apply the above combinations verbatim and arrive at premature conclusions."
   `HTJAH-I:6159–6160`. And: "The greatest mistake that one can commit is to apply
   verhatim the principles to actual charts." `HTJAH-I:6414–6415`
5. **Yukti / interpretative skill.** "emphasis is laid by the sages on yukti or
   interpretative skill"; the rules are "merely … a code of reference."
   `HTJAH-I:6404–6412`
6. **The strongest planet sets physical/mental/spiritual type.** "The strongest
   planet in the horoscope determines the predominance of the physical, mental and
   spiritual peculiarities of the person." `HTJAH-I:6249–6250`
7. **Mind precedes body in diagnosis.** Study the Moon (mind) FIRST, then Mercury
   (nerves); "The first thing for an astrologer is to study the Moon."
   `HTJAH-I:6223`. Sound Sun + Moon can preserve health even with an afflicted
   Lagna. `HTJAH-I:6209–6211`
8. **Never diagnose mentality on one or two combinations.** "The entire horoscope
   should be considered carefully." `HTJAH-I:6245–6246`

### Prasna-Marga 8th-house dosha rule (`HTJAH-I:6499–6502`)

Per *Prasna Marga*, bodily diseases are ascertained from (a) the **8th house**,
(b) the **lord of the 8th**, (c) the **planet aspecting the 8th**, (d) the
**planet occupying the 8th**. The **strongest of these** causes the disease
pertaining to the dosha under its domain, in the particular organ governed by the
zodiacal sign it afflicts. `HTJAH-I:6499–6502`
