# Raman's procedure in practice: the 7th and 8th houses (HTJAH Vol. II, Ch. XI and XII)

Source: `data/knowledge_library/sources/how_to_judge_a_horoscope_raman/chapter_001_full-text-unsplit.md`,
lines 7934-12506, read in full. Cited as HTJAH:<line>. The text is OCR: "benefice" means benefic,
"malefic" is often used for the plural, "h" is often read for "b" (e.g. "hy" = by), and "S" or "Sth" for 8th.
Quotes keep the OCR spelling. Every chart table is mostly garbled or missing, so planetary positions come only
from Raman's prose. No chart was re-cast for this note.

Scope: Ch. XI (7th house) has worked Charts 1-32. Ch. XII (8th house) has Charts 33-84. Chart 33
(Pindayu) and Chart 34 (Amsayu) are worked calculations. Charts 36-42 are short Balarishta notes. Chart 35
and Charts 43-84 are full structured readings. **Total: 84 charts. 32 + 43 = 75 have the full
section-by-section layout.**

Several births appear twice, and that is useful as a consistency check (see section 6):
8 = 33, 7 = 61, 6 = 55, 24 = 72, 31 ≈ 63, 44 = 64, 48 = 66, 50 = 84, 51 = 82, 52 = 83, 53 = 75.

## 1. The chapters' own stated method

### 1.1 Seventh house (Ch. XI)

**Main and secondary considerations**, in his order:
> "In analysing the Seventh Bhava, due consideration must be given to three important factors, viz., (a)
> the House, (b) the Lord, and (c) the Karaka, who in this case would be Venus." (HTJAH:7953-7954)
> "There are the secondary considerations also, viz , the occupants of the 7th house and the planets
> associating with the 7th lord." (HTJAH:7956-7957)

**The Moon, functional nature and the karaka come in later, as a caution:**
> "The mere occurrence of these combinations should not allow one to jump to conclusions. The aspects of
> other planets, not only as natural benefices or malefic, but also as functional benef ics or malefic with
> reference to the Lagna must be taken into account. The strength and associations of the Karaka and of
> the 7th house and 7th lord from the Moon must also be duly weighed before making an assessment of the
> quality of marriage." (HTJAH:8257-8261)
> Lord-placement results "must not be applied literally to charts without considering the entire chart as a
> whole after assessing the strengths and weaknesses of the several planets." (HTJAH:8035-8037)

**Time and fructification.** He lists seven factors in this order:
> "(a) the lord, (b) planet or planets aspecting the 7th house, (c) planet or planets occupying the 7th
> house, (d) planets aspecting the lord of the 7th, (e) planet or planets in association with the lord of the
> 7th, (f) the lord of the 7th from the Moon; and (g) the Karaka of the 7th Bhava." (HTJAH:8269-8272)

The tiers:
> "The sub-periods of planets, capable of influencing the 7th house in the major periods of planets also
> capable of influencing the 7th house, produce results pertaining to the 7th house par excellence or of
> great intensity. The sub-periods of planets associated with the 7th house in the major periods of planets
> not associated with the 7th house can produce results ... only to a limited extent. Similarly the Bhuktis
> of planets, not related to the 7th house in the major periods of planets associating with the 7th, can
> produce results ... only to a limited extent." (HTJAH:8278-8284)

On yogas and affliction:
> "Where the 7th lord is involved in a powerful yoga, he is capable of producing in his Dasha or Bhukti,
> the results of the yoga ... If the 7th house is severely afflicted, the 7th lord may, in his Dasha or Bhukti,
> cause destruction of the marriage" (HTJAH:8286-8290)

**Nature of results:**
> "The general principles enunciated in regard to the six Bhavas in the first volume hold good in judging
> the seventh Bhava also." (HTJAH:8294-8295)

After this comes a long list of 7th-lord-dasa results, keyed to the house the 7th lord joins and qualified
by the Navamsa Lagna (6/8/12 from Navamsa Lagna intensifies or reduces) (HTJAH:8297-8386).

**Timing marriage.** This is the rule set he actually uses:
> "The lord of the sign occupied by the 7th lord or that of the sign occupied by the 7th lord in Navamsha
> are capable of giving marriage in their Dasha. Venus, the Karaka ... and the Moon can also give marriage
> in their periods. The strongest of these lords gives marriage in his Dasha. The lord of the 7th house, if
> he associates with Venus, can also give marriage in his Dasha or Bhukti. The 2nd lord or the ruler of the
> sign occupied by the 2nd lord in Navamsha is also capable ... The lords of the 9th and 10th houses are
> empowered to give marriage in their Dasha if the earlier Dasha are fruitless- Marriage is also possible in
> the period of the planet with the 7th lord or the planet occupying the 7th house." (HTJAH:8392-8399)

Transit:
> "add the longitude of the lords of the Lagna and 7th house. When Jupiter transits the resultant Rashi or
> its trines, it is favourable for marriage." Also Moon + 7th lord. (HTJAH:8401-8404)

Delay:
> "Saturn's aspect on the 7th house and 7th lord both from Lagna and the Moon and on Venus delays
> marriage if the Dasha lord is not very strong. The association or aspect of the lord of the 6th, 8th or 12th
> on the 7th house, 7th lord and Karaka also rules out early marriage. Primary importance must be given to
> the natal positions and Dasha and only secondary consideration to transiting planets." (HTJAH:8407-8411)

Summing up:
> "the periods of the planets related to the 7th house in Rashi or Navamsha, or of Venus, or of the planets
> related to the 2nd house in Rashi or Amsa, or of the 9th or 10th lords or of the Moon, subject to other
> factors, are capable of giving marriage. Beneficeial influences on the 7th house or its lord give happiness
> in marriage while afflictions hy malefic pianets cause misery, death or separation in marriage. The 7th
> house or Venus in dual signs gives more than one marriage." (HTJAH:8484-8491)

**Extra houses for the spouse's longevity (stated just before Chart 21):**
> "Although the Bhava is the seventh, we must take into account the 8th and 9th houses also. The 8 th house
> rules 'mangalya' or the strength of the marital bond. The 9th house rules sowbhagya or good fortune. Good
> fortune covers not only material affluence hut also a long- lived married partner." (HTJAH:9060-9067)

He adds that "an assessment of the running Dasha and Bhuktis is necessary" (HTJAH:9059-9061).

**Mars and Venus (his own experience):**
> "In our experience, apart from the other astrological considerations, the mutual dispositions of Mars and
> Venus are to be carefully studied." (HTJAH:9216-9217)

On karako bhava nashaya:
> "Venus, KalatraKaraka, in the 7th is not generally favoured by ancient writers on the theory of
> karako-Bhavanasaya ... Experience has however revealed that this textual dictum is not quite valid. In fact
> Venus in the 7th is one of the finest combinations for a fairly happy marriage" (HTJAH:9245-9249)

On reading the whole chart:
> "more than Kuta agreement, it is the basic structure of the horoscopes that is important" (HTJAH:9263-9264)

**Kuja dosha.** Mars in 2/12/4/7/8 is counted "either from the Ascendant or the Moon or Venus", with
intensity "in ascending order" 2, 12, 4, 7, 8, and "Bhavas and not Rashis must be taken into account"
(HTJAH:9355-9359). Then come the sign exceptions and a unit table (HTJAH:9362-9433). The table is
OCR-garbled. The legible values are Mars in the 8th/7th: debility 75, inimical 67.5, neutral 60,
friendly 52.5, own 45, exaltation 37.5.

### 1.2 Eighth house (Ch. XII)

**Main considerations:**
> "the following faetors are important while judging the 8th house signifieations, namely, (a) The House,
> (b) Its Lord, (c) Its Oeeupants and (d) The Karaka or indieator. The yogas formed in the 8th house will
> produee their own effeets. The same eonsiderations must he judged from the Navamsha ehart also ...
> we will foeus our attention on the souree and nature, time and plaee of death." (HTJAH:9565-9569)

**Caution on lord-placement results.** They "must he modified on the strength of the Ascendant, the Moon and
Dasha in operation" (HTJAH:9682-9683).

**Four life-spans:**
> "1. Balarishta or Early Death upto 8 years 2. Alpayu or Short Life- 8 years to 32 years 3. Madhyayu or
> Medium Life—32 years to 75 years 4. Purnayu or Full Life 75 years to 120 years." (HTJAH:9697-9705)

**Quick structural rule:**
> "The life of a native may be fixed as long, medium or short according as the Lagna lord and all the
> benefice planets are in Kendras (1, 4, 7, 10), Panaparas (2, 5, 8, 11) and Apoklimas (3, 6, 9, 12)
> respectively. If the 8th lord and maiefic occupy Kendras, Panaparas or Apoklimas, the span of life is
> respectively short, medium or long" (HTJAH:10002-10008)
> "The relative strengths of the planets must be balanced before a correct assessment of longevity can be
> made." (HTJAH:10010-10012)

**Nature, place and cause of death.** He gives:
- the 7th from Mandi in Navamsa (HTJAH:10096-10102);
- malefic or benefic in the 8th, for a violent or natural end (HTJAH:10104-10106, 10292-10293);
- the 22nd drekkana's lord when the 8th is empty (HTJAH:10190-10192, 10295-10296);
- planets in the 8th by nature: "The Sun ... fire, the Moon ... water, Mars hy weapons ..."
  (HTJAH:10296-10302);
- movable, fixed or common 8th sign for place (HTJAH:10309-10314).

**Determining longevity: his stated procedure.**
> "The Maraka vichara (inquiry into death) on the basis of Vimshottari Dasha has, at least in our humble
> experience, proved quite satisfactory" (HTJAH:10327-10329)
> "Although Jaimini's method, Pindayurdaya and Amsayurdaya yield fairly accurate results, it is wise to
> determine longevity on the basis of the strength of the chart and the Dasha and Bhuktis. The first step
> would be to see if a given chart belongs to Bala- rishta, Alpayu, Madhyayu or Poornayu, on the basis of
> the combinations given earlier. Then, longevity can be fixed by determining the most powerful Maraka or
> death-inflicting planets." (HTJAH:10884-10888)

Related points:
- The span is not determinable before age 12 (HTJAH:10319-10320).
- Ashtakavarga longevity "does not seem to be quite reliable" (HTJAH:10875-10876).
- Saturn + Jupiter + Sun + Moon is "over¬simplified" (HTJAH:10879-10882).

**Maraka determinants, in his order (HTJAH:10899-10934):**
> "Primary ... The 3rd and the 8th house are the houses of life. The 12th houses from these two, namely, the
> 2nd and the 7th are the houses of death. (a) The 2nd and 7th lords cause death. (b) The occupants,
> particularly malefic, of the 2nd and 7th houses. (c) The planets, especially malefic, associating with the
> 2nd and 7th lords. Of these, the planets in conjunction with the 2nd and 7th lords are the most powerful in
> causing death." (HTJAH:10907-10917)
> Secondary: "(a) Benefice planets in association with the 2nd and 7th lords ... (b) Lords of the 3rd and the
> 8th. (c) The lord of the 3rd or 8th house associating with the 2nd or the 7th lord." (HTJAH:10919-10925)
> Tertiary: "(a) Saturn in conjunction, aspect or association with any of the primary or secondary
> determinants ... (b) The lord of the 6th or the 8th house. (c) The weakest planet in the horoscope."
> (HTJAH:10927-10934)

**Chidra-grahas.** "the strongest of them can cause death in his period" (HTJAH:10936-10951). They are:
- the 8th lord;
- the 8th occupant;
- the 8th aspecter;
- the lord of the 22nd drekkana;
- the planet with the 8th lord;
- the lord of the 64th Navamsa from the Moon;
- the 8th lord's bitter enemy.

**Special period rules (HTJAH:10953-10984):**
- 8th lord in 6/8/12: death in the 8th lord's Dasa+Bhukti; or in the Dasa of Saturn's dispositor with the
  8th lord's Bhukti; or in the 8th lord's Dasa with the 9th lord's Bhukti.
- "If the 8th lord is strong, the lord of Lagna can kill in his period. If the Lagna lord is strong, the 8th
  lord can kill in his Dasha." (HTJAH:10972-10974)
- An empty 8th: Lagna lord's Dasa when Saturn transits the Lagna or the 8th (HTJAH:10975-10977).
- The Seershodaya / Prushtodaya rules (HTJAH:10979-10984).

**Transits are secondary.**
> "Death indicated on the basis of transits can happen only when the directional influences in the birth
> charts warrant." (HTJAH:10990-10991)
> "Transits are always secondary in importance. They are like catalytic agents. All conclusions must be
> primarily drawn on the basis of Dasha-vichara" (HTJAH:11038-11040)

The transit list, Saturn first (HTJAH:10995-11033):
> "Saturn, the AyushKaraka, is also theMrutyuKaraka. When Saturn passes through the houses occupied by the
> 8th lord or the 12th lord from Lagna, death occurs." (HTJAH:10995-10998)

**Alpayu profile (stated in practice):**
> "In most cases of Alpayu, the Ascendant and 8th house and their lords are weak either due to occupation,
> aspect or other affliction. Benefices may also he weak while malefic may he strong in Kendras."
> (HTJAH:11316-11318)

**Unnatural death:**
> "in the majority of cases, apart from the influence of the 8th house, 22nd drekkana, etc.. Mars, Rahu and
> Saturn have a sinister role to play. The constellations also play an important role in judging both span
> of life and nature of death." (HTJAH:11936-11944)

**Nakshatra lords, brought in mid-chapter by footnote:**
> "Either in the I Valume or in the ii Voiume of this work we have not so far touched the significance of
> asrerisms or consteilations as it forms a distinct subject by itseif. The significance of consteliationai
> lords is highiighted by Satyacharya and hence its importance cannot be under-rated. The lordships of
> pianets assigned to consteilations are on the basis of the Vimshottari Dasha lords." (HTJAH:11371-11374)

**Children:**
> "Predictions regarding the longevity of children are generally not advisable ... the question of death
> itself is best left untouched." (HTJAH:11236-11238)

### 3.2 The opening move

**7th house.** He opens with the sign, then its occupants and aspecters, each named with its lordships.
He immediately labels each one as stabilising, fortifying or afflicting:
- "Saturn's presence as a malefic is not good causing affliction" (8493-8494);
- "Jupiter and Mars are functional benefices and their aspects fortify the 7th house ... The 7th house has
  no afflictions" (8565-8567);
- "There are no benefice influences on the 7th house which is considerably afflicted" (8647-8648).

The opening sentence nearly always ends in a one-word verdict on the house: afflicted, fortified,
blemished or free.

**8th house.** He opens the same way, with the sign plus aspecters and occupants. The verdict on the 8th
is often left implicit, and the weight moves to the Conclusion.

### 3.3 Order inside each section

Within "Lord" and "Karaka" the order is:
1. house position (Kendra / dusthana / "12th from the 8th");
2. sign dignity (own, friendly, inimical, exalted, debilitated, with neechabhanga checked);
3. associates;
4. aspecters;
5. hemming (papakartari / subhakartari);
6. sometimes Vargottama or parivartana.

From the Moon he repeats house and lord in brief, usually two sentences.

### 3.4 Balancing good against bad: the decision rules he actually uses

1. **Preponderance with a named counterweight.** He tallies malefic against benefic influence on the house
   and lord, then names the single factor that tips or limits the result.
   - "There is a preponderance of benefice influences on the 7th house" → happy (8870).
   - "Only Jupiter's aspect on the 7th house from the Moon has prevented a complete break-up although life
     itself is miserable" (8696-8697).
   - "the position of Venus and the benefice disposition of Jupiter rule out any major trouble such as
     separation or divorce" (8952-8953).

   **A benefic factor (usually Jupiter's aspect) does not make the result good. It sets how bad the
   outcome can be.** Friction stays, rupture is prevented.
2. **Total affliction with no redeeming benefic means denial or rupture.** "All factors relating to the 7th
   house being afflicted with no redeeming beneficeial influences deprived the native completely" (8669-8670).
   See also Chart 10 (8823) and Chart 4.
3. **A good factor with a blemish yields a mixed specification, not a cancellation.** "Venus is slightly
   blemished but quite fortified by Jupiter's aspect" (8506). The blemish turns into a quality of the
   marriage: "The afflictions result in temperamental clashes but ... result in a stable marriage"
   (8517-8518); "Ketu, fortified in the 7th, is good but nevertheless gives some points of difference"
   (8588-8589). One planet can give both a social and a moral quality: "Mars with Yoga Karaka Saturn gave
   a well-to-do and socially well-placed partner but the debility of Mars made himdebased" (8826-8828).
4. **Functional status neutralises a malefic.** A malefic that is the yogakaraka, or the significator of
   the house from Lagna or Moon, is defused:
   - "Saturn in the 7th from Venus is not so harmful ... because he is a Yoga Karaka" (8748);
   - "the papakartari yoga ... is toned down by the Sun and Saturn being the significators of the 7th house"
     (8899-8901);
   - "Saturn's aspect on the 7th cannot delay marriage as he happens to be the 7th lord himself" (8594-8595).

   A natural benefic that is a functional malefic does not help: "The aspect of Venus on the 7th house is
   not very helpful since he is a functional malefic" (8930-8931). The same goes for Jupiter as 8th lord in
   the 7th in Chart 36 (11244-11250).
5. **Dual signs count the marriages.** The 7th lord, Venus, and the 7th from the Moon in common signs give
   more than one marriage. This is decisive in Charts 6, 7, 17, 19 and 20. It is overridden when the 7th
   lord and Venus are in mutual aspect (Chart 1, 8722-8724), or when Venus is the yogakaraka in a fixed sign
   (Chart 8, 8776-8778).
6. **Longevity: Lagna lord against 8th lord.** The span group comes from comparing their strength. "This
   would make the 8th lord stronger than Lagna lord causing Alpayu" (11415-11416). "the 8th lord Sun is
   stronger than Lagna lord ... indicating Madhyayu" (11597-11600). Next, the AyushKaraka Saturn can move the
   group up a band: "the strength of Karaka Saturn pushes longevity into the Madhyayu group" (11703-11704).
   **Nakshatra lords override apparent dignity** in both directions:
   - "both planets occupy a Venusian constellation which is ruled by Lagna lord ... has conferred the full
     span" (11731-11734);
   - "Superficially ... does not ... indicate Purnayu. But a closer look will reveal 8th lord Venus occupies
     his own constellation" (11796-11798);
   - "The 8th lord Venus is well placed apparently hut occupies the asterism of the 6th lord" (11957-11959).
7. **Classical yogas are quoted literally, then reconciled to near misses.** Examples:
   - "The yoga is literally fulfilled except for occupation of Lagna by a malefic" (11573-11574);
   - "the conditions are fulfilled indirectly" (12132-12136);
   - "The conditions of the yoga are fulfilled for all practical purposes" (11806-11807).

### 3.5 The concluding formula

Almost every conclusion is **retrospective causal attribution**. He states the known outcome and assigns
it to the chart factors with "resulted in", "gave", "has given", "led the native to", "deprived". Examples:
"resulted in two marriages" (8719-8720), "have given a happy marriage" (8846-8847), "led the native to marry
a foreigner" (9503).

Only one 7th-house conclusion is phrased as a forward prediction from the chart: Chart 21's "indicates loss
of husband or 'vaidhavya' during Mars and Rahu Dasha" (9083-9085). Ch. XII conclusions name a span group
("would place the native in the Poomayu group" 11057-11058; "comes within Alpayu" 11461-11462; "indicating

## 4. Timing in practice

### 4.1 Marriage (Charts 1-3): a fixed candidate list, then elimination by age, then the strongest bhukti

He restates the list as an **ordered** rule:
> "(1) the lords of the Rashi and Navamsha occupied by the 7th lord, (2) Venus, (3) the Moon, (4) the lord of
> sign occupied by the 2nd lord, (5) the 10th and (6) 9th lords and finally, (7) the 7th lord or occupant of
> the 7th house can give marriage in respective order in their periods." (8524-8527)

In each chart he fills the seven slots, then **removes every dasa that does not run in the marriageable
years**. He picks the surviving MD and then the strongest eligible bhukti:

units while the Sun gets the next highest number of 5 units" (11190-11191). Ketu has 4 (11211).

## 5. House-specific twists

**7th house:**
- **Karaka Venus**, with house-counting from Venus: "The 7th house from Venus is occupied by a malefic
  Saturn depriving happiness" (8695-8696); "Saturn in the 7th from Venus" (8748).
- **The 7th lord's Navamsa dispositor** is a marriage timer (8392-8393). In practice it is used only inside
  the candidate list.
- **8th = mangalya, 9th = saubhagya** for the spouse's longevity (9062-9067). The 12th from each is the
  house of loss: "The 9th lord ... is in the 12th (house of loss) from the 9th house" (9149-9150, 9174,
  9193). The 12th from the 7th is counted the same way (9192, 9198).
- **Marakas counted from the 7th** to judge the spouse's death: "2nd lord and occupant from the 7th house
  ... becomes a Maraka to the husband" (9174-9175).
- **Unconventional marriages are read from the 8th house** (Rasi and Navamsa, from Lagna and Moon), not
  from the 7th: "Afflictions to the 8th house and the 8th lord by Rahu, Ketu, Mars and Saturn in Rashi and
  Navamsha usually result in marriages that are socially not accepted" (9528-9529). Applied in 28-32.
- **Number of marriages:** dual signs (above); "relationship between the 7th and 11th houses ... dwikalatra
  yoga" (9013-9014; also 19 at 9034); Jara yoga from the 10th holding the 2nd, 7th and 10th lords
  (9293-9294).
- **Mars-Venus** and **Kuja dosha with exceptions and units** (9216-9433). Kuja dosha is applied in Chart 22
  only: Mars "is particularly harmful in the 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th and 12th houses from Lagna or Chandra Lagna"
  (9114-9115).

**8th house (longevity and maraka logic):**
- **Houses of life are the 3rd and 8th; houses of death are the 2nd and 7th, being the 12th from them**
  (10907-10908). The 3rd house and lord are examined explicitly in Chart 35 (11050-11054) and weighed in
  43, 44, 49 and 60.
- **Karaka Saturn** as both Ayush- and Mrutyu-karaka (10995). It is a full section in every structured
  chart, and its strength can lift the group (11703-11704).
- **Two-step method:** group by combinations and Lagna/8th-lord strength, then the most powerful maraka in
  the window (10885-10888). Pindayu and Amsayu are illustrated (33, 34) but **never used in any death
  chart**.
- **"12th from the 8th"** is used as a weakness marker: "Saturn is in the 7th in a Kendra hut in the 12th
  house from the 8th house" (11330). Also 46, 61 and 67.
- **Nakshatra lords** first appear here and become a main factor (footnote 11371-11377).
- **22nd drekkana and drekkana types** (Ayudha, Nigada/Nigala, Sarpa, Pasa) give the manner of death and
  captivity (12184-12196, 12221, 12397-12400).
- **Mind and soul karakas for suicide:** "the Karakas for the mind, body and soul (Moon and Sun) must be
  afflicted" (12401-12402). Applied in 80 and 81.
- **Children:** a bare yoga match only. No dasa reasoning except Chart 39, which names the period without
  justification. He also advises against such predictions (11236-11238).

## 6. Where his practice departs from his stated rule

1. **The Moon is not in the 7th-house "Main Considerations" but is used in 31 of 32 charts.** The stated
   list is House, Lord, Karaka; occupants and associates are secondary (7953-7957). The Moon enters only as a
   later caution (8259-8261), yet "Considered from the Moon" is a standing section, and in Chart 5 it is the
   deciding factor (8696-8697). In Ch. XII the stated list (9565-9569) also omits both the Moon and the Lagna
   lord. In practice the Moon is in all 43 structured charts and the Lagna lord decides the span group in all
   22 longevity charts.
2. **The stated four-tier dasa grading (8278-8284) is never applied in a worked 7th- or 8th-house chart.**
   Marriage uses the separate seven-slot list. Death uses maraka-ship. No chart grades a period "par
   excellence", "limited" or "feeble".
3. **The 2nd-lord slot is applied from the Rasi, not the Navamsa.** The doctrine reads "The 2nd lord or the
   ruler of the sign occupied by the 2nd lord in Navamsha" (8396). His restated rule and all three charts use
   "the lord of sign occupied by the 2nd lord" (8525). For example: "The lord of the sign occupied by 2nd lord
   Venus- Mercury" (8540), where Venus is described as being in a friendly sign in a quadrant (8504).
4. **The 9th/10th condition and the "strongest" rule are reshaped.** The doctrine makes the 9th and 10th lords
   a fallback "if the earlier Dasha are fruitless" and says "The strongest of these lords gives marriage"
   (8394-8398). In practice the candidate list is ordered (10th before 9th, 8525-8526), and the MD is chosen
   by **which dasa runs at marriageable age**. Strength is used only to pick the bhukti (8554-8555).
5. **Chart 3 goes beyond the list.** Saturn is chosen as an associate of the list's Mercury (8640-8642). No
   rule at 8392-8399 allows associates of a candidate.
6. **The Jupiter-transit marriage rule (8401-8404) is never applied.** No 7th-house chart names any transit.
7. **Karako bhava nashaya.** In Chart 8 he treats Venus in the 7th as a stigma: "Venus, although, as Karaka
   in the 7th, is not good, here in this case the stigma is countered by Venus being Yoga Karaka"
   (8776-8778). A few pages later he says the dictum "is not quite valid. In fact Venus in the 7th is one of
   the finest combinations" (9246-9248).
8. **The maraka practice is broader than the maraka doctrine.**
   - He uses **Navamsa-Lagna and Navamsa-Moon 2nd/7th** in about 20 charts. They are absent from the
     determinant list at 10899-10934.
   - He uses the **12th lord** as a killer (51, 63, 82). The 12th lord is not among the primary, secondary or
     tertiary determinants.
   - He uses **star lords** as transmitters of maraka power (7 charts), a technique he says the book had not
     treated (11371-11372).
   - The stated special rules are never invoked: 8th lord in 6/8/12 with the 9th lord's bhukti (10953-10965);
     "If the 8th lord is strong, the lord of Lagna can kill" (10972-10974); Seershodaya / Prushtodaya
     (10979-10984); and the chidra-graha list (10936-10951).
   - The Lagna/8th-lord strength comparison is used for the span (45, 52), not to name the killer.
9. **Transit doctrine against transit practice.** He lists about 15 death transits (10995-11033). He uses one
   transit once, in Chart 35, and it is not on that list: Saturn to a trine of its own natal Rasi and Navamsa
   (11228-11229). This is consistent with his priority statement (11038-11040).
10. **Balarishta boundaries.** Chart 40's death in April 1929 (born 14-4-1920, so around age 9) is called
    "just at the end of the Balarishtaspan" (11293-11294), though the span is "upto 8 years" (9699). Chart 41
    ("in his 8th year", 11305-11306) is also filed under Balarishta.
11. **Duplicate charts: the same period, different justifications.** In every duplicated chart the death
    period agrees, but the reasons cited change:
    - Chart 50 (Jupiter / Mercury): "7th lord from Lagna" and Mercury "Lagna lord ... heavily afflicted"
      (11546-11547). Chart 84: "7th lord from Lagna and the 8th lord from the Moon" and Mercury "2nd lord from
      the Moon ... in the 8th therefrom" (12501-12503).
    - Chart 51 against 82, and 52 against 83, show the same pattern.

    The maraka channels are wide enough that a justification can usually be found after the fact. This bears
    on the North Star's "measure honestly" clause.
12. **Possible date error.** Chart 58's birth data (24-5-1819, 4:04 a.m., 51N30 0W5) give the death as "in
    1903" (11775). For this birth (by outside knowledge, Queen Victoria) the death year is 1901. This may be
    an OCR or source error. It is flagged here and not corrected.
13. **No 8th-house influence set.** The section titled "Time and Fructification of the Results of the Eighth
    Bhava" (10316) contains only longevity methods. It has no house-influence list like the 7th's
    (8269-8272). This matches the project note that H8 prints no timer list.
