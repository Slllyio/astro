# Real-people test: what to collect

## Step 1. Birth details only (send these first)

For each person, give an ID (P01, P02 …). Names are optional and stay private. Send:

- date of birth
- time of birth, and how reliable it is: birth certificate or hospital record / family memory to within 30 minutes / a guess
- place of birth (town)
- sex

Don't send any life facts yet. I'll seal the readings first and send you a lock code.

## Step 2. Facts (only after I send the lock code)

Ask the person, or someone close to them. **Don't show them any reading.** For each question,
write **good**, **bad** or **na** (not applicable, not known, or too young).

| # | Question | good | bad | na |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q01 | General health as an adult | rarely ill, no major illness | long or serious illness, repeated hospital stays | under 18 |
| Q02 | Own money situation | comfortable or steadily improving | persistent money trouble, debt, big losses | under 25 |
| Q03 | Speech | normal, fluent | stammer, speech delay, speech disorder | — |
| Q04 | Younger siblings | has younger siblings, on good terms | none, a younger sibling died, or estranged/serious conflict | only child by parents' choice |
| Q05 | Mother | alive and well (or died after the native turned 40), close relationship | died before the native was 40, long illness, or estranged | — |
| Q06 | Education | completed a bachelor's degree or higher | stopped before a degree | under 23 |
| Q07 | Own home or property | owns a home or land | does not own any | under 35 |
| Q08 | Children | has children with no serious trouble | wanted children but has none, lost a child, or a child with serious illness | under 35, or chose not to have children |
| Q09 | Long-lasting illness | none | diabetes, heart disease, asthma or another chronic illness | under 25 |
| Q10 | Accidents or surgery | none serious | serious accident, fracture or major surgery | — |
| Q11 | Married life | married and happy | divorced, separated, widowed, or an unhappy marriage | never married, or under 30 |
| Q12 | Father | alive and well (or died after the native turned 40), supportive | died before the native was 40, long illness, or estranged | — |
| Q13 | Career | stable and satisfying, or rising | repeated job loss, long unemployment, stuck or unhappy | under 25 |
| Q14 | Income growth | income has grown over the years | flat or falling | under 25 |
| Q15 | Elder siblings | has elder siblings, on good terms | none, an elder sibling died, or estranged | — |

Aim for 30 or more people. Family, friends and colleagues all count. The more exact the birth
time, the better.
