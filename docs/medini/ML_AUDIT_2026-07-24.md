## Gating consequences for prior results

- Binary natal-mode AUCs: *plausibly recoverable* after dedup + era/source controls (the Round-8.5
  era-stratified collapse to 0.50–0.61 already showed most headline AUCs were era ghosts).
- Event-corpus classifier/multiclass results and the age_at_death regression: **invalid until
  re-run under group splits / proper survival censoring**.
- Dasha-lord doctrine rate-ratios (fame 1.43 etc.): **need re-computation with censored windows.**
- The two surviving positives (WD marriage +0.043; framework career→10H lift 1.37) sit inside the
  blast radius (clean-template dependence; name-join + ghost-exposure dependence respectively) —
  hence the Gauntlet.

## The clean-rerun recipe (minimum)

1. Person identity: key everything on `person_id` (builders exist: `build_person_id_map.py`,
   `resolve_persons_dedup.py`); ban raw-name joins.
2. Splitting: GroupKFold/GroupShuffleSplit by person in all trainers (template exists:
   `wedge_eval_v2.py`).
3. Features: allow-list intersected with `expected_feature_columns()` — never a deny-list.
4. Era control: report the era-only baseline (birth-decade model) for every cohort-dependent
   target; replace age_at_death regression with right-censored survival analysis.
5. Source control: restrict targets to the source whose vocabulary defines them, or stratify.
6. Dasha corpus: cap windows at min(death, scrape) before any rate computation.
7. Multiclass: majority-class accuracy as the stated baseline.
