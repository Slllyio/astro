(OCR, both volumes). Range read in full: HTJAH:3322-6967. Every citation is `HTJAH:<line>` in that
file. Quotes are verbatim, OCR noise included (for example "hy" for "by", "henefice" for "benefice").

## 1. The method each chapter states

### 1.1 Third house (Ch. VI)

**Main considerations.** "As usual the factors to be considered in analysing this house are: (a) The
strength or otherwise of the Bhava, (b) the Karaka, (c) the lord and occupants. A correct appreciation
of indications of the third house rests upon a careful examination of these factors." (HTJAH:3330-3332)

**Lord's sign and Navamsa as supporting tests.** "When the lord of the 3rd occupies beneficial Nakshatra
and Navamsha and is aspected by benefice planets and Mars is equally well disposed, one will be a man of
character and conviction" (HTJAH:3421-3423).

**Denial.** "If the Karaka for brothers (Mars) or lord of the 3rd is debilitated, is in combustion or
with inimical planets, destruction of the indications of the 3rd house should be predicted."
(HTJAH:3436-3437)

**Counting siblings.** "The number of brothers has to be judged by measuring the number of Navamsha
gained by the lord of the 3rd house. Mars, or the planets in the 3rd, whichever is powerful."
(HTJAH:3447-3448)

**When the indications fructify.** The order he gives is: "First take the house, then the lord and finally
the Karaka. Planets in association with or aspecting the lord and Karaka will imprint their
characteristics also. Planets occupying and aspecting the third are also important. Thus (a) the 3rd
lord, (b) planets in association with or aspecting the third lord, (c) planets situated in and
aspecting the third, and (d) the Karaka, and the planets in association with or aspecting Karaka, may
influence the third house either as lords of main periods or as lords of sub-periods."
(HTJAH:3522-3527)

**Grading the periods.** "the sub-periods of planets capable of influencing the third in the major periods of
those connected with the third ... will produce results pertaining to the third house par excellence.
The sub-periods of planets associated with the third house in the major periods, whose lords are not
associated with the third house, will produce results pertaining to the third only to a limited
extent. Consequently the sub-periods of planets, not associated with the third house in the major
periods whose lords are associated, will produce effects pertaining to the third house, to a limited
extent." (HTJAH:3529-3535). Both lower tiers are printed as "limited", and the second is introduced by
an odd "Consequently". This looks like a printing slip.

**Timing the birth of siblings.** "Predict birth of brothers (or sisters) during the Dasha (or Bhuktis) of
the lords of the 3rd, 9th, 11th and 7th; birth of brothers may also occur in the Dasha of the lord of
the third or the most powerful of the planets occupying the third." (HTJAH:3537-3540)

**Nature of results.** These are 3rd-lord-in-house-X results for the dasa of the 3rd lord. Each comes with
a Navamsa 6/8/12 cancellation clause: "If the third lord is in the 6th, 8th or 12th from the 4th lord in
the Navamsha, then either in the sub-period of the 3rd lord or in that of the 4tb lord ... brothers will
die" (HTJAH:3592-3595). He closes: "due attention must be paid to all shades of influences covering a
particular Bhava or house. A lord is capable of conferring the results of not only the house but those
due to Karaka nature of planets joining or aspecting the Bhava." (HTJAH:3665-3667)

**Yogas are deliberately left out of the examples.** "In the examples illustrated 1 have purposely
refrained from taking into account the several yogas that may be present in them as 1 do not want the
readers to get confused." (HTJAH:3742-3744)

### 1.2 Fourth house (Ch. VII)

**Primary considerations.** "Before analysing the fourth Bhava, one has to carefully study the general
strength of faj the Bhava. ff?j the Karaka, and (c) its lord and the occupants. Of the various yogas
present in the horoscope, those that have a bearing on the fourth house should be carefully noted."
(HTJAH:4137-4139; "faj"/"ff?j" are OCR for (a)/(b))

**Strength scales the result.** "The good and bad nature of results ascribed to the fourth lord occupying a
certain house becomes augmented or minimised according to the strength or weakness of the fourth
lord." (HTJAH:4191-4193). "In judging any Bhava, one's interpretation must be intelligent and skilled
and not literal." (HTJAH:4200)

**Time of fructification: the seven factors.** "(a) the lord, (6) planet or planets aspecting the fourth
house, (c) planet or planets posited in the fourth, (d) planets aspecting the lord of the fourth, (e)
planet or planets in association with the lord of the fourth, (f) the lord of the fourth from the Moon,
and (g) the Karaka of the fourth Bhava." (HTJAH:4307-4310)

**The three grades** are the same as for the 3rd house, with the third tier now "only to a limited or even
nominal extent". "It must be noted that the term "association or associated" used in this context
connotes aspect as well as conjunction." (HTJAH:4314-4322)

**Yoga lords.** "Where a lord is involved in a Raja yoga or Arishta yoga, the lord is capable of
producing in his Dasha or Bhukti the Raja yoga or Arishta yoga results, so far as they relate to the
fourth house." (HTJAH:4324-4325)

**Separate karaka per matter.** "Vaidyanatha Deekshitar wants us to look to the fourth house and Jupiter
for judging happiness; the Moon and the fourth for mother ; for vehicles and ornaments Venus and the
fourth Bhava." (HTJAH:4386-4388). Learning: "Acquisition of learning should be ascertained by a
consideration of the fourth house and Jupiter." (HTJAH:4701-4702)

**Mother's longevity, with the 4th as the mother's own lagna.** "Mother's long life should be estimated by
the strength of the fourth house and the fact of the Moon occupying a benefice sign in a Kendra and
aspected by benefice planet. It is also possible to anticipate the longevity of the mother by
considering the fourth house or Matru-Karaka as the ascendant of the mother and studying the strength
or otherwise of the eighth house therefrom." (HTJAH:4394-4397)

**Type of learning comes from the dominant planet.** "Here the planet having to do most with the fourth
house is to be considered and not merely the house or the lord." (HTJAH:4721-4722). "Consistent
with the inherent nature and indications of the planet mainly connected with the fourth house, either
as lord, occupant or associate, one has to infer the type of education" (HTJAH:4724-4726).

**Conveyances.** "Vahana-Karaka is Venus and Vahana-Sthana is fourth. It is by a consideration of these
[actors plus the fourth lord that we have to predict Vahana Yoga." (HTJAH:4934-4935). Chapter
close: "the fourth house plus the appropriate Karaka should be well disposed to assure an optimum share
of the significations." (HTJAH:5005-5006)

### 1.3 Fifth house (Ch. VIII)

**Main considerations, now split into primary and secondary.** "due consideration must be given to the
three important factors, viz., (a) the house, (b) the lord, and (c) the Karaka, which in this case would
he Jupiter. There are the secondary considerations, viz., the occupants of the fifth house, the
associates joining the fifth lord and the Karaka and the Yoga Karaka, i.e , the planets causing the
various yogas hearing on the fifth house." (HTJAH:5018-5022)

**Lifespan check before timing.** "We have to consider whether the native is likely to enjoy the Dasha of
the 5th lord during his lifetime. If there is no chance of the Dasha of the 5th lord being enjoyed,
then we have to find out the planets which will influence the 5th house by way of occupation and
aspect and whether the native will enjoy the Dasha of such planets." (HTJAH:5310-5313)

**Timing factors.** These are the same seven as for the 4th: "(a) The lord, (b) ... aspecting the 5th
house, (c) ... posited in the 5th house, fr/j planets aspecting the lord ..., (e) ... in association with
the lord of the 5th house, (f) the lord of the 5th from the Moon and the Karaka of the 5th Bhava."
(HTJAH:5317-5323). The three grades follow (HTJAH:5328-5333).

**Analysis, then synthesis.** "The art of judging a house can be divided into two parts, analysis and
synthesis. In the former process, the combinations, bearing on a Bhava or event, are split up to
ascertain the exact influence of each conjuction and aspect, while in the latter, the isolated pieces of
information are built up into one co-ordinated whole." (HTJAH:5871-5874)

**Counting children.** "Usually the number of children to be born is guessed by the number of Navamsha
gained by the 5th house or 5th lord. If the 5th lord is aspected by Jupiter or Venus, the number is
doubled. Malefic occupying the intervening Navamsha denote possible losses." (HTJAH:5902-5904)

**Both partners' charts.** "It is very necessary that the horoscopes of both the husband and the wife are
examined ... It often happens that the 5th house afflicted in the case of the husband is made good by a
favourable disposition in the case of the wife and vice versa." (HTJAH:5967-5970). He gives a
Beeja/Kshetra sphuta test for fertility (HTJAH:5517-5527).

### 1.4 Sixth house (Ch. IX)

**Main considerations.** "one has to carefully study the strength of (a) the Bhava, (b) the Karaka, (c) the
Lord, and (d) Occupants. Planets involved in aspect-ing the house and the lord are also important.
Several arishta yogas having a bearing on the sixth house should be carefully studied."
(HTJAH:5994-5997)

**Timing factors and grades.** These are the same seven factors (HTJAH:6273-6275) and the same three grades,
with the third tier "only to a limited or small extent" (HTJAH:6280-6289).

**Interpretive skill (yukti).** "the rules given above for predicting the results likely to happen during
the Dasha of the lord of a particular Bhava can merely serve as a code of reference ... The greatest
mistake that one can commit is to apply verhatim the principles to actual charts." (HTJAH:6408-6415)

**Disease: Moon first.** "The first thing for an astrologer is to study the Moon and thereby the mental
qualities of a patient." (HTJAH:6223). The places to read disease from: "(a) planets in the 6th, 8th and
12th ^(b) planets in association with lord of Lagna ; (c) planets aspecting the lord of Lagna ; and (d)
planets occupying or aspecting Lagna." (HTJAH:6481-6483). "If the 6th and 8th lords are in conjunction
or aspect each other, then say that the disease is strong and deep- rooted." (HTJAH:6572-6573)

**Transit as the trigger for disease.** "the 'active diseases' may make their appearance with every evil
transit, if the Lagna is weak and afflicted." (HTJAH:6205-6206). On acute illness: "there will usually be
found an affliction in the 5th or 6th house by the transit of Mars and mutual aspects between
transiting Mars and transiting Saturn. Obviously, the prevailing Dasha and Bhukti influences provide
their quota of evil." (HTJAH:6733-6736)

## 3. The recurring sequence

### 3.1 Opening move

In houses 3 to 5 he **always opens with the house itself**: its sign (benefic or malefic, movable,
fixed or common), then its occupants, then its aspects, each one weighed as good or bad. Counts for
the full readings:
- 3rd house: opens with the house in 10 of 12. The exceptions are the deafness note 55, which opens with the lord, and 63, which opens with the house's sign and occupants.
- 4th house, mother: 12 of 12.
- 4th house, education: 7 of 7.
- 5th house: 12 of 12 childlessness charts (91-102) and 5 of 6 number charts (108 opens with the karaka sitting in the 5th).

Conveyance charts 83, 84 and 85 open with the lord or the Lagna lord. They are worked as yoga-matching
exercises.

### 3.2 The fixed order: House → Lord → Karaka → Conclusion

He labels these as headings ("The Third House.—", "The Third Lord.—", "Lord of Brothers." or
"Karaka.—", "Conclusion.—"). This exact three-step order, followed by a "Conclusion" paragraph, is
used in:
- 3rd house: 9 of 12 (52, 53, 54, 58-63; the deafness notes 55-57 are two-line sketches);
- 4th house, mother: 11 of 12 (64 puts the karaka before the lord);
- 4th house, education: 7 of 7;
- 5th house, 91-102: 12 of 12.

**Inside each step**, the order is placement → sign quality → dignity → conjunctions → aspects →
Navamsa. Navamsa comes last, as a relieving or aggravating note, never first. Examples:
- 58: "In Navamsha he is in the 12th in his own house with Ketu. He is further afflicted by the powerful aspect of Saturn" (3758-3762);
- 62: "The only relieving feature is Mars exalted in Navamsha, here again aspected by the Sun" (3849-3850).

**The use of ☾ grows from chapter to chapter.** It is rare in the 3rd and 4th houses, and nearly
universal in the 5th, where it often enters only after the verdict, as corroboration. Examples:
"Mark also the affliction of the 5th lord from Chandra Lagna" (5731); "Mark also the affliction of the
5th lord from Chandra Lagna and of the 5th house from Navamsha Lagna" (5690-5691).

### 3.4 Which factor decides when they conflict

1. **The lord usually outweighs the house.** Examples:
   - 53: "whilst the third house is not good, the third lord and Mars are well disposed with the result the native is blessed with a number of brothers" (3696-3697);
   - 62: "Though the third house is somewhat favourable, as the lord cum the Karaka is severely afflicted, the subject has no brothers" (3854-3855);
   - 59: the lord's combustion overrides a strong karaka (3788-3790).
2. **Two of the three decide, with one exception.** In the 3rd and 5th houses, two afflicted factors out of three carry the verdict:
   - 60 (house and karaka against the lord, 3815-3816);
   - 94 and 96 (house and lord against the karaka). In 96 he says it outright: "Since the house and the lord are considerably afflicted, the Karaka's moderately benefice disposition should be ignored." (5729-5730)
3. **For mother (4th), the karaka is decisive.** The Moon is "the primary indicator of the mother" (4620). A strong house with an afflicted Moon still kills the mother early:
   - 64: "the fourth house as such is not weak but Matru-Karaka is definitely afflicted indicating early death to mother" (4435-4436);
   - 68: the house and lord are strong, and the mother was lost in the 16th year.

   The strength of the house and lord sets *how long* the mother lives, not *whether* she dies early:
   - 71: "lived till the 36th year ... on account of the general strength of the fourth house and fourth lord" (4619-4620);
   - 74: all three "feebly blemished", so she lived to the native's 46th year;
   - 75: all strong, so she lived to his 52nd.
4. **For education, the karaka and the house/lord answer different questions.** The karaka (Jupiter) gives knowledge and intellect; the house and lord give formal schooling:
   - 80: "VidyaKaraka's affliction resulted in the native getting very ordinary school education. But the fairly strong disposition of the fourth house and the lord rendered him acquire knowledge of men and matters" (4856-4858);
   - 82: "It is the Vidya-Karaka that is really strong. The natives' general education in the sense of school knowledge is poor" (4903-4904).
5. **For children (5th), there are three grades of outcome.** He separates denial, birth with loss, and birth:
   - Denial: all three, or house and lord, afflicted (91, 94, 96, 97, 98).
   - Birth with loss: house and lord promise, but an afflicted karaka brings sorrow. 99: "The disposition of 5th house and the 5th lord indicates the birth of children. But as PutraKaraka has the combined influences of two first-rate malefic ... sorrow" (5795-5798).
   - Birth without survival: 101, "birth of issue is denoted. But as the 5th house is afflicted and the 5th lord almost weak, their survival is not shown" (5839-5841).
   - Birth not denied: when the lord and karaka are clean against a bad house. 103: "birth of children is not denied" (5879).

### 3.5 How he handles a good factor with a blemish

He uses a set of named devices, each with a worked example:

- **Dual role.** A planet's natural nature and its functional nature are weighed separately and both are named:
  - "Saturn's presence in the fourth is good as lord of Lagna and bad as a natural malefic" (4405);
  - "The aspect is good because Jupiter is a natural benefice and bad as Jupiter is a malefic for Aquarius Lagna" (4418-4419);
  - "The aspect of Mars a natural malefic is bad but as Lagnadhipati it is good" (5925);
  - the general rule: "the astrologer must be able to balance the benefice effects due to Jupiter being a natural benefice and the unfavourable result he gives as a malefic lord" (5791-5793).

  Sometimes he resolves it for the natural nature. 68: Saturn's aspect "could have been construed as beneficial but for the fact Saturn happens to be a natural malefic" (4558-4559). 101: "aspected by Saturn (ignoring the fact . that Saturn is lord of the 5th) is not desirable" (5835-5837).
- **"No doubt ... but".** This is his most frequent clause:
  - "Mercury the lord is no douht exalted hut he is considerahly afflicted" (5638-5639);
  - "Jupiter ... is no doubt in the 9th with the Moon but as he is subject to Papakarthari Yoga ... he is also blemished" (5619-5620).

  The blemish usually wins the sentence.
- **Benefic aspect neutralised by the aspecter's own condition:**
  - "Jupiter is vitiated due to contact with Ketu" (3842);
  - "even here it is in name only as Jupiter stands in an evil house from the Bhava—the 12th" (3828-3829);
  - "He is, of course, aspected by Jupiter, who being lord of the third and sixth is himself afflicted" (4999-5000).
- **Quantity of malefics against benefics.** "Jupiter's aspect and the presence of Venus, do not counterhalance the evil due to the location of three malefic planets" (5675-5676). An equal balance gives "neutral": "rendered neutral by the almost equal distribution of benefice and malefic influences" (5704-5705).
- **Aspect strength.** An aspect from the aspecter's own sign counts as stronger: "Of the two the aspect of Mars is more powerful, as the planet is in his own house" (3774-3775).
- **Rashi against Navamsa.** The Rashi wins ties: "The moderate affliction to which Jupiter is subjected to in Navamsha is more than counterbalanced by his strong disposition in Rashi" (4688-4689). A Navamsa dignity alone does not rescue a planet (60: exalted in D9 but "not sufficiently strong", 3807-3809).
- **Rashi against Bhava.** Both are read: "According to Bhava, Mercury comes to the 2nd Bhava though the results have to be based on the Rashi position also to an appreciable extent" (4082-4083). 60: "in Bhava there is Jupiter-Ketu association" (3807).
- **Graded vocabulary.** For the house: "fairly strong", "moderately strong", "nominally or feebly strong", "not sufficiently strong", "considerably fortified". For the lord and karaka: "feehly blemished", "blemished", "moderately blemished", "considerably afflicted", "severely afflicted", "powerless". This is an ordinal scale of roughly five steps per factor.

### 3.6 His concluding formula

In nearly every full reading the formula is: **"Conclusion.—[house grade]; [lord grade]; [karaka
grade]. Hence/Consequently/with the result [event]."**

- "The fourth house is moderately strong, but the lord and the Karaka are considerably afflicted. Hence the native lost his mother very early." (4485-4486)
- "The fourth house is unblemished. The fourth lord and Matru-Karaka are both considerably afflicted with the result the mother died when the native was aged 3" (4641-4642)
- "The 5th house is feehly strong, the lord is weak and the Karaka powerless." (5819)

The verdict is then stated as a **fact about the native's life** ("The native has no brothers", "has had
no issues"). It is not a probability. For most charts the conclusion is checked against a known
outcome.

## 5. Twists specific to these houses

**3rd house**
- Karaka Mars, labelled "Lord of Brothers" (3677, 3783).
- When the lord is Mars, lord and karaka merge, and he says "subject to the same afflictions" (3765, 3832, 3852).
- Siblings are counted by the Navamsas gained by the strongest of the 3rd lord, Mars or the occupants (3447-3448). He applied it in 52, 53 and 54, picking the strongest in 53 ("Venus is more powerful than the planets aspecting the third", 3697-3698) and in 54 ("the third lord is more powerful than the third house or Mars", 3713-3714).
- Sex of siblings comes from masculine planets in the intervening Navamsas (3701-3703).
- Deafness is read from malefic aspects on the 3rd and an afflicted 3rd lord (55-57).
- Birth of siblings is timed by the lords of the 3rd, 9th, 11th and 7th (3537-3539). This is never applied in a chart.

**4th house**
- There is a separate karaka per matter (4386-4388): the Moon for mother ("Matru-Karaka"), Jupiter for learning ("Vidya-Karaka"), Venus for vehicles ("Vahana-Karaka"), and Mars for houses ("Griha-Karaka", 4970).
- **Mother's death is timed by bhavat-bhavam maraka logic.** The 4th or the Moon is treated as the mother's lagna (4396-4397). The 2nd, 7th, 8th and 12th from it, and the marakas from it, decide the dasa (65-73).
- **Nakshatra lord.** "the fact of a planet being situated in a particular constellation is of paramount importance especially in the matter of predicting significant events" (4518-4520). Rahu acts through its star lord (4478-4479).
- **Karaka in its own bhava harms.** "the Karaka for the mother in the appropriate Bhava is not a happy combination" (4424-4425); "The Moon is in MatruBhava (4th house). This is definitely injurious to the mother." (4504-4505)
- The nature of learning is read from the planets tied to the 4th and the karaka (76-79, 81).

**5th house**
- Karaka Jupiter ("PutraKaraka").
- **The karaka in the 5th, or in the 5th from the Moon, is bad:** "Jupiter is PutraKaraka in the fifth house is not a good position" (5834); "his being in the 5th from Chandra Lagna, as PutraKaraka, is harmful" (5859-5860). Also 98 and 108.
- The emotional nature comes from the element of the sign on the 5th Bhavamadhya (5025-5106).
- Beeja/Kshetra sphuta fertility test (5517-5527, charts 88-90).
- Both spouses' charts are to be read (5967-5970).
- Child counts: Navamsas gained by the 5th lord, doubled by a benefic aspect, reduced by malefics in the intervening Navamsas (5902-5904, charts 105-108).
- The 7th house is cross-checked in 93 (5660-5661).

**6th house**
- Karaka Mars ("RogaKaraka", 6668; "Karaka for 6th Bhava", 6621, 6648).
- The worked charts are diagnosis and case notes. Disease is read from the 6th lord plus the **8th lord** (109, 112, 114, 120), the Lagna and Lagna lord (114, 117, 119), the Moon first for mental factors (6223), and the organ rulerships of houses from Lagna and from the Moon (the 9th for limbs, the 3rd for arms, the 2nd for vision: 6664-6665, 6867-6871).
- Bandhana yoga (imprisonment) is read from the Lagna lord joined with the 6th lord and Rahu/Saturn (6952-6953; 111, 121).
- Death in disease cases uses the maraka and Ayushkaraka (Saturn) together with Saturn's transit (110, 116).

## 6. Where his practice departs from his stated rule

1. **The grade vocabulary is never applied.** Every chapter prints the par excellence / limited / nominal grading (3529-3535, 4314-4322, 5328-5333, 6280-6289). No worked chart in the range uses it or labels a period by grade.
2. **Death timing uses a different selector from the stated influence set.**
   - The stated set is: lord, aspecters, occupants, the lord's aspecters and associates, the lord from the Moon, and the karaka (4307-4310).
   - For the mother's death he picks lords by their **maraka relation to the 4th or the Moon** (2nd, 7th, 8th or 12th from it, or a maraka of the sign holding the karaka), and by **star lord** (64, 65, 66).
   - Some chosen lords are outside the stated set: 69 Rahu, "twelfth or house of loss from Matru-Sthana" (4585-4586); 67 Rahu, 3rd from the karaka; 65 Rahu, through its star lord.
   - Others happen to be in the set, but he names the maraka role, not the influence role: 72 Venus occupies the 4th; 73 Saturn aspects the karaka.
3. **The 3rd-house main considerations omit yogas, and the examples exclude them on purpose** (3742-3744). The 4th, 5th and 6th main considerations require yogas (4138-4139, 5021-5022, 5996-5997), yet the worked charts in those chapters still rarely name one (79, 80, 105, 122).
4. **His own placement lists contradict each other for the 3rd lord in the 11th.**
   - "The 3rd lord well-disposed in the 3rd, 6th or 11th indicates a number of younger brothers" (3351).
   - "If the lord of the 3rd and the Karaka ... are not placed in the 6th, 8th and 11th houses ... the native will have many long-lived and well-to- do brothers" (3428-3430).
   - In practice (60) he calls the 3rd lord in the 11th "good" (3806-3807), and in 62 he treats the lord in the 6th as "an evil place" (3844).
5. **Child-count multipliers go beyond the stated rule.**
   - The rule doubles the count only if the 5th lord is aspected by Jupiter or Venus (5903).
   - 105 doubles it on an aspect of the Moon and Jupiter (5920-5921).
   - 106 **trebles** it because the lord is Vargottama and the 5th Bhava-madhya falls in the same Navamsa (5934-5935). That rule is stated nowhere.
   - 108 doubles 5 to "about 10" because Mars is "in the 11th from PutraKaraka in Navamsha and otherwise unafflicted" (5960-5961). That is not the stated aspect condition.
6. **The 4th chapter's own order is house, karaka, lord** (4137-4138). In practice 11 of the 12 mother charts run house → lord → karaka. The exception is 64, which follows the stated order. The 3rd chapter states the same order (3330-3331) but practises house → lord → karaka in every full chart. The timing paragraph's "First take the house, then the lord and finally the Karaka" (3522) matches the practice, not the main-considerations list.
7. **From-the-Moon is listed only as a timing factor** (4309, 5322, 6275). It is not in any main-considerations list, yet he uses it as a strength test in 16 of 18 fifth-house charts.
8. **The 6th-house main considerations (bhava, karaka, lord, occupants) are not followed in its charts.** Charts 109-123 are event-first backward explanations. The house is never graded on its own.
9. **Minor slips.** He says "Above we have given six charts bearing on education" (4909), but there are seven (76-82). He refers to "Chart No 16" (4837) inside Chart 79's text. The 3rd chapter's third grade repeats "limited" (3533-3535).

## 7. Notes for the engine (observations only, no changes proposed here)

- **Navamsa is a modifier.** In every chart it is read after the Rashi and labelled a "relieving feature" or "further afflicted". It never reverses a Rashi verdict on its own (59, 60, 62, 75). This is consistent with `house_template.NAVAMSA_ONE_STEP`.
- **The verdict rule differs by matter.**
  - 3rd and 5th: two of three factors decide, and the lord usually outranks the house.
  - Mother (4th): the karaka decides whether the mother dies early; the house and lord set how long she lives.
  - Learning: karaka for knowledge, house and lord for schooling.
  - Children (5th): denial, birth with loss, and birth are separate outcomes.

  A single house-level verdict collapses these distinctions.
- **Three tests appear repeatedly in practice but are absent from the stated factor lists:** the karaka sitting in its own bhava (4th or 5th, from Lagna or the Moon) counted as an affliction; a Jupiter aspect cancelled when Jupiter is 12th from the bhava or itself afflicted; and an aspect from the aspecter's own sign counted as stronger.
- **Mother-death timing (65-73) needs a maraka selector** counted from the 4th house and from the Moon, plus a star-lord channel for Rahu. The influence-set grader alone does not reproduce these choices.
