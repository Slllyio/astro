प्रथमः SEC. I

Of the Ascendant and its Lord

Virgo rises at the eastern horizon, its lord Budha — Mercury, the swift-footed messenger and significator of intellect, commerce, and the spoken word.1

Mercury hath placed himself in the eleventh bhava, the sthana of gains, in Karka (Cancer). For the lord of the lagna to dwell in the eleventh from his own house is a most fortunate disposition.2

लग्नेशो लाभ-स्थाने यदि
वित्त-वाक्-लाभं ददाति सः ॥
lagneśo lābha-sthāne yadi
vitta-vāk-lābhaṁ dadāti saḥ
When the Lord of the Ascendant occupies the Eleventh,
He confers gain through wealth and through speech.
Phaladeepika XI · 18

द्वितीयः SEC. II

Of the Currently Active Period

The native presently dwelleth in the Mahadasha of Budha — Mercury's great period of seventeen years, of which some fifteen years and seven months yet remain.3

Budha Mahadasha · महादशा 15 yr 7 mo remain
Shani Antardasha · अन्तर्दशा 2 yr 1 mo remain

Within this period unfoldeth the antardasha of Shani — Saturn, posited in his own sign of Makara (Capricorn), bringing his full strength upon the matters of the eleventh house.4
