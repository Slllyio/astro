## 1. Each chapter's own stated method

### 1.1 Ninth house (Ch. XIII)

**Significations.** "The ninth house signifies father, righteousness, preceptor, grand-children,
intuition, religion, sympathy, fame, charities, leadership, long journeys and communication with
spirits" (HTJAH:12509-12510). "The ninth house, although it denotes several significations, is
primarily concerned with father and long distance travel" (HTJAH:12515-12517).

**Main considerations, in his order.** "(a) The House, (b) Its Lord, (c) Its Occupants and (d) The
Karaka or signifi-cator. The Sun is the Karaka for father. Yogas formed in the 9th house or involving
the 9th lord produce their own results. These considerations must he judged from the Navamsha chart
also" (HTJAH:12512-12515).

**How to weigh them.**
- "These combinations are very general and cannot be applied without taking into account the
  concerned Karaka and the other planetary dispositions in the chart" (HTJAH:12586-12587).
- A strong lord beats a bad house: "If the ninth lord is very powerful, he becomes capable of
  conferring fortune even if placed in a malefic house" (HTJAH:12587-12588).
- A good house softens an afflicted lord: "If the ninth lord is afflicted but the ninth house is
  beneficeially disposed, the intensity of the malefic results will be greatly reduced"
  (HTJAH:12588-12590).
- "in no case is a literal application of the results mentioned above desirable" (HTJAH:12590-12591).

**When indications fructify** (HTJAH:12733-12750). The factors are "(a) Lord of the 9th house, (b)
Planet or planets aspecting the 9th house, (c) Planet or planets occupying the 9th house, (d) Planets
aspecting the lord of the 9th house, (e) Planet or planets in association with the lord of the 9th
house, (f) The 9th lord from the Moon, and (g) the Sun who is the Karaka for father"
(HTJAH:12735-12738). He prints three grades:
1. "The sub-periods of planets capable of influencing the 9th house in the major periods of those who
   are also capable of influencing the 9th house produce results relating to the 9th house par
   excellence" (HTJAH:12741-12743).
2. Bhukti lord connected, dasa lord not: "only to a limited extent" (HTJAH:12743-12745).
3. Dasa lord connected, bhukti lord not: "to a very small extent" (HTJAH:12745-12750).

No "ordinary" grade and no association test between the two lords is printed for the 9th house.

He then sets a priority on the lord's own dasa: "The results relating to the 9th house may manifest
during the Dasha of the lord of the 9th house. If this Dasha does not operate during the life-time of
the native, planets influencing the 9th house hy aspect, occupation or in association with the lord
of the 9th house give rise to the 9th house significations" (HTJAH:12768-12772).

**Nature of results** (HTJAH:12774-12919). There is a planet-by-planet list ("Subject to the general
principles enumerated so far", HTJAH:12776), then the 9th lord's dasa read by its house placement. The
Navamsa recurs as a moderator in almost every paragraph: "If the 9th lord is weak, debilitated or
eclipsed or in malefic Navamsha, that is, the 6th, 8th or 12th from Navamsha Lagna, the results will be
quite contrary" (HTJAH:12809-12811). The same 6/8/12-Navamsa clause appears at HTJAH:12824-12825,
12830-12831, 12837-12838, 12865-12866, 12895-12896 and 12905-12906.

**Final caution:** "In predicting results, the strengths of the Karaka Sun, 9th lord and other planets
influencing him by aspect and association, the running Dasha and Bhuktis must be judiciously assessed
first. Conclusions must be drawn only after the entire chart is analysed. No planet, by itself, if
afflicted or beneficeially disposed, can entirely influence the significance of the ninth Bhava without
being itsetf affected by the other relevantfactors" (HTJAH:12921-12925).

**Father's longevity, a sub-method:** "The longevity of father is judged o/the strength of the 9th house
and the position of the natural significator, the Sun. The 7th and 8th houses from the 9th house and
other Maraka houses and planets from it help in estimating the span of life of the father. Sometimes
Maraka planets with reference to the Karaka Sun can also be used to judge death of father. Afflictions
to the Sun or his occupation of malefic houses results in early death of father. If the 9th lord is in a
Kendra or the 11 th house or aspects the 9th house or is otherwise fortified, the native's father lives
long" (HTJAH:12929-12934).

**Foreign travel, a sub-method** (HTJAH:13349-13419):
- The 9th house and lord come first: "The ninth house and its lord are important in finding out if a
  chart holds prospects for such travel" (HTJAH:13351-13352).
- Then sign quality: "Common and movable signs are more capable of giving foreign journeys than fixed
  signs. Watery signs are also to be taken into account" (HTJAH:13352-13354).
- Then the Lagna lord: "The Lagna lord and its position must also be considered since foreign travel
  may be just a journey and back ... or it could also end in settling there for good"
  (HTJAH:13355-13357).
- There is no karaka for travel: "Ancient texts have not mentioned any planet as Karaka or natural
  significator of travel" (HTJAH:13359).
- The 12th from the Lagna lord's sign: if the planet there is well placed, "the native will prosper in
  his homeland" (HTJAH:13379-13381); if weak, "the native goes abroad" (HTJAH:13383-13384).
- The timing set for travel: "The Dasha of planets in the 9th house or aspecting the 9th house, the 9th
  lord and 12th house are very important in predicting foreign travel or residence" (HTJAH:13388-13389).
- The houses involved: "Besides the all-important 9th house ruling long journeys, the 3rd house governs
  short journeys, the 7th house rules foreign travel and the 12th house, long journeys and residence in
  distant lands" (HTJAH:13391-13393).
- The reason for a journey comes from the lordships involved (HTJAH:13395-13419). For example, "if the
  4th and 9th lords are related, one may go abroad for higher studies ... If the 9th and 10th lords are
  related it may be for reasons of career" (HTJAH:13396-13398).
- Sahams (Paradesa, Jalapathana) are offered tentatively, as something a student must test:
  "how far they can be effectively made use of can be determined by an earnest student only after he
  has studied a number of charts" (HTJAH:13360-13361).

**Pilgrimage** (HTJAH:13691-13726). Classical yogas only. "The Dasha of the 4th lord gives pilgrimages
to many holy spots" (HTJAH:13710-13711).

### 1.2 Tenth house (Ch. XIV)

**Significations.** "The tenth house refers to occupation, profession, temporal honours, foreign
travels, self- respect, knowledge and dignity and means of livelihood" (HTJAH:13741-13742).

**Primary considerations, in his order.** "Before analysing the tenth Bhava, one must study the general
strength of the (a) the house, (b) its lord, (c) its occupants and (d) the Karaka or indicator or natural
significator of the 10th house. Different yogas in the horoscope bearing on the 10th house, its lord and
Karaka also influence the 10th house. The Navamsha chart must also be taken intoaccount"
(HTJAH:13746-13750). **He does not name the 10th-house karaka here.** In the worked charts he uses the
Sun: "The Karaka Sun also occupies a Navamsha of Mercury" (HTJAH:14377-14378), "Karaka Sun occupies a
mercurial sign" (HTJAH:15080), and "the RajyaKaraka exalted the Sun" (HTJAH:15895).

**Preliminary for profession:** "Before judging the profession it is necessary to ascertain the mental,
intellectual and physical abilities of the native by a careful examination of the strength or weakness
of the Sun, the Moon and the Lagna respectively. The position of Mercury is equally important"
(HTJAH:13845-13847).

**A limit on the 10th house itself:** "A statistical study of over 50 charts has revealed that it is only
in 15 charts that the 10th lord and the 10th Bhava were definite factors in determining the native's
profession. But the most powerful planet in the chart will also have a bearing on profession"
(HTJAH:13862-13865).

**Vocational determinant rules** (HTJAH:13936-13962):
1. 10th house empty: "take the 10th lord as primary determinant and the lord of the Navamsha occupied
   by the 10th house as the secondary determinant. Between the two, the stronger planet generally
   indicates the nature of profession" (HTJAH:13944-13946). The words "occupied by the 10th house" are
   probably an OCR slip for "by the 10th lord". HTJAH:14293-14294 says "the lord of the Navamsha
   occupied hy the 10th lord".
2. One occupant: "of the lord of the 10th house, its occupant and the lord of the Navamsha occupied by
   the 10th lord, the strongest planet becomes the primary determinant" (HTJAH:13948-13950).
3. Several occupants: "the most powerful planet becomes the prime determinant provided there is no
   conjunction. If there is a conjunction, the stronger of the Navamsha lords of these two planets will
   give the clue", or a blend of influences when strengths are equal (HTJAH:13952-13959).
4. Three reference points: "The 10th house should be reckoned not only from Lagna but also from the
   Moon and the Sun. It is also common experience that the reckoning made from the strongest of these
   three centers gives good results" (HTJAH:13960-13962).
5. Shadvarga: "the planet whose shad- vargas predominate in regard to the 10th lord and the 10th house"
   (HTJAH:13983-13984).

Further layers, each added as a separate section of the chapter:
- The lord of the 10th lord's Navamsa as a list of trades (HTJAH:14293-14312).
- The nature of the 10th sign: fiery, airy, earthy, watery, movable, fixed or common
  (HTJAH:14319-14345).
- "Besides the 10th lord and house, the sign occupied by the 10th lord, other planets and sign or
  signs predominant in the chart as well as a fortified Lagna are some more factors that go to colour
  the occupation" (HTJAH:14347-14349). He then gives a sign-by-sign list (Aries to Pisces), each with
  an example chart (HTJAH:14352-14625).
- The planets' own professions (HTJAH:14627-14652).
- The Jaimini AtmaKaraka and Karakamsa (HTJAH:14654-14672).
- Yogas raise the level of success but do not set the profession: "They do not indicatethe nature of
  career a native is likely to take up but their presence enhances the degree of success in
  profession" (HTJAH:14727-14730).
- Other houses: "An examination of professional prospects would include the study of the 2nd house
  also ... The ninth house is also of relevance ... The 11th house rules gains" (HTJAH:14282-14288).

**Time of fructification** (HTJAH:14066-14084). The factors are "(a) Lord of the 10th house, (b) planet
or planets aspecting the 10th house, (c) planet or planets posited in the 10th house, (D) planets
aspecting the lord of the 10th house, (e) planet or planets in association with lord of the 10th house,
and (f) the 10th lord from the Moon" (HTJAH:14071-14075). **There is no karaka in the 10th-house timing
list.** The grades are the same three: "par excellence" (HTJAH:14077-14080), "only to a limited
extent" (HTJAH:14080-14082), and "only to a smallextent" (HTJAH:14082-14084).

**Nature of results:** "The lordship, aspects, location and the general strength of a planet capable of
influencing the 10th house determine the nature of results the planet produces in its Dasha"
(HTJAH:14127-14128). The 10th lord's dasa is then read by its house placement, again with the
6/8/12-Navamsa reduction (HTJAH:14159-14164, 14182-14183, 14202-14204, 14247-14248, 14268-14270). A
feeble but unafflicted lord gives a middle result: "if the 10th lord is feeble in strength but not
afflicted, his Dasha fetches the native moderate success and wealth" (HTJAH:14269-14270).

**Raja yoga timing, quoted from Varahamihira:** "a kingdom is obtained during the Dasha or Bhukti of (1)
the planet in the Ascendant, or (2) the planet in the 10th house from the Ascendant, or (3) the most
powerful planet in the chart. A kingdom is lost during the period ... of (1) a planet in an inimical
sign or (2) a planet that occupies the 7th from the sign itrules" (HTJAH:15680-15685). He adds: "since
Raja-Yogas occur in large numbers, a clever student should assess the worth of each combination taking
into account the chart in its entirety" (HTJAH:15687-15688).

## 2. Per-chart tables

Keys for the "order examined" column:
- H = the house (its sign and sign nature), O = occupants, A = aspects on the house, PK = papa- or
  subha-kartari.
- L = the lord's placement and dignity, LA = aspects or association on the lord.
- K = karaka (the Sun), M = the same house counted from the Moon, Su = counted from the Sun.
- Nav = Navamsa, Nak = the nakshatra lord, Y = yogas, Lg = the Lagna or Lagna lord.
- PS = predominant or strongest sign, AK = AtmaKaraka, TS = the timing-set list.
- MK = maraka reasoning (2nd, 7th, 8th or 12th counted from the 9th house, the 9th lord or the Sun).

## 3. The recurring sequence

### 3.1 Ninth house (father charts 86-97, n = 12)

**Opening move: always the house.** All 12 open with "The Ninth House:". He gives its sign, its
occupants by name and lordship, its aspects, and any kartari.

**Fixed order in 12/12: house → lord → karaka (the Sun) → the same three from the Moon → Conclusion →
timing.** The only variants are the merged heading in 97 and "The same picture obtains" in 91.

**Factor counts across the 12:**

| Step | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| House: occupants and aspects | 12/12 | |
| Lord: placement and afflictions | 12/12 | |
| Karaka Sun: placement and afflictions | 12/12 | |
| From the Moon (9th house and 9th lord) | 12/12 | |
| Nakshatra of the 9th lord or karaka | 11/12 | Not in 89 |
| Papa- or subha-kartari | 6/12 | 86, 89, 91, 94, 97, and the house in 86 |
| Navamsa used as a judging factor | 1/12 | Only 89 (13067); elsewhere only "vargottama" as a label |
| Yogas named | 2/12 | 86 "royal parents"; 94 neechabhangas |
| Timing given | 11/12 | 10 past deaths plus 1 forecast (95); none for 94 |

**Decisive factor when they conflict: the karaka.** He says so in his own summary: "Afflictions to the
pitruKaraka by association, aspect, placement or otherwise are very important in depriving a native of
his father" (HTJAH:13126-13127). Chart 90 shows it: "Although the 9th house from Lagna is relatively
free of affliction, the pitruKaraka is in debility in the constellation of Rahu" (13089-13090). The
father died at about 11.

**Second decider: the nakshatra lord of the 9th lord or karaka.** "The asterism occupied by a planet is
very important in judging its significations" (13239-13242). In 96 and 97 he argues the
counterfactual: "If in Chart No. 96, Jupiter had occupied Visakha, the fatherwould have lived longer"
(13344).

**A good factor with a blemish.** He states both, then lets the karaka and nakshatra decide:
- Exalted but hemmed, as in 86: "Since the planets capable of influencing the 9th house are all well
  placed, the native's father could enjoy Raja-Yoga results but his longevity was curtailed"
  (12974-12976). Quality and longevity are judged separately.
- "Exalted hut he is eclipsed" (88, 13028).
- An affliction can be cancelled by its source. A malefic aspect from the dispositor strengthens
  (94, 13221-13222); "Saturn does not afflict in the strict sense of the term being the Lagna lord"
  (93, 13193-13194).
- Mixed strength gives a middle span: "cannot live very long ... cannot deprive the native of her
  father very early in life either" (91, 13117-13119).

**Concluding formula.** "Conclusion:" then a strength verdict on house, lord and karaka. Typical
wording: "both quite afflicted", "quite heavily afflicted", "well placed with slight afflictions",
"strongly fortified", "free of afflictions hut ... blemished". He ends with a statement about the
father's lifespan ("must therefore live fairly long", "not conducive to longevity of father") and then
"The native's father died ... in X Bhukti of Y Dasha", followed by one sentence justifying each lord.

### 3.2 Ninth house (travel charts 98-112, n = 15)

**Opening move.** The Lagna lord's sign (movable, fixed or watery) and the 12th from the Lagna lord
(98, 99, 103, 107, 109, 110), or the dasa lord that gave the journey (100, 101, 102, 104, 105, 106,
108). In about half the charts **the event comes first and the chart is read backwards from it.**
Sahams are a final check in 108-112 (5/15).

### 3.3 Tenth house: profession (Charts 117-194, n = 78; the author's tally)

**Opening move.**
- 10th house (its sign, occupants or aspects): 41/78.
- Lagna, Lagna lord or the predominant sign: 33/78.
- 10th lord: 4/78 (117, 135, 155, 193).

**Factor counts:**

| Step | Count |
|---|---|
| 10th-house occupants or aspects examined | 55/78 |
| 10th lord placement, dignity or association | 56/78 |
| Lagna or Lagna lord used as a vocational factor | about 28/78 |
| Predominant or strongest sign (the sign-catalogue method) | about 22/78 |
| Navamsa (usually the lord of the 10th lord's Navamsa) | 18/78 |
| Yogas (mostly for level or rank, not kind of work) | 18/78 |
| From the Moon or Sun | 8/78 |
| Nakshatra of the 10th lord or a key planet | 7/78 |
| AtmaKaraka | 5/78 |
| Sun named as karaka | 2/78 |

**Typical order:** house (sign nature, occupants, aspects) → 10th lord (sign, aspects, association) →
Navamsa of the 10th lord or the predominant sign → yogas for the level reached. The house-lord-occupant
-karaka order of his "Primary Considerations" is **not** followed as a fixed template here, unlike the
9th-house father charts. He picks whichever factor best names the known profession.

**Decisive factor.** In theory, the strongest of the determinants (13944-13959). In practice he gives
several overrides:
- The strongest sign in the whole chart: "Note how Libra dominates in Chart No. 125" (14521).
- The Lagna, over the 10th house: "the Lagna and the influences it comes under explain the vocation (?)
  of the native more clearly than the 10th house" (176, 15368-15370).
- Aspect strength by exact degree, over the dignity of the 10th lord: "the aspects of Mars and the Sun
  are stronger, being to the exact degree of the 10th cusp, and determines the nature of work"
  (149, 14970-14971).
- Mars over the exalted 10th lord Jupiter, because Jupiter "moves into Lagna Bhava and his aspect on the
  10th is therefore not total" (14975-14976). That is a bhava (cusp-based) argument.
- Bhava sandhi disqualifies an occupant: "as the Moon is in a Bhava-sandhi (junction point), he is
  rendered incapable of giving the results of the 10th house" (115, 13989-13991).

**Splitting kind of work from level reached.** The planets' natures set the kind of work. Dignity,
kendra placement and raja yogas set the rank. He states this in paired comparisons: 148 vs 149
(14979-14991), 158 vs 160 (15125-15129), 166 vs 167 (15231-15245).

**A good factor with a blemish.** Rahu or Saturn on the 10th "coming in the way of advancement" (124,
14508-14509). Combustion is offset by Jupiter's aspect, but the 12th-house placement still limits it
(202, 15933-15940). "The Sun-Saturn combination is no doubt good regarding career but comes in the way
of domestic harmony" (195, 15719-15722).

**Concluding formula for profession.** "The native is a/an ___." or "Chart No. N is of a ___." He
usually puts it in the middle of the paragraph, after the first factor, and then gives the remaining
factors as consistent with it. The reasoning is always retrodictive: "has given", "explains",
"consistent with".

### 3.4 Tenth house: raja yoga (Charts 195-208, n = 14)

He lists the yogas first, then the "seeds of destruction" (bhanga factors), then the timing of rise and
fall. Chart 204 alone starts from "the ruler of the nativity" (16001-16002).

## 5. House-specific twists

**Ninth house**
- The karaka Sun **inside** the 9th house is a negative for the father, even when exalted: "The presence
  of Karaka Sun in the 9th house with 8th lord Jupiter is not conducive to longevity of father"
  (12955-12956); "not at all desirable" (13029); "not very welcome" (13224). This is karako bhava
  nashaya, applied but never named.
- The father's death is read as maraka logic counted from three starting points (the 9th house, the Sun,
  and the 9th from the Moon), with the 12th from the 9th used as well as the 2nd and 7th (86, 88, 89,
  93) (12929-12932; Section 4.1).
- Moola gandanta of the Moon "is capable of causing the death of father" (12982-12984).
- Heredity is compared across father and son, Charts 93 and 94 (13244-13258).
- Travel uses a separate engine: the Lagna lord's sign quality, the 12th from the Lagna lord, the
  7th, 3rd and 12th houses, sign water and mobility, and Sahams (13349-13419).
- Pilgrimage comes from classical yogas plus the 4th lord's dasa (13705-13722).

**Tenth house**
- Profession is the lord of the 10th lord's Navamsa (13944-13950, 14293-14312), checked from the
  strongest of the Lagna, Moon and Sun (13960-13962, 14295).
- The predominant sign of the whole chart, with a 12-sign trade catalogue (14352-14625).
- AtmaKaraka and Karakamsa (Jaimini) (14654-14672).
- Ability gate: the Sun, the Moon, the Lagna and Mercury for mental and physical fitness for
  responsible work. Rahu or Saturn afflicting the Moon or Mercury makes the native unfit for critical
  responsibility (13845-13856).
- Shadvarga predominance (13983-13992).
- Bhava sandhi kills an occupant (13989-13991). Bhava placement over rasi placement: Jupiter "actually
  in the 10th Bhava though in the 11th Rashi" (15822-15823); "Saturn's situation exactly in the 10th
  Bhava" (15998).
- Saturn in the 10th gives initial success and later defeat in political charts (16074-16076,
  16080-16081). Mars and Saturn combined in the 7th to 10th give fascist tendencies (15776-15779).
  Persistence (Saturn) and fire (Mars) are needed for a political career (15763-15764).
- A theory of delegated results: "if Saturn and the Sun are together in association with say Mercury,
  the effects of the yoga generated by the Sun and Saturn are delegated or transferred to Mercury also"
  (15914-15917). Three functions of a planet: "(a) due to ownership, (b) due to Karakatwa and (c) due to

- Raja yoga timing per Varahamihira: the Lagna occupant, the 10th occupant, or the strongest planet

## 6. Where his practice departs from his own stated rule

1. **The father's death is timed by marakas, not by the (a)-(g) fructification set.** The stated 9th
   timing rule (12735-12750) grades periods by connection to the 9th. For death he uses maraka distances
   from the 9th, the Sun and the 9th lord from the Moon. That is consistent with his separate "Father"
   paragraph (12929-12932) but not with the general rule. In 10 of the 11 timed father charts, the
   reason he gives is never "this lord influences the 9th".
2. **He never applies the printed grade labels.** He prints three grades for both houses but never says
   "par excellence", "limited" or "small" of a worked chart. For 116 he says only "both connected with
   the 10th house" (14106-14107).
3. **No association test is printed for the top grade, yet he uses relationships in practice.** The H9
   and H10 grade-1 sentences need only that both lords influence the house (12741-12743, 14077-14080).
   In the 10th-house examples he grades the MD-AD relationship (3/11 "ideal", 14115-14116; mutual
   quadrants, 14122-14123; mutual aspect, 16102; shashtashtaka, 16105). The relationship is used as a
   quality judgment, not as the printed tier.
4. **The karaka is listed for the 9th but never used for the 10th, although the 10th's considerations
   name one.** The 10th primary considerations list "(d) the Karaka" without naming it (13748). The
   10th timing list has no karaka (14071-14075). The worked charts use the Sun as karaka only
   descriptively (14377, 15080, 15895, 15899), never as a timer.
5. **The Navamsa is barely used in the 9th-house worked charts.** The considerations say "must he judged
   from the Navamsha chart also" (12515), and "Nature of results" repeats the 6/8/12-Navamsa reduction
   about seven times. Yet in the 12 father charts the Navamsa is a judging factor only once (89, 13067).
   In the 10th-house profession charts it is used far more (18/78), as the lord of the 10th lord's
   Navamsa.
6. **The "house, lord, occupants, karaka" order is not followed for profession.** Over half the
   profession charts open with the Lagna or the predominant sign (33/78). In 176 he says outright that
   the Lagna explains the vocation better than the 10th house (15368-15370). His own statistic (15 of 50
   charts) already concedes this (13862-13865).
7. **Travel timing goes beyond the stated set.** The 12th lord, the 7th lord and the dasa lord's sign
   quality (movable or watery) are used as reasons, though only the 9th occupant, aspecter and lord plus
   the 12th house are named at 13388-13389. The 3rd and 7th houses come in from 13391-13393. He does not
   use the 9th lord from the Moon or the Sun here, except in 112.
8. **A period lord acts outside its own period.** In Chart 91 he says Saturn's "Dasha must take away the
   father, who died just before the commencement of the Dasha" (13120-13121). He accepts the
   near-miss as confirmation.
9. **Possible OCR "8th" for "9th" in Charts 98 and 99.** "Note in both charts the Dasha of planets in the
   8th house or of the 8th lord have not operated so far" (13439) and "not directly involved with the
   8th house" (13445) sit beside "the Dasha lords not having a direct bearing on the 9th house"
   (13442). The context (travel, the 9th house) suggests 9th. Treat this as unresolved OCR, not doctrine.
10. **Forecasts are rare and are not checked here.** There are two: 95 (Rahu-Saturn may see the
    father's death, 13285) and 149 (Mars dasa should bring a very high position, 14990-14991). Every
    other timing is retrodictive.
