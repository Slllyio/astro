## 1. The chapters' own stated method

### 1.1 Eleventh house

**Significations.** "The eleventh house refers to gains, elder brother, friends, acquisitions, freedom
from misery, and happy tidings. An affliction to the 11th house may result in the loss of brother,
friends, loss of wealth, misery or unhappy news. The exact nature of the result can be gleaned only
after an assessment of the whole chart and the afflicting planets. To some extent the eleventh also
concerns marriage." (HTJAH:16194-16198)

**Primary Considerations (his order).** "In analysing the significations of the eleventh Bhava or house
the general strength of the following factors must be studied: (a) the Bhava, (b) its lord, (c) the
Karaka, and (d) the occupants. Yogas in the chart bearing on the 11th house must also be taken into
account." (HTJAH:16202-16204)

**Karakas named.**
- Brothers: "Although there is no Karaka as such for elder brothers Mars who is the Karaka for
  'brothers' according to Vaidyanatha Dikshitar and other classical writers can also be considered for
  the 11th house." (HTJAH:16554-16556)
- Gains: "Jupiter, DhanaKaraka, can also be taken as the Karaka for labha or gains." (HTJAH:16791-16792)

**General weighing rules.** "These results are very general and must not be applied directly without
weighing other factors." (HTJAH:16274) "Benefices in the 11th house augment the significations of the
Bhava while maiefics diminish them both qualitatively and quantitatively." (HTJAH:16278-16280) Source
of gains = nature of the occupant: "The source of gains is indicated by the nature of planet in the
11th house" (HTJAH:16295-16297), and "the source of acquisition can be judged from the planet in the
11th house or the planet associating with the 11th lord" (HTJAH:16455-16457).

Brothers: "Benefice aspects and associations on the 11th house give elder brothers and ensure their
happiness and long life. Malefic aspects and associations on the 11th house and 11 th lord deny elder
brothers or give them short lives... When the 11th lord is in the 6th, 8th or 12th house and if Mars is
afflicted, the native may not have any elder brother or may lose him by death." (HTJAH:16556-16560)
Counting: "a native may have as many elder co-horns as the number of planets taken together in the 11th
and 12th houses. This rule cannot be applied verbatim, it is subject to qualifications and students
should test its veracity for themselves." (HTJAH:16562-16564)

**Time of Fructification (his list).** "(a) Lord of the 11th house, (b) Planet of planets aspecting the
11th house, (c) planet or planets occuyping the 11th house, {d) planets aspecting the 11th lord, (e)
planet or planets In association with the lord of the 11th house, and (f) the 11 th lord from the
Moon." (HTJAH:16389-16395) No karaka is in this list.

Grades: "(1) The sub periods of planets capable of influencing the 11 th house in the major periods of
those who are also capable of influencing the 11th house produce rasults pertaining to the 11th house
par excellence. (2) The sub-periods of planets associated with the 11th house in the major periods of
planets, who are not associated with the 11th house, will produce results pertaining to the 11th house
only to a limited extent. (3) Similarly the sub-periods of planets who are not associated with the
11th house in the major periods of planets who are associated with the 11th lord will produce results
pertaining to the 11th house only to a feeble degree." (HTJAH:16398-16404) Note the printed tier 3 says
"associated with the 11th **lord**", where the H12 text (HTJAH:17745) says "12th house"; probably a
slip, but it is what is printed.

**Gains timing (an addition beyond the list).** "The Dasha of a weak or depressed planet causes loss of
'labha' or gains. The 11th house necessarily comprises the 2nd also so that timing of results due to
benefice dispositions or afflictions to the 11th house will have to be made taking into account the 2nd
house factors as well." (HTJAH:16794-16796) "Thus a person acquires gains in the periods ... of the
planet or planets in the 11th house, the 11th lord or of the planet or planets-aspecting the 11th house
or 11th lord- The same result may also be expected in the major period or sub period of the planet
occupying the 2nd house, the planet aspecting it or of the planet owning it." (HTJAH:16798-16801)
Losses in the same periods when those planets are "afflicted hy malefic planets, or are combust or
depressed, eclipsed or with inimical planets" (HTJAH:16805-16807). Also: 2nd/5th or 2nd/11th lords in
exchange, 5th and 9th lords in the 5th and 9th (HTJAH:16809-16811); 11th lord with 12th lord gives loss
"during their mutual periods" (HTJAH:16813-16814).

**Nature of the Results.** "The exact nature of results produced by a planet who is capable of
influencing the 11th houss in its Dasha ... or Bhukti ... is qualified by its ownership/ aspect and
position. The general principles enunciated with regard to other Bh3vas hold good here also."
(HTJAH:16433-16435) "Predictions regarding acquisition of money should be made after assessing the
strength of the 11th house and the 11th lord" (HTJAH:16454-16455). The 11th-lord-in-houses period
results add a Navamsa check: "If the 11th lord is in the 6th, the 8th or the 12th from Navamsha Lagna,
results will be moderate." (HTJAH:16471-16472); "Troubles will be minimised if the 11th lord is well
fortified in the Navamsha." (HTJAH:16492-16493)

### 1.2 Twelfth house

**Significations and factors.** "The twelfth house signifies losses, extravagance, expenditure,
confiscation, sayana sukha (pleasures of the couch), left eye, feet, incarceration, divine knowledge
and piety and Final Emancipation (Moksha). As in the case of previous Bhavas, the factors to he
considered in analysing this Bhava are (a) the Bhava, (b)iis lord, (c) its occupants and (d) the
Karaka. A proper assessment of the indication of the twelfth house rests on a balanced examination of
these factors." (HTJAH:17344-17350) Order here is bhava, lord, **occupants, then karaka** (H11 put the
karaka before the occupants).

**Weighing caveats.** "These results are general and must be adopted to suit individual cases."
(HTJAH:17430) "These results are possible in toto only if there are appropriate indications in the
chart as a whole and the particular Bhava concerned to show such loss. One should be very careful in
weighing the afflictions correctly." (HTJAH:17496-17499) "The nature of the planet in the twelfth house
gives us a clue as to how one's wealth is lost, dissipated or spent." (HTJAH:17486-17487)

**Karaka-in-12th dictum (Bhavartha Ratnakara).** "a person will be fortunate in respect of that house
whose Karaka is situated in the 12th house from the Ascendant" (HTJAH:17657-17659); list ends
"VyayaBhava or 12th house — Saturn" (HTJAH:17665).

**Time of Fructification.** Same six factors "(a) Lord of the 12th house, (b) planet or planets
aspecting the 12th house, (c) planet or planets posited in the 12th house, (d) planets aspecting the
lord of the 12th house, (e) planet or Planets in association with the lord of the 12th house, and (f)
the lord of the 12th house from the Moon. The position of Saturn is also of significance as he is the
Karaka of loss, sorrow, after-life as well as renunciation and Emancipation" (HTJAH:17730-17735).
Grades par excellence / limited / "only to a feeble extent" (HTJAH:17738-17746). Extra rule: "Note the
Bhava ruled by the planet occupying the 12th, and also the Bhava occupied by the lord of the 12th
house. During the periods of these lords, loss or trouble in respect of these Bhavas can be expected."
(HTJAH:17748-17750)

**Nature of Results.** 12th lord well placed: "his Dasha will make the native benevolent"; weak "due to
association with malefic, debilitation or combustion, one will face much suffering" (HTJAH:17754-17758).
"Dasha of planets in the 12th house generally produce untoward results or else give interest in
spirituality and religion. If, however, there are benefice yogas, benefice results can also be expected
on a material level" (HTJAH:17780-17786). "The results of the Dasha must he predicted taking into
account the Karaka and lordship of the house occupied hy the 12th lord." (HTJAH:17790-17791) Summing up:
malefic influence on the 12th lord -> "debts, expenses"; "When both benefices and malefic influence the
12th lord, mixed results can be expected"; benefic -> "religious piety and legitimate and charitable
expenditure" (HTJAH:17884-17888).

### 1.3 Ch. XVII general statement (applies to all houses)

"the Rashi, Navamsha and Bhava charts must all be taken into account. Malefic afflictions cause denial
or lack of happiness or positive misery in a particular sector of life. On the contrary, benefice
dispositions through aspects, associations and occupation give happiness and success... A proper
assessment and balancing of all factors must be made before coming to any conclusion. The
Dasha-Bhuktis are of very great importance since if appropriate periods do not operate in the
life-time of an individual, he may not experience the results in their entirety." (HTJAH:18225-18231)
Also: "it is only an intuitional perception that can help us to assess the real worth of a horoscope"
(HTJAH:18214-18215).

## 3. The recurring sequence

### 3.1 Eleventh house: a fixed five-step template (Charts 210-232, 23 charts)

Every 11th-house chart from 210 to 232 is written under the same printed headings, in the same order:
**"The Eleventh House:" -> "The Eleventh Lord:" -> "Karaka:" -> "Considered from the Moon:" ->
"Conclusion:"**.

| Step | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The house: sign nature, occupants, aspects, kartari, then a strength label | 23/23 | Sign nature is named when the matter calls for it: "fruitful" for brothers (210, 212, 215; Chart B), "feminine" (215), "barren" for the 12th in Chart B (HTJAH:18594). Strength label at the end of the step in 14/23 ("quite strong", "strongly disposed", "fortified", "very powerful", "has no afflictions") |
| 2. The lord: house placement, dignity, associates, aspects | 23/23 | Dignity (exaltation, debility, neechabhanga, own sign, inimical sign) in 22/23 |

**Opening move.** Always the house itself: its sign and what occupies or aspects it ("In Chart No. 210
Cancer, a fruitful sign, is the 11th house neither aspected nor occupied by any planet", HTJAH:16570).
An empty, unaspected house is stated as such, never skipped (210, 213, 216, 218, 224, 226).

**What is decisive when factors conflict.**
- *Affliction to the house and its lord, from both Lagna and Moon, against the karaka.* In 210 the
  karaka's link with the lord "gave one elder brother", but "The afflictions to the 11th house and 11th
  lord are quite severe both from Lagna and the Moon without any benefice aspects" (HTJAH:16583-16584),
  so the brother died. Agreement between Lagna and Moon carries the verdict.
- *A single benefic influence prevents denial but does not undo the affliction.* "The aspect of the Sun
  on the 11th house prevents denial of elder co-horns" (HTJAH:16725); "The aspect of Jupiter on the 11th
  lord from the Moon did not deprive the native totally of an elder brother but the afflictions did not
  let him live long" (HTJAH:16584-16589); "The Moon's influence on Karaka Mars in Navamsha cannot deny
  elder co-horns" (HTJAH:16700-16704). So he grades: denial -> limited number -> some survive.
- *Strength beats appearance.* A good-looking factor is overruled when its strength fails: 216, the
  11th from the Moon "is superficially well disposed" (HTJAH:16771-16772) but "The Sun and Saturn, both
  powerful malefic, influence it in mutual aspect destroying it" (HTJAH:16776-16777). 231: the 11th
  lord "though apparently well placed in his own constellation suffers loss of strength due to his
  neecha position" in the Navamsa (HTJAH:17294-17296).
- *For gains, yogas outrank the 11th house.* 218 and 224 have empty 11th houses and still give great
  wealth through lord combinations; 222 has a well-aspected 11th but "not particularly strong" factors
  and so ranks lower than 220 and 221 (HTJAH:16996-16997).

**A good factor with a blemish.** He names the blemish, sizes it ("slightly", "only"), and keeps the
good verdict unless the blemish lands on the lord that delivers the result:
- "The Karaka is quite strong, though his association with Rahu blemishes him slightly." (HTJAH:17105-17106)
- "He is with Rahu which blemishes him slightly." (HTJAH:17194)
- "The 11th lord Jupiter occupying a duSthana is the only blemish on the 11th house." (HTJAH:18562-18563)
- When the blemish is on the lord, it decides the **duration** of the result rather than its presence:
  230, the house is "extremely strongly disposed" and the throne came, but the lord is "blemished" and
  eclipsed by Rahu, so "the gains acquried during Saturn's Dasha could not last long" (HTJAH:17246-17251);
  232, "The 11th lord Jupiter is combust which indicates that his malefic lordship can gain an upper
  hand during adverse planetary periods" (HTJAH:17334-17335).
- Two-sided charts get a two-part conclusion: 231 "indicative of power, wealth and riches hy hirth.
  However, note the simultaneous planetary dispositions indicative of loss of power." (HTJAH:17288-17292)

**Concluding formula.** The conclusions are **retrodictive**: he states the known fact first and then
attributes it to factors, with causal verbs. "The native has no surviving elder brother. The
association of Karaka Mars with the 11th lord Moon gave one elder brother." (HTJAH:16582-16583);
"...has given an elder sister. Malefic Saturn afflicting the male planet Jupiter in the 11th house has
killed the elder brother." (HTJAH:16617-16619); "...has generated a powerful Dhana Yoga" (HTJAH:17025).
Recurring verbs: "has given", "gave", "is responsible for", "has generated", "resulted in",
"has made the native". Brothers conclusions are **quantitative** (a number of elder brothers and
sisters), using three counting devices:
1. Planets in the 11th and 12th from Lagna and from the Moon, averaged (210, 211, 212; HTJAH:16624-16633,
   16672-16673), Rahu ignored "as a shadowy planet" (HTJAH:16629-16630).
2. Navamsas gained by the strongest 11th-house planet or the 11th lord (212 "Mars is in the 4th
   Navamsha indicating 4 elder co-boms", HTJAH:16675; 215 "gained 7 Navamsha", HTJAH:16750; 216 "gained
   only one Navamsha", HTJAH:16782).
3. Chart B: average of completed navamsas of all planets acting on the 11th from Lagna and Moon
   ("(6 + 7 + 5 + 6) = 24 divided by 4. This would give 6 Navamsha", HTJAH:18568-18569).
Each count is then reduced by the malefics acting on it ("Malefic Saturn aspect-ing Mars destroys one
elder co-bom", HTJAH:16675-16676; "acted upon by 2 malefic, Mars and Saturn, reducing the total number
by 2", HTJAH:16752-16753). Sex of the co-born comes from the planet's or sign's sex (211 Venus "a
female planet" -> sister; 215 "predominance of feminine signs"; Chart B "eunuch planets", "female
planet", "male planet", HTJAH:18575-18578).

### 4.2 The pattern

1. **Good 11th-house events (gains, office) use the house's influence set plus the 2nd lord and the
   yoga partners.** Lords chosen: 11th occupant (209, 219, 220), 11th lord (219, 220), aspecter of
   house/lord (209), 11th lord from Moon (219), 2nd lord (220 Moon, 228 and 229), the planets that make
   the wealth yoga (219, 222, 228, 229, 232), conjunction with the 11th and 10th lords (226), parivartana
   with the 11th lord (225). This follows the Gains rule that brings in the 2nd (HTJAH:16795-16801), not
   only the (a)-(f) list.
2. **A planet that joins the lords "reflects their results"** (226, HTJAH:17119); a node gives its
   dispositor's results (222, HTJAH:17000-17001) or its associate's (Chart B death, HTJAH:18467-18468).
3. **Death of a relative uses derived houses, not the influence set.** All four brother deaths (210, 211,
   212, Chart B) and both fathers' deaths and the husband's death in Ch. XVII are timed by counting from
   the relative's house or its lord: the 2nd, 7th, 12th (and 8th) from the 11th house or from the 11th
   lord; the 7th lord from the 11th; a natural maraka (Saturn); papakartari on the 11th from the Moon;
   the star lord's position (211: Mercury in the Sun's star, the Sun in a marakasthana; Chart B: Jupiter
   in Rahu's star). The 11th lord can be the killer through its own derived position: "The Dasha lord
   Jupiter is the lord of the eleventh house and placed in the 8th (a duSthana) from the 11th house"
   (HTJAH:18580-18581).
4. **Bhukti relation to the dasa lord appears once**: Chart B, Saturn "is in dwirdwaDasha (2/12 position)
   from Dasha lord as well as 11th lord Jupiter" (HTJAH:18583-18584). 227 uses kendra relation (Rahu "in
   a Kendra from the Dasha lord Venus", HTJAH:17148).
5. **Fall of a result: blemished period lords.** Functional-malefic dasa lord in an inimical sign causing
   yoga-bhanga, and a bhukti lord in a node's star (231); the combust 11th lord's "malefic lordship" in
   "adverse planetary periods" (232); the 12th lord's bhukti following the high (230).
6. **Lifespan pruning before choosing a dasa** (Chart A marriage, HTJAH:18287-18294; Chart B longevity,
   HTJAH:18455-18456).
7. **The grade words are never used in a worked chart.** "Par excellence / limited / feeble" occur only
   in the stated rules (HTJAH:16398-16404, 17738-17746). 209 says only that the period "was important".
8. **Transits: absent from Ch. XV and XVI.** Neither chapter prints a transit rule, and none of the 50
   charts names a transit. The only transits in the range are in Ch. XVII, both for marriage: Jupiter
   over the sign of (Lagna cusp longitude + 7th cusp longitude), additionally aspecting the natal 7th
   and natal Jupiter (A, HTJAH:18296-18299) or transiting the natal 7th lord (B, HTJAH:18426-18427).
   Transit is only a confirmation added after the dasa has been picked.
9. **Twelfth-house dasa timing is rare** (2/26) and always for incarceration, through bandhana-yoga
   planets (239 hemmed dasa lord; 247 Rahu 12th from Moon, Jupiter 6th lord in 8th).

## 5. House-specific twists

**Eleventh.**
- Two karakas, chosen by the matter: Mars for brothers (with an apology, "there is no Karaka as such for
  elder brothers", HTJAH:16554) and Jupiter for gains (HTJAH:16791).
- The 11th includes the 2nd for timing gains (HTJAH:16795-16796).
- Brothers: sign fertility ("fruitful"), planet sex for elder brother vs elder sister, counting rules (planets
  in 11th+12th averaged from Lagna and Moon, Rahu excluded; Navamsas gained; Chart B average of
  completed Navamsas), reductions for each malefic acting.
- Brothers' longevity by bhavat-bhavam: maraka houses counted from the 11th and from the 11th lord.
- Gains: source from occupant (Saturn: labour, mines, industries; Venus: vehicles; Mercury+Rahu:
  engineering; Mercury with 11th lord: money-lending), and once from the sign (Taurus: soaps).
- Wealth judged mainly from 2-5-9-11 lord links and Lagna, from both Lagna and the Moon (220).
- 225 and 229 bring in the AtmaKaraka and spiritual signs (Pisces) to name the kind of gain (a seat of
  spiritual power, HTJAH:17079-17083).

**Twelfth.**
- Stated karaka Saturn (HTJAH:17665, 17734), but the worked charts use matter-specific karakas instead:
  Venus (bed, eyes), Sun and Venus (eyes), Rahu "moksha-Karaka" (HTJAH:17989, 18185), Ketu
  "kaivalyaKaraka" (HTJAH:18163, 18201), Jupiter as the spiritual planet, the AtmaKaraka Sun (240, 257,
  258, Chart B).
- Bhavartha Ratnakara: the karaka of any house placed in the 12th makes that house fortunate (233; then
  qualified by the karaka's own affliction).
- Bandhana yoga (imprisonment) is the dominant 12th-house yoga: Lagna/lord in papakartari, 6th & 8th
  lords afflicting Lagna lord, malefics in 2/5/9/12, 8th & 12th lords in a kendra, Keeta (insect) sign
  in a kendra (HTJAH:17571-17589; applied 239, 244, 247-250).
- Eyes: 2nd and 12th together, with Sun, Moon, Venus as eye karakas (251-254); a watery sign shapes the
  symptom (253).
- Spirituality and Moksha: 9th lord in or influencing the 12th, a benefic-hemmed 12th (256), the 12th
  lord in a dusthana ("As lord of mokshaSthana in a duSthana, he has made the native a great mystic",
  HTJAH:17908-17910), pravrajya yoga (236). The after-death combinations are explicitly unverifiable:
  "There is no way of veryfying predictions bearing on the state of the soul" (HTJAH:17653-17654).
- The 12th lord in the 6th or 8th (or with the 6th/8th lords) reads as **good** (vipareeta-like):
  "If the 12th lord combines with the 6th and 8th lords, the native enjoys good food, riches..."
  (HTJAH:17813-17814); "The 6th lord joining the 12th lord confers results par excellence" (HTJAH:17851-17852).
- Mixed results split in time: Chart B sayana-sukha, about 15 good years then about 15 bad, because the
  12th lord and karaka carry both sides (HTJAH:18615-18620).

## 6. Where practice departs from the stated rule

1. **Timing set for gains is wider than the printed (a)-(f) list.** The list has no karaka and no 2nd
   house (HTJAH:16389-16395), but the Gains section adds the 2nd-house occupant, aspecter and lord
   (HTJAH:16798-16801), and in practice 2nd lords time gains in 220 (Moon), 228 (Jupiter) and 229 (Sun).
   Yoga partners with no tie to the 11th in the list also time it (227 Venus/Rahu by Raja Yoga; 229
   Jupiter as "a constituent of the Dhana Yoga"; 222 Mars as yogakaraka). Note that the engine's locked
   timer for H11 ("none" karaka, `vimshottari.TIMER_KARAKA`) matches the printed list; the 2nd-house
   extension for gains is a separate printed rule in the same chapter and is not encoded there.
2. **Relatives' deaths are never timed with the influence set or graded.** They use 2nd/7th/12th/8th
   counted from the 11th house or lord, the 7th lord from the 11th, natural maraka, star lords,
   papakartari and the 2/12 relation to the dasa lord (210, 211, 212, Chart B; also A and B fathers).
   None of this is in the chapter's stated timing rule.
3. **The par excellence / limited / feeble grades are never applied** to a worked chart in range.
4. **Stated karaka for the 12th (Saturn) is never used as karaka in the 26 worked 12th charts**; Saturn
   appears there only as an occupant or aspecter (237, 241, 258).
5. **Stated order for the 12th puts occupants before the karaka, and the 11th puts the karaka before the
   occupants (HTJAH:16203 vs 17349).** In the 11th-house charts, occupants are folded into step 1 (the
   house), so the practical order is house+occupants -> lord -> karaka -> Moon -> conclusion, not the
   printed (a)-(d).
6. **"Considered from the Moon" is not in either chapter's Primary Considerations**, yet it is a
   headed step in 23/23 eleventh-house charts (and only in the (f) timing factor of the rules).
   In the 12th-house charts it appears in only 3/26.
7. **Yogas are listed last in the H11 rule ("must also be taken into account", HTJAH:16204) but decide
   the gains verdicts** (13/16 conclusions name a yoga or combination; 218 and 224 have empty 11th houses
   yet are judged wealthy on yogas alone).
8. **The planet-count rule is flagged as untested by Raman himself** ("cannot be applied verbatim",
   HTJAH:16563-16564) yet applied to 210, 211, 212, and replaced in Chart B by a different Navamsa-average
   method.
9. **Tier-3 wording in the H11 rule says "associated with the 11th lord"** (HTJAH:16403-16404) where the
   H12 rule says "associated with the 12th house" (HTJAH:17745). Likely a slip.
