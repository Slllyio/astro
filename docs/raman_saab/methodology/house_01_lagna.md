# House 1 — Tanu Bhava / Lagna

Raman's First-House chapter ("Concerning the First House"). All claims below are drawn
**only** from HTJAH-I:971–2313. Where Raman judges from the Moon or the Karaka rather than
the Lagna, this is noted explicitly.

A foundational instruction frames the whole chapter: it is the **Bhava** that matters, not the
Rashi, and the **Navamsha is equally important** — "Every combination mentioned should be applied
to the Rashi, Bhava and Navamsha charts and then a conclusion drawn." (HTJAH-I:973–979)

## Significations (karyas)

- Body and physical appearance / stature / complexion (HTJAH-I:992, 1124–1125, 1575)
- General health and constitution (HTJAH-I:1089–1091, 1124–1125, 1575)
- Childhood (HTJAH-I:1575)
- Personality and character (HTJAH-I:992, 1575)
- Mental characteristics / mental disposition — but judged via the Moon (HTJAH-I:992, 1231)
- Complexion, caste, nature of birth, fame and defame, success — per Satyacharya, judged from the
  strength of Lagna and the Karaka (HTJAH-I:991–993)
- General fortune (HTJAH-I:1124–1125)
- Head — affliction wounds the head (HTJAH-I:1104–1105, 1254–1255)
- Longevity-in-general is implicated through the Maraka check before timing first-house results
  (HTJAH-I:1578–1582)

## Three Pillars

Raman explicitly enumerates the elements of judgment: "(a) the house, (b) its lord, (c) its
occupants, and (d) its Karaka or indicator." (HTJAH-I:983–985)

- **House (the Lagna / Bhava itself):** Judge the Bhava, not merely the Rashi; apply every test to
  Rashi, Bhava **and** Navamsha (HTJAH-I:973–979). Strength factors: aspects on Lagna, occupants,
  Subha/Papa-kartari (hemming), Vargottama, watery-vs-dry sign nature.
- **Lord (functional + own Karaka role):** "the functions of a lord are those of the house or
  houses owned by him. Apart from this, each lord has his own Karaka or inherent functions."
  (HTJAH-I:985–989). Judge by (a) the lord's house-position, (b) its aspects/associations, (c) the
  yogas it forms — and apply the same in the Navamsha (HTJAH-I:989–991). Lord of Lagna in a decent
  position "is itself a great asset which sustains a person throughout his life." (HTJAH-I:1137)
- **Karaka (how to judge it):** The **Sun is the Thanu Karaka / indicator of body**
  (HTJAH-I:1810, 1814). Per Satyacharya, complexion, appearance, mental characteristics, fame,
  success "should all be based on the strength or otherwise of the Lagna and the Karaka."
  (HTJAH-I:991–993). The Karaka's strength is read in both Rashi and Navamsha (e.g. HTJAH-I:1810–1811,
  1839–1841, 1867–1868). Steady fortune is assured if at least two of the three — **Lagna, the Sun,
  and the Moon** — are well disposed (HTJAH-I:2111–2113).
- **Secondary (occupants, associates):** Planets posited in the 1st impress their own influence
  (HTJAH-I:1467–1497). Also weigh planets aspecting the 1st, planets associated with / aspecting the
  lord (the five-factor list, HTJAH-I:1586–1588). The "trinity of life" — Lagna, Moon, Sun = body,
  mind, soul — should be harmonious (HTJAH-I:1873–1874).

## Main Considerations — Raman's opening guidance

- Judgment is "the summing up of the influence of planets in a horoscope." (HTJAH-I:983)
- Four elements of any house: house, lord, occupants, Karaka (HTJAH-I:983–985).
- A lord carries both its **ownership function** and its **inherent Karaka function**; example given
  is the Sun owning the 7th for Aquarius Lagna also signifying father (HTJAH-I:985–991).
- Base judgment on (a) the lord's position, (b) its aspects/associations, (c) its yogas — and repeat
  in the Navamsha (HTJAH-I:989–991).
- Satyacharya's rule: complexion, caste, physique, mental characteristics, nature of birth, fame,
  success rest on the strength of **Lagna and Karaka** (HTJAH-I:991–993).
- Every combination is of **general application**; the exact result is set by the lord's strength,
  weakness, and attendant aspects (benefic vs evil) — "The same rules apply to all Bhava."
  (HTJAH-I:1003–1007)
- In analysing Lagna Bhava, "due attention must be paid to the dispositions of Chandra Lagna and
  Navamsha Lagna also." (HTJAH-I:1148–1149)

### Constitution / Sushka (dry-planet) rules — consolidated
- Sushka (dry) planets = **Sun, Mars, Saturn**; Sushka Rashis = the signs they own (HTJAH-I:1105–1107).
- Dry planet in Lagna → lean; ascendant in a Sushka Rashi → emaciated; Lagna-lord conjunct Sushka
  planets → same (rules #13–#15).
- Watery counterweight: watery ascendants (Cancer/Scorpio/Pisces) with good planets, watery
  Lagna-lord (Venus/Moon), or Jupiter in/aspecting Lagna from a watery sign → stout/corpulent
  (rules #16–#19).

### Mind / liability rules read from the Moon (and Sun + Lagna jointly)
- Mental disposition "should always be judged by reference to the Moon and his disposition."
  (HTJAH-I:1231) — **Moon frame.**
- Liability to diseases and accidents judged from planets rising/setting and those configured with
  the Moon (HTJAH-I:1236–1237) — **Moon frame.**
- When the **Sun, Moon, and ascendant** are afflicted by more than one malefic → liability to
  accidents and a violent/sudden death (HTJAH-I:1237–1238) — **Karaka+Moon+Lagna frame.**
- Rahu & Ketu in angles with the Sun & Moon, the angles being Aries/Taurus/Scorpio/Capricorn →
  body afflicted with distortions, lameness, even paralysis (HTJAH-I:1238–1240).

### Results of Different Signs Ascending — essence
Each sign, when rising, "imprints its own definite characteristics, mental, physical and personal,"
to be blended with rising/aspecting planets; descriptions are for the sign **alone with no planet in
the 1st**, and a planet there modifies them (HTJAH-I:1216–1221). Planetary tints on the figure:
Sun adds nobleness + health; Moon, better proportion/delicacy; Mars, ruddiness + sturdy figure
(heat/dryness temperament); Mercury, well-shaped stature + yellowish complexion; Jupiter, fair
complexion + large eyes + dignified stature; Venus, beauty/softness/delicacy; Saturn, black curled
hair + tall figure + narrow inactive chest (HTJAH-I:1221–1227). Per-sign mental/physical/general
tendencies follow for all 12 signs (HTJAH-I:1242–1465): Aries 1242, Taurus 1258, Gemini 1276,
Cancer 1292, Leo 1310, Virgo 1328, Libra 1345, Scorpio 1367, Sagittarius 1387, Capricorn 1405,
Aquarius 1426, Pisces 1450. (Per-sign affliction notes, e.g. Aries afflicted → head diseases /
Saturn+Moon in Aries → mental derangement, HTJAH-I:1254–1256.)

## Timing of Fructification

The most important directional system is **Udu Dasha / Vimshottari** (HTJAH-I:1570–1572). The 1st
house indicates body, general health, childhood, personality (HTJAH-I:1575).

**Longevity / Maraka gate (run first):** Determine whether the native will enjoy the Dasha of the
ascendant lord — longevity must be ascertained first; if the Lagna-lord's Dasha does not begin
before the Maraka period, identify which other planets influence the 1st house and whether the
native enjoys their Dashas (HTJAH-I:1578–1583).

**Five timing factors** (each can act as Dasha or Bhukti lord), HTJAH-I:1586–1588:
(a) lord of the 1st, (b) planet(s) aspecting the 1st, (c) planet(s) posited in the 1st,
(d) planet(s) aspecting the lord of the 1st, (e) planet(s) associated with the lord of the 1st.

**Definition:** "A planet is said to influence a house if it owns, occupies or aspects the said
house or its lord." (HTJAH-I:1628–1629)

**Strength-of-fructification rule:** A planet combining **two** of the five functions influences the
house most (e.g. a lord that also aspects the Lagna), HTJAH-I:1625–1628.

**Period/sub-period weighting** (HTJAH-I:1591–1596, 1635–1640):
- Sub-lord influencing the 1st, within a major-lord also influencing the 1st AND associated with the
  major lord → 1st-house results "par excellence."
- Sub-lord influencing the 1st but NOT associated with the major lord → only ordinary 1st-house
  results.
- The major/minor lord produces 1st-house effects **in relation to the other houses** it owns,
  aspects, or occupies (HTJAH-I:1646–1650).

**The Tara (nakshatra) rule:** When Lagnadhipati is weak/ill-disposed AND occupies the **3rd (vipat),
5th (pratyak), or 7th (naidhana) constellation from the Janma Nakshatra** (the star of the radical
Moon), the evil indications are intensified; if Lagnadhipati is strong but in those same stars, the
favourable indications are lessened (HTJAH-I:1142–1146).

## Nature of Results (dasha-phala)

The nature of results varies per case (HTJAH-I:1631–1633). Good/bad turns on whether the period-lord
is benefic or malefic, favourably/unfavourably disposed to the Lagna, and whether it is a Raja-Yoga
Karaka or a Maraka (HTJAH-I:1642–1644). In sub-periods the same influence applies, but the influences
of major + minor lord are **compounded** and their mutual relation considered
(HTJAH-I:1702–1704).

## Engine Notes (for house_01.py)

- **Karaka routing:** House-1 Karaka = **Sun** (Thanu Karaka). Judge body/appearance/health from the
  triad {Lagna, Lagna-lord, Sun}, plus the Moon for the mind. The integration test is "≥2 of
  {Lagna, Sun, Moon} well disposed ⇒ steady fortune" (HTJAH-I:2111–2113).
- **Varga routing:** Use **D1 (Rashi/Bhava) + D9 (Navamsha) only**. Raman applies every combination
  to Rashi, Bhava AND Navamsha (HTJAH-I:973–979). Navamsha is decisive for the lord-with-lord
  results (rules #40–#65): adverse Navamsha — especially Lagna-lord in the **6th/8th/12th in
  Navamsha from the relevant house-lord** — inverts/minimises the fortified reading. Drekkana (D3)
  appears once (friendly Drekkana, rule #23) but no full D3 routing is required.
- **Reference frames:** Three overlays must be supported — **Rashi-Lagna, Chandra-Lagna (Moon), and
  Navamsha-Lagna** (HTJAH-I:1148–1149). Mind & accident-liability default to the **Moon** frame;
  body/appearance to the **Lagna + Sun** frame.
- **Tara (nakshatra) frame:** Compute the count of Lagnadhipati's star from the **Janma Nakshatra
  (radical Moon's star)**; flag 3rd (vipat) / 5th (pratyak) / 7th (naidhana) for the
  intensify/lessen modifier (rules #35–#36).
- **Maraka gate before timing:** Resolve longevity / Maraka first; only then test whether the native
  reaches the Lagna-lord's Dasha (HTJAH-I:1578–1583). Timing influencers = the five-factor set
  (own / aspect-house / occupy-house / aspect-lord / associate-lord); a planet with **two** of the
  five functions dominates (HTJAH-I:1586–1588, 1625–1629).
- **Sushka vs. watery constitution toggle:** Sushka planets = {Sun, Mars, Saturn}; Sushka Rashis =
  {Aries, Scorpio, Capricorn, Aquarius, Leo} — **derived from "signs owned by Sun/Mars/Saturn"
  (HTJAH-I:1106–1107), not verbatim**: Raman names the rule ("Ascendant falls in a Sushka Rashi —
  signs of Mars, Saturn, Sun") but does not list the five signs explicitly; the enumeration is the
  engine's expansion (Sun→Leo, Mars→Aries+Scorpio, Saturn→Capricorn+Aquarius). Watery set for
  corpulence = {Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces} + watery planets {Venus, Moon} + Jupiter in/aspecting from
  a watery sign (rules #13–#19).
- **Gotchas:**
  - Hemming (kartari) severity is **positional**: Rahu worse in 2nd than 12th; Saturn worse in 12th
    than 2nd (rule #27, HTJAH-I:1128–1129). Do not treat the two flanks symmetrically.
  - "Ketu in Lagna" may actually fall in the **12th Bhava** (Chart 17) — Raman judges by Bhava, not
    Rashi; a Rashi-only engine would mis-flag it (HTJAH-I:1945).
  - Apparent conjunction ≠ effective conjunction: the Sun ~18° from Saturn (Chart 14) and Mercury/
    Jupiter ~20° behind Rahu (Chart 30) are deemed **free of the conjunction's effect**. An orb gate
    is needed (HTJAH-I:1872, 2190–2196).
  - Neechabhanga (debility-cancellation) repeatedly converts a "bad" placement to neutral/Raja-yoga
    (Charts 13, 19, 22, 31) — must be evaluated before applying debility penalties.
  - A powerful 10th-house stellium does **not** rescue an afflicted 1st house (Chart 33) — keep
    house-1 verdicts decoupled from 10th-house strength.
  - Vargottama / Vargottamamsa (same sign Rashi & Navamsha) is a strong-Lagna flag (Charts 14, 16).
  - The Sun and Moon can be **functional malefics** relative to a given Lagna ("Jupiter here is a
    malefic," "the Moon, a malefic in this case") — benefic/malefic status is chart-relative, not
    fixed (HTJAH-I:1806, 1818).
  - The **1st-lord ↔ 6th-lord no-contact** rule is a health/defamation diagnostic; its violation is
    the load-bearing reasoning in Chart 18 (HTJAH-I:1972–1974).
  - Raman cites the late **Prof. B. Suryanarain Rao as a typical example of Taurus ascending**
    (HTJAH-I:1273) — a named real-world exemplar of the Taurus-rising type, useful as a fixture/
    sanity anchor for the Taurus sign-tendency block but not itself a predictive rule.
