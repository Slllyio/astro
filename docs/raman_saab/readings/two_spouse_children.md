## 4. Why there is no single combined verdict — and how to read it

Raman prescribes judging both 5th houses (5436) and allows offsetting (5968), but he gives **no
algorithm** for combining two parental verdicts into one child verdict. Per the Prime Directive's
*no silent approximation*, the engine **refuses to invent one** — it reports both testimonies,
their concordances, and the redemptive thread, and leaves the synthesis to the human. So, read
honestly:

- **The affliction is real and doubled** — both charts, both sphuṭas, the 8th-lord exile in both.
  A reading of "no difficulty" is not what these charts show.
- **The promise is withheld, not void** — the two-witness navāṁśa-own Jupiter, the mother's
  exalted Mercury and Jupiter-in-5th, lean the matter toward **struggle, care, and coping** rather
  than loss.
- **Nature of the difficulty** — the communication theme is legible (Mercury), consistent across
  the parental testimony.

**Confidence: moderate (~55–60%).** The *afflicted-with-a-redemptive-thread* structure is robust
(it now agrees across two charts, both verdicts, all four sphuṭas, and the D-7 overlays). The
"coping rather than loss" lean rests on the redemptive significators, which are genuine but
dignity-mixed. This is a chart reading in Raman's system, **not** a medical prognosis.
