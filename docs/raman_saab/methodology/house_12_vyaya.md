> Note on nodes: Raman uses **Ketu** as the kaivalya/moksha karaka via *karakamsa*
> (Jaimini overlay), but also calls **Rahu** "mokshakaraka/moksha-karaka" when in the
> 12th in the example charts (17134, 17595). For the soul-after-death sub-matter the
> primary natural karaka remains **Saturn** (16667–16669).

## Main Considerations — cited

- A *"proper assessment… rests on a balanced examination of these factors"* — never read the house in isolation (HTJAH-II:16123–16124).
- **Varga of the 12th lord decides the moral colour of expenditure**: benefic vargas → wealth spent *"in honourable and approved ways"*; with/aspected by malefic/depressed/eclipsed planet → spent illegally (HTJAH-II:16250–16254).
- **The planet in/afflicting the 12th names the channel of loss** — *"The nature of the planet in the twelfth house gives us a clue as to how one's wealth is lost"* (HTJAH-II:16307–16308); afflicting planet's significations (4th lord → mother, 6th lord → enemies/litigation, etc.) name the agent of loss (16327–16335).
- **Caution against over-prediction**: results hold *"in toto only if there are appropriate indications in the chart as a whole"*; *"weigh the afflictions correctly"* (HTJAH-II:16322–16325).
- **General results are templates**: *"These results are general and must be adopted to suit individual cases"* (HTJAH-II:16230).
- **Bhavartha Ratnakara karaka-in-12 dictum**: a person is *fortunate* in the matters of that house **whose karaka sits in the 12th from Lagna** (HTJAH-II:16543–16569) — Sun-in-12 → fortunate re 9th house; Moon-in-12 → re 4th; Venus-in-12 → re 7th, etc. **Source-list caveat**: the printed karaka enumeration (16549–16563) is **OCR-truncated for the 4th house** — *"Matrubhava or 4th house — The"* (the Moon is dropped after "The", `:16552`) — and the list assigns **no karaka to Mercury** at all. So the engine's `Mercury→?` is *faithful to the source*, not an oversight; the 4th-house karaka is the Moon (restored from the worked example at 16566–16567: *"if the Moon is in the 12th house, [fortunate] in respect of the 4th house indications"*).
- **Saturn's position is always significant** as the karaka of loss/after-life/renunciation (HTJAH-II:16667–16669).
- **Mediocre-life dictum**: *"If the Lagna lord is in the 12th house and the trines or quadrants are not occupied by benefics, the native will be a man of mediocre or poor means leading a monotonous life"* — a general modifier on the Lagna-lord-in-12th placement (HTJAH-II:16240–16242).

## Timing of Fructification — governing factors + dasha rule

Governing factors of the 12th (HTJAH-II:16661–16669): (a) **lord of the 12th**,
(b) planets **aspecting** the 12th, (c) **occupants** of the 12th, (d) planets aspecting
the **lord**, (e) **associates** of the lord, (f) the **lord of the 12th from the Moon**,
plus **Saturn** as karaka of loss/sorrow/after-life/renunciation/Emancipation.

**Two-level activation rule** (HTJAH-II:16671–16686):
1. AD-lord influencing 12th **in** MD-lord also influencing 12th → 12th-house results *"par excellence"*.
2. Influencing-planet's sub-period in a non-influencing major period → results *"to a limited extent"*.
3. Non-influencing sub-period in an influencing major period → results *"to a feeble extent"*.

**House-ledger rule** (HTJAH-II:16688–16691): note the Bhava **ruled by** the planet
occupying the 12th and the Bhava **occupied by** the 12th lord — *"During the periods of

## Nature of Results (dasha-phala) — fortified vs afflicted; navamsa overlays

Source block HTJAH-II:16693–16888.

**Fortified vs afflicted 12th lord in its Dasha** (HTJAH-II:16695–16702):
- **Well-placed / benefic aspects**: Dasa makes the native *benevolent, virtuous, honoured by rulers*.
- **Weak (malefic association / debilitation / combustion)**: *"much suffering"* — health breaks, calumny, captivity, dwindling earnings.

**Per-planet dasha results when connected to the 12th** (HTJAH-II:16707–16732):
Sun → unsuccessful ventures, takes to occult, children-unhappiness · Moon → obstacles,
cruel/evil-minded, deceived, obscure life · Mars → diseases/accidents, popularity-loss,
dishonest/duped · Mercury → philosophical, mental delusion then new skills, limited progeny,
mother in danger · Jupiter → poor/unlucky, loss of children, sins then piety · Venus → women
of ill-repute, separation from spouse, miserly · Saturn → business fails, debts/litigation,
eyesight fails, dispassion if late in life · Rahu → loss of limbs via accident, limited progeny,
unconventional earnings · Ketu → mental disturbance, wandering, residence abroad, servile work.

**Default tilt** (HTJAH-II:16734–16739): *Dasas of planets in the 12th "generally produce
untoward results or else give interest in spirituality and religion"*; benefic yogas can flip
to material gain (profession, prosperity, wife, children, luxuries).

**12th-lord-in-each-house Dasa results** (HTJAH-II:16748–16880) — read with the
**karaka and lordship of the house occupied** (16745–16746). Key navamsa/relational overlays:
- **12th lord with Asc-lord in 1st** → loses others' affection, dull intelligence, miserly; benefic 12th lord reduces evil (16748–16758).
- **In 3rd** with 3rd lord: if 12th lord in **6/8/12 from Navamsa-Lagna** or aspected by benefics → contrary (good) results, country-travel; with 6th & 8th lords → good food/riches/luxuries (16770–16779).
- **In 4th**: benefics on 4th lord → property + spiritual literature interest; both benefic+malefic aspect → troubles overcome (16781–16793).
- **In 6th** with 6th lord → *"joyous festivities"*; with 8th lord → political power/fame; **6th lord joining 12th lord → results par excellence** (16806–16834).
- **In 8th** with 8th lord → luck mixed with trouble, slow success; 12th lord related to 5th lord → deeply devoted to God (16827–16836).
- **In 10th**: benefic aspect → detachment/spirituality; malefic → propitiation of Kshudra-Devatas; papakartari → dreaded for evil powers (16843–16851).
- **In 11th**: financial set-backs; Jupiter/5th lord afflicting → wealth dissipated by a bad son (16852–16865).
- **In 12th** (own): much wealth, no untoward expenditure; Asc+lord well-placed → income exceeds expenditure, feeds holy men; affliction + weak Asc → obscure, menial life (16867–16879).
- **Summing-up** (16881–16888): 12th lord malefic-influenced → debts/expenses/wicked company; mixed → mixed; benefic → religious piety + charitable expenditure.

## Engine Notes (gotchas for `house_12.py`)

- **Dual karaka, partitioned by sub-matter** — do NOT collapse to one karaka:
  - **Saturn** = karaka for **loss, sorrow, expenditure, after-life, renunciation, Emancipation** (HTJAH-II:16667–16669). Use Saturn for loss/grief and after-death timing.
  - **Ketu** = karaka for **Moksha / Kaivalya**, evaluated **from karakamsa** (Jaimini), not from Lagna (HTJAH-II:16527; "kaivalyakaraka Ketu" 17073, 17540).
  - **Rahu** is also labelled "moksha-karaka/mokshakaraka" when occupying the 12th in worked charts (17134, 17595) — treat as a node-driven spiritual amplifier, secondary to Saturn/Ketu.
  - Topic-specific karakas: **Venus** for sayana-sukha (16289); **Sun + Moon + Venus** jointly for the eyes (16456).

- **Reference frames** — the engine must reckon the 12th from **three origins**:
  - from **Lagna** (default),
  - from the **Moon** (e.g. poverty rule C1 "Jupiter in 12/6/8 from Moon" 16395; bandhana strength "stronger from the Moon" 17239; Rahu in 12th-from-Moon 17312),
  - from the **Karaka/karakamsa** (Ketu-in-12-from-karakamsa for kaivalya 16527; "malefic in 2/4/5 from Jupiter" for poverty 16410).

- **Navamsa is the decisive overlay** for: charity magnitude (16276–16287), beggary/poverty (16398–16401), after-death state (16517–16523), and **blindness** — Chart 252's verdict turns on *"affliction to Venus both in Rasi and Navamsa"* (17494–17496). D12 is NOT separately invoked here.

- **Bhavartha-Ratnakara inversion (must special-case)**: karaka-in-12-from-Lagna makes the native *fortunate* in **that karaka's own house**, not unfortunate in the 12th (16543–16569). Encode the karaka→house map (Sun→9, Moon→4, Mars→3, Mercury→?, Jupiter→2/5/10/11, Venus→7, Saturn→6/8/12) and flip the polarity. **`Mercury→?` is deliberate**: the source karaka list (16549–16563) names no karaka for Mercury, and is OCR-truncated for the 4th house (Moon dropped at `:16552`, restored from the example at 16566–16567) — see the *Bhavartha Ratnakara* caveat under *Main Considerations*.

- **Bandhana Yoga is a composite, sign-modal**: imprisonment fires from stacked conditions (malefics in 2/5/9/12; 6th+Lagna lords with Saturn; nodes with 6th/Lagna lords; 6th/8th lords on Lagna & Lagna-lord), and the **mode of confinement is set by the rising sign** (ropes / underground cell / fetters / protected building — 16431–16436). Many example charts (244, 247, 248, 249, 250) are jail cases — use them as regression fixtures.

- **Eyes: left vs right disambiguation** — Mars in 12th → **right** eye (16470); Moon+Venus or Venus+malefic in 12th → **left** eye (16472–16474); afflict Sun/Moon/Venus AND the 12th for defective vision (16456–16458).

- **Expenditure morality switch**: 12th-lord varga (benefic vs malefic shashtyamsa) flips honourable↔questionable spending (16250–16254); the **afflicting planet's house-significations name the channel/agent of loss** (16327–16335) — encode a planet→loss-channel lookup.

- **Dasha intensity is two-level** (MD×AD both touching 12th → "par excellence"; one → "limited"; neither-but-occupant → "feeble") — HTJAH-II:16671–16686. Default tilt of any planet-in-12 Dasa = untoward OR spiritual unless benefic yogas redeem (16734–16739).

- **Modernization caveat (do not over-weight blindness)**: Raman explicitly notes defective vision is *"of little consequence"* today vs the classical era (16476–16489) — surface severity should be down-weighted for eye-defect outputs.
