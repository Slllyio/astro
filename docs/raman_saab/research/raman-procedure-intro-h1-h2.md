# Raman's house-reading procedure in practice: HTJAH Ch. I-V and Ch. X

Source: `data/knowledge_library/sources/how_to_judge_a_horoscope_raman/chapter_001_full-text-unsplit.md`
(OCR of both volumes). Citations are `HTJAH:<line>` in that file. Quotes keep the OCR spelling
("benefice" for benefic, "hy" for "by", and so on).

Range read in full: HTJAH:384-3321 (Ch. I General Introduction, Ch. II Considerations in Judging a
House, Ch. III Determination of Longevity, Ch. IV First House, Ch. V Second House) and
HTJAH:6968-7810 (Ch. X Practical Examples, then the Vol. I index and the Vol. II title pages).

**Read this first: what Ch. X really holds.** Ch. X in this OCR is *not* a reading of whole charts
from start to finish. Pages 155-156 are blank in the OCR (HTJAH:6964-6966), and the chapter's
surviving text starts in the middle of a sentence about the 4th house: "Atma-Karaka and hence
possesses good strength. Saturn lord of the 4th is in the 3rd house" (HTJAH:6974). From there it
covers only the 4th, 5th and 6th houses (Charts 124, 125 and 126) and ends with "OM TAT SAT"
(HTJAH:7163), which closes Vol. I. So **no opening or closing formula for a whole-chart reading
survives in this range.** What survives is the order in which he takes houses and sub-matters
(section 7). The three chart labels are probably three natives, not one. Chart 124 puts the end of
Rahu Dasha at about 1969 (HTJAH:7011-7012), while Chart 126 dates Saturn Dasha 1963-1982
(HTJAH:7159). One native cannot have both.

## 1. The chapters' own stated method

### 1a. Ch. I, General Introduction (HTJAH:384-464)

- "In order to analyse a horoscope properly and thoroughly each house or Bhava should he carefully
  scrutinised" (HTJAH:388-389).
- When a textbook rule fails in a real chart: "conclusion should not he drawn that the principles of
  astrology are contradictory. On the other hand, a further analysis of the relations and
  interrelations of planets should he made" (HTJAH:390-392).
- "no hard and fast rules can he laid down for guidance, except the hroad principles"
  (HTJAH:396-397).
- Weigh the pros and cons: "Significations have been assigned to the various dignities and debilities
  of planets, the pros and cons of which must be carefully considered. No prediction should be given
  in a haphazard manner. If the fifth house is well disposed, and its lord occupies favourable
  situations the subject will be happy with regard to children" (HTJAH:436-439). His example is the
  same Jupiter exalted as Lagna lord against Jupiter debilitated as 4th lord: "Common-sense tells us
  that the results must differ" (HTJAH:442-446).
- The house significations follow (HTJAH:446-464). For the 2nd: "family, face, right eye, food,
  wealth, literary gift, and manner and source of death, self- acquisition and optimism"
  (HTJAH:450-451).

### 1b. Ch. II, Considerations in Judging a House: the general method, in full and in order (HTJAH:468-646)

Opening principle: "In examining a house due importance should be given not only to the Rashi but
also to the Navamsha and other appropriate diagrams" (HTJAH:470-471).

His eight factors, verbatim and in his order (HTJAH:471-495):

1. "The strength, aspects, conjunctions and location of the lord of the house." (HTJAH:474)
2. "The strength of the house itself." (HTJAH:476)
3. "The natural qualities of the house, of its lord and the planets in it or having aspects." (HTJAH:478)
4. "Whether the influence has been altered by any yogas occurring in any particular house or houses." (HTJAH:480-482)
5. "The exaltations and debilitations of the house lords are equally important." (HTJAH:484)
6. "The situation—favourable or adverse—of the house-lord or the lord in whose house the particular
   house- lord is situated, in the Navamsha." (HTJAH:486-487)
7. "The age, position, status and sex of the subject." (HTJAH:489)
8. "Each sign has certain planets well-disposed or ill- disposed. For instance for those born in Aries
   the Sun is good. The relation between the house lord (Mars) and the Sun is very important and its
   proper consideration should not be overlooked." (HTJAH:491-493)

"All these must be properly weighed before any result can be deduced." (HTJAH:495)

Decision rules in the paragraph that follows (HTJAH:497-508):

- Lord's strength governs: "If the strength of the lord is full or complete there will be in general
  a good influence on the house" (HTJAH:497-498). "The Adhipati or lord of the house is responsible to
  an immense extent for the control of the house" (HTJAH:502-503).
- A sentence that is hard to read: "Among the many events comprehended by a house, those are to be
  selected with which the planets are not concerned" (HTJAH:498-499). The OCR prints it so; the sense
  is unclear.
- Aspects: "The good or bad nature of the aspects to which a lord or the house itself is subjected
  plays no mean part" (HTJAH:499-500). "The benefice and malefic qualities of the planets should be
  taken under all conceivable relations. The occupations and avasthas of planets go a long way in
  directing the results" (HTJAH:500-502).
- **Conflict rule (the house can override a bad lord):** "But if in a particular case, the lord is
  badly placed, and the house itself has good conjunctions and aspects then evil results should not be
  predicted" (HTJAH:503-505).
- **Timing rule, as Ch. II states it:** "When the Dasha or Bhukti of a house lord comes, the results
  of the Bhava in question will be realised" (HTJAH:505-506). Example: a weak Lagna lord in the 7th
  aspected by a malefic shows more than one wife, so "During the Dasha or Bhukti of such a lord,
  wife's death may be predicted" (HTJAH:506-508).

Functional benefics and malefics for each Lagna (HTJAH:519-604). The table for each Lagna is at
HTJAH:525-566. The general rule:

- Benefic lords: "(1) First house lord, other than the Moon; (2) the 5th and 9th lords
  (Thrikonadhipathis); (3) and (4) lords of the 4th, 7th and 10th when they do not happen to be
  natural benefices" (HTJAH:575-576). "The lord of the 9th is decidedly more benefice than the lord of
  the 5th" (HTJAH:578).
- Malefic lords: "(1) Lords of the 3rd, 6th and 11th houses; (2) lords of the 4th, 7th and 10th houses
  (Kendradhipathis) if they happen to be natural benefices" (HTJAH:583-584). "the 11th lord is the
  worst malefic, the 6th less malefic while the 3rd the least malefic" (HTJAH:584-585).
- Neutrals: the Moon as Lagna lord; the Sun or Moon as 8th lord, or as lords of the 2nd and 12th
  (HTJAH:587-588).
- The kendradhipati blemish lapses in its own kendra (HTJAH:599-604).
- He states it is for Dasha use: "The details will be useful when predicting the results of Dasha"
  (HTJAH:521).

Yoga karakas and raja yoga (HTJAH:606-646):

- A planet owning a kendra and a trikona (HTJAH:613).
- A kendra lord associated with a trikona lord, where the acting karaka is the one "not blemished
  either by the other ownership (such as 3rd, 6th, etc.) or by associating with lords of 3, 6, 8"
  (HTJAH:622-625). The 9th-10th lord combination is the strongest (HTJAH:625-626).
- Strong combinations "can overcome, to a large measure, the slight blemish caused by other malefic
  lordships" (HTJAH:627-628).
- Orb: "The Yoga can really be of some substantial effect provided the planets causing the yoga are
  within 12° of each other" (HTJAH:643-644).
- **Reference point:** "In measuring the yogas or even the general nature of the horoscope, the
  starting point should be either the ascendant or the Moon, whichever is stronger" (HTJAH:644-646).

### 1c. Ch. III, Determination of Longevity: the maraka method (HTJAH:761-967)

- Order: first the span class and balarishta, then the marakas. "Before the period of death is
  actually determined one should be able to find whether the longevity is long, medium or short, and
  whether there are Balarishta combinations threatening early death" (HTJAH:763-765).
- Primary: "The 3rd and 8th are houses of life and the 2nd and 7th are houses of death. (i) Lord of
  the 2nd or 7th is a determinant of death; (ii) the (malefic) occupants of these two houses, and (iii)
  the (malefic) planets in association with the above lords" (HTJAH:776-781).
- Ranking: "planets in conjunction with lords of 2nd and 7th are the most powerful in causing death,
  while the lords themselves are least powerful" (HTJAH:783-784).
- Secondary: benefics associated with the 2nd or 7th lord; the 3rd and 8th lords; a 3rd or 8th lord
  associated with the 2nd or 7th lord (HTJAH:790-791).
- Tertiary: "(i) Saturn in conjunction, association or aspect with the Maraka ... (ii) lord of the
  6th or 8th whether or not he is associated with a Maraka planet, and (iii) the least powerful planet
  in the horoscope" (HTJAH:793-795).
- He rejects the classical exemptions: "It is suggested in certain works that the following planets
  will not cause death, (i) The Sun and the Moon and (ii) the 9th lord. In actual practice, however,
  this is not found to be true" (HTJAH:795-797).
- The maraka list for each Lagna is at HTJAH:800-814, followed at once by his own caveat:
  "Instances are wanting to prove that the above assignment of so-called Maraka always holds good"
  (HTJAH:818-820).
- Transit: "Of course in the determination of death, the transiting influences have a great bearing"
  (HTJAH:932).
- Infant death: "Generally if the Eagna, the Sun and the Moon are afflicted, the child dies early"
  (HTJAH:963-964).

### 1d. Ch. IV, First House: method statements (HTJAH:971-1793)

- **Bhava over Rashi, and Navamsa equally:** "In the delineation of any house, it is the Bhava that is
  important and not the Rashi" (HTJAH:973). "The Navamsha is also equally important ... It is the pivot
  on which the horoscope revolves" (HTJAH:974-978). "Every combination mentioned should be applied to
  the Rashi, Bhava and Navamsha charts and then a conclusion drawn" (HTJAH:979-980).
- **Four elements:** "Judgment is the summing up of the influence of planets in a horoscope. Several
  elements are required for judging a horoscope, viz-, (a) the house, (b) its lord, (c) its occupants,
  and (d) its Karaka or indicator" (HTJAH:983-985).
- Three things to judge about the lord: "Judgment should therefore be based upon the (a) position of
  the house lord, (b) aspects and association of the house lord, (c) the yogas, etc., formed by the
  house lord and other modifications. This holds good in the Navamsha also" (HTJAH:989-991).
- Satyacharya on the Lagna: "should all be based on the strength or otherwise of the Eagna and the
  Karaka" (HTJAH:993).
- A placement result is general and must be qualified by the lord's condition: "The exact nature of
  the results should be interpreted by taking into account the strength, weakness and other factors
  attendant upon the house lord ... The same rules apply to all Bhava" (HTJAH:1003-1007).
- **A benefic aspect vetoes an evil reading:** "If there are any beneficial aspects this evil should
  not be predicted" (HTJAH:1087). "Provided favourable aspects exist, this result should not be
  predicted" (HTJAH:1101).
- Dispositor: "The native will have a sickly constitution if the lord of the house in which
  Lagnadhipati is situated is also disposed unfavourably" (HTJAH:1101-1103). "Even when Lagnadhipati is
  strong but the lord of sign occupied by Lagnadhipati is blemished, the benefice results decrease"
  (HTJAH:1146-1148).
- Nakshatra rule: a Lagna lord in the 3rd, 5th or 7th star from the Janma Nakshatra intensifies evil
  or lessens good (HTJAH:1142-1146).
- Three Lagnas: "In analysing Lagna Bhava, due attention must be paid to the dispositions of Chandra
  Lagna and Navamsha Lagna also" (HTJAH:1148-1149).
- Dwirdwadasa: "that horoscope is a fortunate one in which planets are not disposed in DwirdwaDasha
  positions (12th and 2nd from each other)" (HTJAH:1139-1140).
- Mind from the Moon: "The mental disposition should always he judged by reference to the Moon and his
  disposition" (HTJAH:1231).
- **Procedure for a planet in a house:** "First see whether in the first house, any planet is exalted
  or debilitated and whether the planet in the first house has caused any special yogas, etc. (This
  principle applies to all other houses also.) Then modify the reading accordingly" (HTJAH:1482-1484).
  His summary: "(a) consider the nature of the rising planet, (b) whether it is in debility or
  exaltation, in a friendly or inimical sign, (c) note the aspects it receives from the planets—the
  houses from which aspects are cast and the conjunctions the rising planet has, (d) the nature of the
  sign—movable, immovable, common, airy, watery etc. Finally, blend all the influences carefully"
  (HTJAH:1493-1497).
- The chart as a whole: "The horoscope should be considered as a person and not as a set of
  disconnected planetary influences" (HTJAH:1473-1474).
- The three-factor sufficiency rule: "the strength or otherwise of Lagna, the Sun, and of course the
  Moon also. A steady flow of fortune is generally assured if at least two of the three factors named
  above are well disposed" (HTJAH:2111-2113).

**"When Do Indications of the First House Fructify?" (HTJAH:1568-1629)**

- Lifespan first: "Whether the native of the horoscope is likely to enjoy the Dasha of the ascendant
  lord or for that matter, the Dasha of the lord of any house in consideration. For knowing this, the
  longevity should be previously ascertained" (HTJAH:1578-1580).
- Five timers: "(a) Lord of the 1st house, (b) planet or planets aspecting the 1st house, (c) planet or
  planets posited in the 1st house, (d) planets aspecting the lord of the 1st house and (e) planet or
  planets in association with the lord of the 1st house. The same consideration holds good with
  reference to the other houses" (HTJAH:1586-1589).
- Grading: "(1) the sub-periods whose lords influencing the first house in the major periods of whose
  lords also influence the 1st house, produce results predominantly of the 1st house. (2) The
  sub-periods whose lords are associated with the 1st house, in the major periods whose lords are not
  associated with the 1st house, will produce results pertaining to the 1st house only to a limited
  extent" (HTJAH:1592-1596).
- Double function: "those in which two functions (out of the 5 mentioned supra) are combined will be in
  a position to influence the house most" (HTJAH:1625-1627).
- Definition: "A planet is said to influence a house if it owns, occupies or aspects the said house or
  its lord" (HTJAH:1628-1629).

**"Nature of the Results" (HTJAH:1631-1793)**

- "(i) the sub-period of another influencing the 1st house, who is associated with the main lord, will
  produce results pertaining to the 1st house— par excellence" (HTJAH:1635-1637).
- "(ii) The sub-period of a planet influencing the 1st house who is not associated with the main lord
  will produce only ordinary results" (HTJAH:1639-1640).
- "(iii) The good or bad nature of the results depends upon whether a particular lord is benefice or
  malefic, whether he is favourably or unfavourably disposed towards the particular Lagna, and whether
  he is a Raja-Yoga Karaka or a Maraka planet" (HTJAH:1642-1644).
- "(iv) The major lord or minor lord (influencing the 1st house) will produce effects, pertaining to
  the 1st house in its relation to the other houses with which the lord may have anything to do by way
  of ownership, aspect, location, etc." (HTJAH:1646-1648).
- Combining the two periods: "the influence of both the planets (major and minor lords) are to be
  compounded and the relation between them taken into account" (HTJAH:1702-1704).
- Navamsa test on Dasha results: the Lagna lord in the 6th, 8th or 12th of the Navamsa from the other
  house lord reverses or lessens the results. The rule is repeated for each house (HTJAH:1717-1789).
  Close: "all those peculiarities have to be considered and then a consistent conclusion drawn"
  (HTJAH:1792-1793).

### 1e. Ch. V, Second House: method statements (HTJAH:2314-3318)

- The same four elements: "cognisance must he taken of all the important factors referred to on page
  21 while dealing with the first house: viz-, (a) The House, (b) Its Lord, (c) Its Occupants and Id)
  The Karaka or Indicator. The several yogas—good and had—formed hy the second lord or in the second
  house will produce their own effects. These considerations hold good in the Navamsha also"
  (HTJAH:2317-2320).
- Balance: "The celebrated Satyacharya insists upon YUKTA VICHARYA ... all sources of strength and
  weakness of the planets should be evenly balanced before pronouncing an opinion" (HTJAH:2427-2429).
- **Timers for the 2nd, now six:** "(a) Lord of the 2nd house, (b) planet or planets aspecting the 2nd
  house, (c) planet or planets posited in the 2nd house, (d) planets aspecting lord of the 2nd, (e)
  planet or planets in association with lord of the 2nd, and (f) lord of the 2nd from the Moon"
  (HTJAH:2584-2586).
- **A third grade appears:** "Likewise, the sub-periods whose lords are not associated with the 2nd
  house, in the major periods whose lords are associated with the 2nd, will produce effects pertaining
  to the 2nd house only to a feeble extent" (HTJAH:2597-2599).
- Navamsa in Dasha judgement: "one should carefully consider the position of the 2nd lord and the
  planet in question, not only in Rashi but in the Navamsha also. There should be a balanced weighing
  of the favourable influences" (HTJAH:2703-2705).
- Arudha Lagna: "In my own humble experience, Arudha Lagna should be given as much importance as the
  usual Janma Lagna" (HTJAH:2517-2518). Its 11th shows just or unjust means of earning
  (HTJAH:2521-2522).
- Chandramangala Yoga conditions: dignified, in the 2nd, 9th, 10th or 11th, conjunct or in mutual
  aspect (HTJAH:2879-2896).
- Gajakesari "occurs in the I2th, 6th, or 8th from Lagna, it cannot advance the horoscopic indications
  for good" (HTJAH:2950-2951). A yoga can work in one sphere and not another (HTJAH:2951-2954).
- Dhana Lagna method (HTJAH:3247-3272). Take the kalas of the 9th lords from the Lagna and from the
  Moon, take the sum mod 12, and count from the Moon. Planets in it, aspecting it, or in kendra or
  trikona from it "will also give wealth in the course of their Dasha and Bhuktis, while those
  situated in 3, 6, 8 and 12 from this special Dhana Lagna will destroy wealth" (HTJAH:3269-3272).

## 2. Per-chart tables

Abbreviations: **H** = the house itself (sign, occupants, aspects on it); **L** = house lord
(placement, dignity, conjunctions, aspects); **K** = karaka (Sun for H1, Jupiter for H2, others named);
**Nv** = Navamsa; **M** = counted from the Moon; **Y** = yoga or special combination (kartari,
neechabhanga, raja yoga, parivartana, Chandramangala); **DD** = Dwirdwadasa (2/12) spread of planets;
**Desc** = description of physique, character or life; **T** = timing.

### 3c. Verdict vocabulary: a graded scale for each factor

He grades each factor separately on a verbal ordinal scale and then combines the grades:

- "very powerful" / "very strong" (14)
- "fairly powerful" / "fairly strong" / "fairly good" (12, 13, 22, 45)
- "moderate" / "moderately good" / "middling" (12, 14, 22, 27, 29, 43, 48)
- "ordinarily disposed" (44)
- "feebly strong" (45)
- "somewhat blemished" (22)
- "weak" / "certainly weak" (23, 31)

### 3d. Which factor decides when they conflict

- **Stated:** the lord dominates ("responsible to an immense extent", HTJAH:502-503), but a good house
  can override a badly placed lord (HTJAH:503-505).
- **In practice the verdict goes by count.** It follows how many of the three factors are good:
  - "all the three factors ... unfavourably disposed" (20) or "weak" (23) gives a weak result.
  - "all the three factors ... strong" gives immense wealth (40).
  - A mix gives a middling verdict (22, 44, 48).
  - The rule printed at HTJAH:2111-2113 is "two of three well disposed" for a steady fortune.
- **Aspecters can outvote the lord.** In Chart 126 a weak 6th lord's evil "will not only he helied hut
  also he replaced hy henefice effects" because the aspecting Sun and Mars are strong in vargas
  (HTJAH:7151-7155).
- **Jupiter as antidote.** "Jupiter's presence in the ascendant is a strong factor acting as an
  antidote for other malefic influences" (HTJAH:2283-2284).
- **The Navamsa restores a Rasi verdict.** Chart 12: the lord is bad in the Rasi, "In the Navamsha,
  however, Saturn is with Jupiter in the sign of Venus ... Hence the strength of the ascendant lord is
  also fairly good" (HTJAH:1806-1808).
- **"Foundation" overrides.** "Since the foundation of the horoscope is strong, the general disposition
  is sound" (Chart 27, HTJAH:2144-2145). In Chart 5, Purnayu from an exalted, unafflicted Lagna lord
  overrode the marakas (HTJAH:915-917).

### 3f. Concluding formulas

  the house.**

  (20, 23, 33); "Because all the three factors connected with the second house are strong ..." (40).

minus the Moon's, which gives Capricorn; Jupiter or Saturn crossing that sign or its trines is the
trigger. Raman's general statement of who can kill the mother: "The function generally falls on the

**Patterns:**

1. **The bhukti lord carries the specific event more often than the dasa lord.**
   - Chart 4: the 7th lord's dasa gives death, but in the bhukti of the 7th lord's associate.
   - Chart 38: the Venus bhukti is "the important one".
   - Chart 39: the Rahu bhukti blinds.
   - Chart 40: the Mercury bhukti brings the empire.
   - Chart 44: the Venus bhukti ruins.
   He grades no worked period with the printed words "par excellence", "ordinary", "limited" or
   "feeble". **The tier vocabulary is never applied to a worked chart in this range.**
2. **Both lords are almost always in the house's timer set** (11, 13, 38, 39, 40, 42), as rule (1) at
   HTJAH:1592-1594 requires. The exceptions are Chart 44, where Ketu enters through the 6th
   (debts), and Charts 3 and 7, where the bhukti's link is not stated.
3. **Lordship counted from the Moon is used for timing:** Charts 4, 38 and 45 (and 3, 6 by position
   from the Moon). This is the "(f) lord of the 2nd from the Moon" of HTJAH:2586. In Chart 4 he
   already uses it for death timing, which is a 1st-house matter at the maraka level.
4. **Nodes act as proxies.** Ketu gives the Sun's results (40); Rahu is a maraka by occupying the 7th
   (4); Rahu kills the mother as the malefic in Jupiter's sign associated with the 4th lord (124).
5. **Transits appear only twice (5 and 124), always after the dasa, and only Saturn and Jupiter:**
   - Saturn in the 2nd from the Moon at death (HTJAH:933).
   - Saturn in the 12th from the Moon, the start of sade sati (HTJAH:7018-7019).
   - Jupiter in a trine of the Sun-minus-Moon sign (HTJAH:7013-7018).
   Neither Rahu nor Mars is used as a transit trigger in this range.
6. **Lifespan screens the dasas, as stated.** "Since the longevity is about 75 years. Mars, Jupiter,
   Saturn and Mercury can influence the 1st house both as major and minor lords while Venus can give
   the effects of first house only as minor lord" (HTJAH:1621-1623).

## 5. Twists specific to each house

**H1**
- Karaka: the Sun ("Thanu Karaka"). In practice the Moon is added for the mind, making a three-way
  test of Lagna, Sun and Moon: "the trinity of life—^Lagna, the Moon and the Sun representing the body,
  mind and soul" (HTJAH:1873-1874); rule at HTJAH:2111-2113.
- The output is mostly physique and temperament. He reads it as the sign's description, blended with
  the Lagna lord and with the planets aspecting the Lagna or its lord. Health goes to the 6th: "The
  exact nature of his ill- health should be ascertained from the 6th house" (HTJAH:1208-1209).
- Movable, fixed or common signs of the Lagna, its lord and the Navamsa Lagna decide travel
  (HTJAH:1117-1119, Charts 8 and 9).
- Kartari on the Lagna: Rahu in the 2nd and Saturn in the 12th mean thieves (HTJAH:1127-1129,
  1193-1195); kartari also means an "unsteady career" (35).
- Contact between the 1st and 6th lords is bad: litigation (18).
- Planets massed in the 10th, or in the 1st and 10th, are a strength marker (8, 19). Six in the 10th
  can still fail (33).

**H2**
- Karaka: Jupiter, the Dhana-karaka, for wealth. For the eyes he switches to the **Netra-Karaka (the
  Sun)** (39, HTJAH:2734). So he chooses the karaka by the sub-matter.
- **The 6th-house link means debts.** "Note lord of the 2nd and the 2nd house have no connection with
  the 6th or 6th lord. The subject will therefore contract no debts" (43). The 6th lord aspecting the
  2nd shows hard-won, modest wealth (48).
- **The 2nd from the Moon** shows how money is spent: Saturn and Rahu there make him "spend his
  earnings on evil purposes" (44).
- **Wealth yogas specific to the 2nd:** Chandramangala (46, 47); Gajakesari (stated only); Dhana Lagna
  (49-51); Arudha Lagna and its 11th (stated only); the Drekkana tables of "important years" for each
  Lagna (HTJAH:2960-3227). He never applies these tables to a chart.
- 2nd as maraka: "Depending upon the other lordship of the second lord and his affliction, the native
  may die or separate from his wife" (HTJAH:2639-2640).

**Longevity (Ch. III)**
- Life houses are the 3rd and 8th; death houses the 2nd and 7th. Associates outrank lords. The 6th or
  8th lord and Saturn are tertiary. He refuses the Sun/Moon and 9th-lord exemption.
- In practice he overrides his own table: in Chart 5, Purnayu from the Lagna lord defeats the listed
  marakas and the killer is found in the Navamsa; in Chart 6 the 8th lord kills. He states as much:
  "Instances are wanting to prove that the above assignment of so-called Maraka always holds good"
  (HTJAH:818-820).

**Ch. X, the 4th, 5th and 6th (beyond my houses, noted for the procedure)**
- He uses Jaimini as a **confirmation pass** after the Parasari reading: "The same conclusion can be
  arrived at by an examination of the Matru-Bhava as per the system of Jaimini" (HTJAH:6990); "The
  same conclusion follows even as per Jaimini" (HTJAH:7101). He uses a chara Matru-karaka, the 4th
  from the Arudha Lagna, and the Upapada.
- **Varga attainment counts as strength:** Gopuramsa (6981, 7153), Simhasanamsa (7153), vargottama
  (6982, 7026).
- **Counting the number of children:** Navamsas completed by the 5th cusp, by the 5th lord, by the
  planet aspecting the 5th, and by the planet in the 5th from the Moon. Then a majority count: "6 is
  the number almost uniformly indicated" (HTJAH:7111-7116).
- **The "three classes" rule in the 6th:** "Three classes of planets have influence in connection with
  any Bhava—the lord of the Bhava, planets in the Bhava and planets aspecting it" (HTJAH:7138-7151).
  Here the karaka is left out.
- **Viparita-type relief:** "The lord of the 6th in the 8th constitutes yoga. His debilitation is more
  than relieved by his being retrograde" (HTJAH:7160-7161). This is the only retrograde rule in the
  range.

## 6. Where his practice departs from his own stated rule

1. **The order of factors.** Ch. II lists the house lord first and the house second (HTJAH:474-476).
   In every labelled worked example (12-14, 39-45, 48), and in 18/29 H1 and 9/9 H2 readings, he
   starts with the house and then takes the lord. His practice matches the Ch. IV list ("(a) the
   house, (b) its lord, (c) its occupants, and (d) its Karaka", HTJAH:984), not Ch. II's numbering.
   He folds the occupants into "the house".
2. **Bhava over Rashi** is stated as a rule (HTJAH:973). He applies it explicitly only three times:
   Ketu in the Lagna sign but in the 12th Bhava (17, HTJAH:1945); the Lagna lord "in the 9th house
   (8th Rashi)" (14, HTJAH:1862); the Sun "in the 2nd Bhava" (48, HTJAH:2937). Ch. X adds the Moon "in
   the 7th Bhava" (6976) and the 4th Bhavamadhya (7039). Everywhere else he counts by sign.
3. **Ch. II factor 7 (age, position, status, sex)** is never invoked as a reasoning step. Factor 6
   (the Navamsa of the lord's dispositor) is never applied. Only the lord's own Navamsa position is
   read.
4. **The timer set grows beyond the printed list.**
   - Chart 12's Jupiter and Chart 13's Saturn qualify only through the **Navamsa**. Ch. IV's
     "This holds good in the Navamsha also" (HTJAH:991) allows this, but the five-factor list at
     HTJAH:1586-1589 does not mention it.
   - Chart 40's Ketu qualifies as proxy for the Sun.
   - Chart 44's Ketu qualifies through the 6th.
   - The H1 list has no karaka, yet the H1 influence lists sometimes cite the Thanu-karaka's
     aspecter (Jupiter "aspects ThanuKaraka in Amsa", HTJAH:1844-1845) as a reason for influence.
5. **Grade words are never used in the worked periods.** The printed scheme (par excellence /
   ordinary / limited / feeble) appears only in the method sections. The worked charts state the
   event and, at most, the lords' links.
6. **The Ch. II timing rule** ("When the Dasha or Bhukti of a house lord comes", HTJAH:505) is
   narrower than his practice. Most of his timed periods (39, 40, 41, 44) are run by occupants,
   aspecters, the lord from the Moon or node proxies, not the house lord.
7. **The maraka table against the cases.** He prints the table and then shows cases where it fails
   (5, 6). His practice is the three-tier determinant scheme plus Navamsa and transit, not the
   table.
8. **The stated balancing rule is lord-centred** (HTJAH:497-505). In practice the aspecters can
   outvote a weak lord (126), and a single Jupiter in the Lagna can act "as an antidote" (35).
9. **Rules stated and never applied in the range:**
   - the Lagna lord in the 3rd, 5th or 7th star from the Janma Nakshatra (HTJAH:1142-1146);
   - the Navamsa 6/8/12 test between two house lords for dasa results (HTJAH:1717-1789,
     2639-2695);
   - the Drekkana tables of "important years" (HTJAH:2960-3227);
   - the Arudha Lagna's 11th (HTJAH:2521-2522) in Ch. V. The Arudha Lagna appears only in
     Ch. X's Jaimini check.
10. **OCR and text cautions.**
    - Chart 17 is printed "Chart No. 11" (HTJAH:1939).
    - The Chart 21 text says "Compare this horoscope with the Chart No. 21" (HTJAH:2016) when it
      means Chart 20.
    - HTJAH:1191 attributes the thieves case to "Chart No. 8", but the Rahu-in-2nd and
      Saturn-in-12th positions it describes are Chart 9's (HTJAH:1184).
    - The Chart 5 death date is printed 7-8-1940 (HTJAH:921).
    - Chart diagrams are mostly unreadable OCR fragments. The positions above are taken from
      Raman's prose.
