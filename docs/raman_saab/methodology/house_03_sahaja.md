## Three Pillars — House / Lord / Karaka / Secondary

Raman states the analysis factors explicitly: "(a) The strength or otherwise of the Bhava, (b) the Karaka, (c) the lord and occupants." (HTJAH-I:3330-3331)

| Pillar | What to assess | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| **House (Bhava)** | Strength of the 3rd sign itself; whether occupied/aspected by benefics or malefics; benefic disposition minimises evil from the lord | HTJAH-I:3330, HTJAH-I:3362, HTJAH-I:3669-3670 |
| **Lord (3rd lord)** | Functional placement across the 12 houses + its **own karaka role**; the lord's results "out of which only a few may pertain to the third house" must be filtered (HTJAH-I:3415-3417); friendly 3rd lord in a Trikona from Lagna or with Lagnadhipati "generally assures a family of brothers" (HTJAH-I:3423-3424) | HTJAH-I:3334-3424 |
| **Karaka — Mars (Kuja / Bhratru-Karaka)** | The natural significator of brothers; assessed jointly with the 3rd lord; if Mars or 3rd lord is debilitated, combust, or with inimical planets → "destruction of the indications of the 3rd house" (HTJAH-I:3436-3437) | HTJAH-I:3428, HTJAH-I:3765, HTJAH-I:3811, HTJAH-I:3832 |
| **Secondary** | Planets in association with / aspecting the 3rd lord; planets occupying and aspecting the 3rd; planets in association with / aspecting the Karaka; the 11th for the elder brother | HTJAH-I:3523-3527, HTJAH-I:3444-3445 |

Note: when the 3rd lord **is itself Mars**, lord and Karaka collapse into one planet, so "Mars being the Karaka is subject to the same influences as the third lord" (HTJAH-I:3832; also HTJAH-I:3765, HTJAH-I:3852).

## Main Considerations — cited

- The three factors (Bhava / Karaka / lord+occupants) govern a correct appreciation of 3rd-house indications. (HTJAH-I:3330-3332)
- Results of the 3rd lord in different houses must be **adapted to the individual chart**; the 3rd lord in various positions gives many results "out of which only a few may pertain to the third house." (HTJAH-I:3415-3417)
- Beginners err by mis-applying these principles and "bring the science into contempt and disrepute." (HTJAH-I:3417-3419)
- A 3rd lord in a beneficial Nakshatra and Navamsha, aspected by benefics, with Mars equally well disposed → a man of character, brave and straightforward. (HTJAH-I:3421-3423)
- One needs good memory, ready powers of analysis and the ability to weigh "varied and conflicting evidence." (HTJAH-I:3744-3746)

## Timing of Fructification — governing factors + dasha rule

**Governing factors** (same principle as 1st & 2nd houses — house first, then lord, then Karaka): the four influences that fructify 3rd-house results in main or sub-periods are (HTJAH-I:3521-3527):
1. (a) the 3rd lord;
2. (b) planets in association with / aspecting the 3rd lord;
3. (c) planets situated in and aspecting the 3rd;
4. (d) the Karaka (Mars), and planets in association with / aspecting the Karaka.

**Strength gradient of sub-periods** (HTJAH-I:3529-3535):
- Sub-periods of planets connected with the 3rd, inside the major period of a planet connected with the 3rd → results "par excellence."
- Sub-periods of planets associated with the 3rd, inside a major period whose lord is **not** associated → results to a **limited** extent.
- Sub-periods of planets not associated, inside a major period whose lord **is** associated → effects to a **limited** extent.

**Dasha rule for birth of siblings** (HTJAH-I:3537-3540): Predict birth of brothers/sisters during the Dasha (or Bhukti) of the lords of the **3rd, 9th, 11th and 7th**; birth may also occur in the Dasha of the 3rd lord or "the most powerful of the planets occupying the third."

When Mars *is also* the 3rd lord (Aries/Scorpio-type Lagnas), lord and karaka collapse into one planet and share affliction — do not double-count, but do flag the collapse (HTJAH-I:3765, HTJAH-I:3832, HTJAH-I:3852).
- **Three-pillar evaluator:** score (a) Bhava strength, (b) Karaka Mars, (c) 3rd lord + occupants. The **strongest** of {3rd house / 3rd lord / Mars} is the deciding factor for the sibling-count routine (Charts 52-54 all pick the strongest then read its Navamsa).
- **Varga routing:**
  - **D9 (Navamsha) is load-bearing**, not optional. (1) Sibling **count** = number of Navamsa gained by the strongest of {3rd lord, Mars, planets-in-3rd} (HTJAH-I:3447-3449). (2) Elder-brother count = Navamsa passed by the **11th** (Uttara Kalamrita, HTJAH-I:3449-3453). (3) Younger-brother count = Navamsa of the 3rd Bhava *yet to pass* (HTJAH-I:3453-3454). (4) Many dasha rules test whether the 3rd lord is **6th/8th/12th from another lord in the Navamsha** (4th, 5th, 7th, 8th lords) — implement a "Navamsha-relative-house" check.
  - **D3 (Drekkana)** = classical-tradition siblings varga; **NOT invoked in HTJAH-I §VI** — Raman's sibling counts here are exclusively from D9 Navamsha (D12 for ear/throat). Treat D3 as an optional external-tradition overlay, never as the source of the worked-example counts.
  - **D12 (Dwadasamsa):** ear defects/deafness "particularly in Dwadasamsa" — route throat/ear/deafness afflictions through D12 (HTJAH-I:3468).
- **Reference frames:** evaluate the 3rd from **Lagna** AND from the **Moon (Chandra Lagna)** — Chart 57 and Chart 63 both cross-check "from the Moon." Several rules are Moon-frame specific (HTJAH-I:3726, HTJAH-I:4090).
- **Elder vs younger split (critical gotcha):** the 3rd house is for **younger** siblings; **elder** brother is read from the **11th** (HTJAH-I:3444-3445, HTJAH-I:3449-3453). Do not lump all siblings under house 3. "Sun in 3rd kills *elder* brothers" specifically targets the 11th-significator domain (HTJAH-I:3353-3354).
- **Brother-vs-sister sex determination:** odd signs + masculine-planet aspects on Mars/3rd lord → brothers; even signs + feminine-planet aspects → sisters; the 3rd "has more to do with feminine planets" tilts toward sisters (HTJAH-I:3439-3441, HTJAH-I:3700-3703). Implement a masculine/feminine planet & odd/even sign tally between the 1st and Nth Navamsa to apportion the count (Chart 53 method).
- **Affliction taxonomy that denies brothers** (Charts 58-62): debilitation, combustion, conjunction with inimical planets, **Papakarthari Yoga** (malefic enclosure, esp. in Amsa), dusthana placement (6/8/12) of lord or Karaka, and Saturn's aspect from a Kendra. Any of these on lord *and/or* Karaka → "destruction of the indications" / no brothers (HTJAH-I:3436-3437).
- **Dasha trigger sets:** birth of siblings fires in Dasha/Bhukti of lords of **3rd, 9th, 11th, 7th**, or of the strongest planet occupying the 3rd (HTJAH-I:3537-3540). "Mars afflicted" Dasha → death of a brother (HTJAH-I:3553-3554).
- **Special-sign mitigation:** Mars in 3rd has its evil "largely modified" if the 3rd sign is **Capricorn, Aries or Scorpio** (HTJAH-I:3486) — encode as a sign-based dampener.
- **Lord-result filtering:** the 3rd-lord-in-12-houses table mixes generic-life results with sibling-specific results; "only a few may pertain to the third house" (HTJAH-I:3415-3417) — tag each effect as `sibling` vs `general` so the engine can isolate house-3 signal.
