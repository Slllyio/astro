# How Raman reads a horoscope: the procedure in his worked charts (HTJAH)

Date: 2026-10-25. Source: *How to Judge a Horoscope*, Vol I and II, every worked chart (about 395
house readings across Charts 1-258 of both volumes, Ch. X Charts 124-126 and Ch. XVII Charts A and
B). Citations are `HTJAH:<line>` in

This document is the synthesis: what Raman does first, in what order he weighs things, how he
concludes, and how he times. It describes his PRACTICE. Where the practice differs from the rule he
prints at the head of the chapter, both are given (section 7).

One caution runs through everything below. Nearly all of Raman's conclusions are **retrodictive**:
he knows the native's life and explains it ("has given", "resulted in", "is responsible for",
HTJAH:16582-16583, 8719-8720). His order of reasoning is therefore the order in which he *explains*,
which is the best evidence we have of the order in which he *judges*, but it is not a blind forward
reading.

## 1. The procedure in one page

**Before any house (done once per chart)**
1. Fix the functional benefics, malefics and yogakarakas for the Lagna (HTJAH:519-646).
2. Take the Lagna or the Moon, "whichever is stronger", as the starting point (HTJAH:644-646).
3. Settle the span of life (long, middle, short, balarishta) before timing anything (HTJAH:763-765).
   The span decides which dasas can ever operate.

**For each house (one chapter = one house; inside it, one matter at a time)**
4. **The house itself**: sign nature, then occupants, then aspecters, each named with its lordships
   and labelled good or bad; end with a one-word grade on the house.
5. **The lord**: house position, sign dignity, associates, aspecters, hemming; grade.
6. **The karaka** of the matter: same sub-order; grade.
7. **Considered from the Moon**: the same house and lord counted from the Moon, briefly.
8. **The Navamsa** is folded into each of 4-6 as a last sentence ("In the Navamsha ..."), adjusting
   the grade up or down, not as a separate verdict.
9. **Star lords** where they matter (decisive for longevity and for the father).
10. **Conclusion**: "[house grade]; [lord grade]; [karaka grade]. Hence [event]."

**Timing (only after the verdict)**
11. Strike out every dasa that cannot run in the relevant years (span, marriageable age).
12. Choose the dasa and bhukti lords by the kind of event (section 5): the house's own
    influence set for its good events, the yoga's own planets for rise and fall, marakas
    counted from the relative's house for a relative's death, the seven-slot list for marriage,
    and the span window plus marakas from four reference points for the native's death.
13. The bhukti lord usually carries the specific event.
14. Nodes act through their sign lord, star lord, a conjoined planet, Sanivad Rahu / Kujavad Ketu.
15. Transit last, if at all, as an "accelerator" of what the dasa has already shown.

## 2. Before any house

### 2.1 Functional nature for the Lagna

He fixes the lords' functional nature first, and says it is for dasa work: "The details will be
useful when predicting the results of Dasha" (HTJAH:521). Benefic lords are the Lagna lord (not the
Moon), the 5th and 9th lords, and kendra lords that are not natural benefics; "The lord of the 9th
is decidedly more benefice than the lord of the 5th" (HTJAH:575-578). Malefic lords are the 3rd, 6th
and 11th lords and kendra lords that are natural benefics; "the 11th lord is the worst malefic, the
6th less malefic while the 3rd the least malefic" (HTJAH:583-585). Yogakaraka: a planet owning a
kendra and a trikona (HTJAH:613), effective "provided the planets causing the yoga are within 12° of
each other" (HTJAH:643-644).

In practice this table governs every later step: functional status regularly overrides natural
status (section 4.3).

### 2.2 The span of life comes first

"Before the period of death is actually determined one should be able to find whether the longevity
is long, medium or short, and whether there are Balarishta combinations threatening early death"
(HTJAH:763-765). He then uses the span to prune dasas in every house, not only the 8th: "Since the
longevity is about 75 years, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn and Mercury can influence the 1st house both as
major and minor lords while Venus can give the effects of first house only as minor lord"
(HTJAH:1621-1623). In Chart 92 a child is denied partly because the timer comes too late: "If the
native had good longevity, perhaps he would got an issue in Jupiter Dasha" (HTJAH:5650).

### 2.3 His eight general considerations (Ch. II)

He prints them once, for every house (HTJAH:471-495): the lord's strength, aspects, conjunctions
and location; the strength of the house; the natural qualities of house, lord, occupants and
aspecters; yogas; exaltation and debility of house lords; the house lord and its dispositor in the
Navamsa; the subject's age, position, status and sex; and the Lagna's friendly and unfriendly
planets. "All these must be properly weighed before any result can be deduced" (HTJAH:495).

His printed order puts the **lord** first. His worked order puts the **house** first (section 3).

## 3. The house reading: the template

### 3.1 The headings he prints

In most chapters the worked charts carry literal headings, always in this order:

> The [N]th House → The [N]th Lord → Karaka → Considered from the Moon → Conclusion → (timing)

So the template is firm for the houses of relatives and of the body (1st-5th, 7th-9th, 11th).
It loosens where the matter is a *kind* of thing to identify (the 10th's profession, the 12th's
expenses and spirituality, the 6th's diseases): there he starts from the known fact and finds the
factors that explain it.

### 3.2 The opening move

He opens with the house itself in almost every templated chart: "In Chart No. 210 Cancer, a fruitful
sign, is the 11th house neither aspected nor occupied by any planet" (HTJAH:16570). An empty,
unaspected house is stated, never skipped. The sign's nature is named when the matter calls for it:
fruitful or barren for brothers and children, movable or watery for travel, common signs for
plural marriages. The opening sentence usually ends in a one-word grade: "The 7th house has no
afflictions" (HTJAH:8565-8567); "There are no benefice influences on the 7th house which is
considerably afflicted" (HTJAH:8647-8648).

### 3.3 Order inside each factor

For the lord and the karaka the sub-order is the same in every chapter:
house position (kendra, dusthana, "12th from" the house) → sign dignity (own, friend, enemy,
exaltation, debility, neechabhanga checked) → associates → aspecters → hemming (papa or subha
kartari) → Navamsa last, as "the only relieving feature" or a further affliction (HTJAH:3849-3850,
3758-3762).

### 3.4 The grade scale

Each factor gets its own verbal grade on an ordinal scale of about five steps, which he then
combines:

- House: "very powerful", "fairly strong", "moderately strong", "feebly strong", "not sufficiently
  strong", "considerably fortified".
- Lord and karaka: "feebly blemished", "blemished", "moderately blemished", "considerably
  afflicted", "severely afflicted", "powerless".

(HTJAH:1801-1815, 4485-4486, 5819; full lists in the per-chapter files.)

## 4. How he weighs good against bad

These are the decision rules visible across the chapters, with the strongest examples.

### 4.1 Two of three carry the verdict

With house, lord and karaka graded, the verdict usually goes with the majority. "All the three
factors ... strong" gives immense wealth (Chart 40); "all the three factors ... unfavourably
disposed" gives a weak result (Chart 20). Stated outright for the 5th: "Since the house and the lord
are considerably afflicted, the Karaka's moderately benefice disposition should be ignored"
(HTJAH:5729-5730). The 2nd-house rule prints the same count: two of three well disposed for steady
fortune (HTJAH:2111-2113).

### 4.2 Which factor wins a split, by matter

| Matter | Decider | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Brothers (3rd), children (5th) | the lord usually outweighs the house | HTJAH:3696-3697, 3854-3855 |
| Mother (4th) | the Moon decides *whether* she dies early; house and lord decide *how long* she lives | HTJAH:4435-4436, 4619-4620 (ages 3, 16, 36, 46, 52) |
| Education (4th) | Jupiter gives knowledge; house and lord give schooling: two separate questions | HTJAH:4856-4858, 4903-4904 |
| Father (9th) | the karaka Sun: "Afflictions to the pitruKaraka ... are very important" | HTJAH:13126-13127 |
| Longevity (8th) | Lagna lord against 8th lord, then Saturn moves the band; star lords override dignity | HTJAH:11415-11416, 11703-11704, 11796-11798 |
| Profession (10th) | the strongest sign or factor in the chart, even the Lagna over the 10th | HTJAH:14521, 15368-15370 |
| Gains (11th) | yogas linking the 2nd, 5th, 9th, 11th lords outrank the 11th house | HTJAH:16930-16931 |
| Elder brothers (11th) | agreement of Lagna and Moon views | HTJAH:16583-16584 |

### 4.3 Functional nature overrides natural nature

A malefic that is the yogakaraka or the house's own lord is defused: "Saturn in the 7th from Venus is
not so harmful ... because he is a Yoga Karaka" (HTJAH:8748); "Saturn's aspect on the 7th cannot
delay marriage as he happens to be the 7th lord himself" (HTJAH:8594-8595). A natural benefic that is
a functional malefic does not help: "The aspect of Venus on the 7th house is not very helpful since he
is a functional malefic" (HTJAH:8930-8931); "Venus no doubt aspects Jupiter but even this aspect is
not good because Venus is lord of 6 and 11" (HTJAH:2218-2219). Where both natures pull, he names both:
"Saturn's presence in the fourth is good as lord of Lagna and bad as a natural malefic" (HTJAH:4405),
and asks the astrologer to "balance" them (HTJAH:5791-5793).

### 4.4 One benefic sets a floor; it does not make the house good

"Only Jupiter's aspect on the 7th house from the Moon has prevented a complete break-up although life
itself is miserable" (HTJAH:8696-8697). "The aspect of the Sun on the 11th house prevents denial of
elder co-borns" (HTJAH:16725). So the outcome is graded: denial (no redeeming benefic, HTJAH:8669-8670)
→ the thing comes but suffers (one benefic) → the thing comes and lasts (benefics prevail).

### 4.5 A blemish becomes a quality or a duration, not a cancellation

- On a good 7th: "The afflictions result in temperamental clashes but ... result in a stable
  marriage" (HTJAH:8517-8518).
- On a strong 11th whose lord is blemished: the throne came, but "the gains acquired during Saturn's
  Dasha could not last long" (HTJAH:17246-17251).
- On a well-placed father: "the native's father could enjoy Raja-Yoga results but his longevity was
  curtailed" (HTJAH:12974-12976). Quality and duration are judged separately.

He sizes the blemish in words ("slightly", "only", HTJAH:17105-17106, 18562-18563) and his most
frequent construction is "no doubt ... but", in which the blemish usually wins the sentence
(HTJAH:5638-5639).

### 4.6 Named devices that cancel a blemish

Neechabhanga (HTJAH:1822-1823); own sign ("This blemish is cancelled because Mars is in his own
house", HTJAH:2740-2741); an exalted benefic's aspect (HTJAH:2743-2744); distance in degrees
("nearly 20 degrees behind Rahu so that they are free from the effect", HTJAH:2196-2197); Bhava
position ("Ketu is actually in the 12th Bhava. Thus Lagna is free", HTJAH:1945-1946); the malefic
being the dispositor ("Jupiter is the depositor of Saturn ... his aspect will not cause much
mischief", HTJAH:7033-7035); bhava sandhi disqualifying an occupant (HTJAH:13989-13991).

### 4.7 The Navamsa adjusts; it does not reverse

The Navamsa comes last inside each factor and moves its grade: "In the Navamsha, however, Saturn is
with Jupiter ... Hence the strength of the ascendant lord is also fairly good" (HTJAH:1806-1808).
Between the two, the Rasi wins: "The moderate affliction to which Jupiter is subjected to in Navamsha
is more than counterbalanced by his strong disposition in Rashi" (HTJAH:4688-4689); a Navamsa
exaltation alone is "not sufficiently strong" (HTJAH:3807-3809). This is the one-step rule the
engine adopted on 2026-10-24.

### 4.8 Star lords decide where dignity misleads

"The asterism occupied by a planet is very important in judging its significations"
(HTJAH:13239-13242). "Superficially ... does not ... indicate Purnayu. But a closer look will reveal
8th lord Venus occupies his own constellation" (HTJAH:11796-11798). "If in Chart No. 96, Jupiter had
occupied Visakha, the father would have lived longer" (HTJAH:13344). Used in 25/43 death charts and
11/12 father charts, rarely elsewhere.

### 4.9 Counting (brothers, children)

For the number of siblings or children he counts and then subtracts: planets in the 11th and 12th
from Lagna and Moon averaged; Navamsas gained by the strongest planet ("Mars is in the 4th Navamsha
indicating 4 elder co-borns", HTJAH:16675); then one off for each malefic acting ("reducing the
total number by 2", HTJAH:16752-16753). Sex comes from the planet's or sign's sex (HTJAH:18575-18578).
The multipliers in Charts 106 and 108 (treble, double) follow no rule he states.

## 5. Timing: how he chooses the period

### 5.1 Order of operations

1. The house verdict is settled first; timing is always the last paragraph.
2. **Prune by age.** Every dasa that cannot run at the right time is struck out: "Mercury Dasha ends
   in childhood ... Jupiter Dasha and Saturn Dasha operate very late" (HTJAH:8549-8551); "Saturn
   Dasha will come in the native's 90th year, which is far too late" (HTJAH:11193-11195).
3. **Choose lords by the kind of event** (5.2).
4. **The bhukti carries the event.** In Chart 4 the 7th lord's dasa gives death, in the bhukti of
   the 7th lord's associate; in Chart 38 "the Venus bhukti" is the important one. The same bhukti
   can give a marriage and its destruction (Charts 21, 22, 23).
5. **Check the MD-AD relation** where rank or survival is at stake (5.4).
6. **Transit last** (5.5).

### 5.2 Five different selection rules, by kind of event

This is the most important finding. Raman does not use one timing rule for everything.

| Event | Who gets the period | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| A good event of the house itself (wealth, office, a vehicle, a child) | the house's **influence set**: lord, occupants, aspecters, planets with or aspecting the lord, the lord from the Moon; for gains also the 2nd lord and the wealth-yoga planets | Charts 38-45, 85 (car), 209, 219-229; Gains rule HTJAH:16795-16801 |
| Rise and fall in rank (raja yoga) | the **constituents of the yoga** (yogakaraka, 9th/10th lords, parivartana partner), or a node reaching them by star or sign | Charts 199-207 |
| Death of a relative (mother, father, brother, spouse) | treat the relative's house, its lord or its karaka as a Lagna, and take its **marakas**: 2nd and 7th lords, occupants of the 2nd, 7th, 8th or 12th from it | mother 64-73; father 85-97 (10/11 counted from the 9th, 7 from the Sun); brothers 210-212; husband 24-25 ("in the house of loss from the 7th house", HTJAH:9191-9192) |
| Marriage | a **fixed ordered list** of seven: lords of the Rasi and Navamsa occupied by the 7th lord; Venus; the Moon; the dispositor of the 2nd lord; the 10th lord; the 9th lord; the 7th lord or its occupant; then age pruning, then the strongest bhukti | HTJAH:8524-8527; Charts 1-3, A, B |
| The native's death | the **span window** first, then each lord shown to be a maraka from **Lagna, the Moon, the Navamsa Lagna or the Navamsa Moon**, often through its star lord; associates of the 2nd/7th lords strongest, the lords themselves weakest (HTJAH:783-784) | Ch. XII, 43 charts |

The printed "When do indications fructify" list (lord, occupants, aspecters, associates, lord from
the Moon, karaka) is the rule for the first row only. For deaths he uses marakas, as his own
sub-rules for the mother and father say (HTJAH:7003-7005, 12929-12932).

### 5.3 Nodes and star lords

A node gives the results of its sign lord, its star lord, or a planet it joins: "Ketu must give the
results of the Sun, lord of the 11th" (HTJAH:2761-2765); "Rahu being a shadowy planet should give the
results of Mars whose star he occupies" (HTJAH:4478-4482); Kujavad Ketu and Sanivad Rahu (Charts 35,
85, 86, 95). Every node dasa or bhukti in the death charts is justified through one of these
(about 20 cases). A planet in its own star cannot kill through its own dasa: "because the Moon in
this case is in his own constellation, he could not cause the death of the mother in his own Dasha"
(HTJAH:4515-4516). A planet whose dasa falls outside the window "passes on his killing powers"
to a node (HTJAH:11707-11709).

### 5.4 The relation between dasa and bhukti lords

Graded only for rank and death: 3/11 is "ideal" (HTJAH:14116), mutual kendra or mutual aspect good,
2/12 and 6/8 bad (HTJAH:11222-11223, 18583-18584), and Saturn with Venus "fatal to Raja-Yogas"
(Chart 206).

### 5.5 Transits

Rare, and always after the dasa. Across the whole book: about 7 charts in houses 3-6, 2 in the
1st/2nd/Ch. X, 1 in the 8th (Chart 35), 0 in the 9th, 10th, 11th and 12th chapters, and 2 marriage
transits in Ch. XVII.

- **Saturn** is the transit planet in nearly every case: sade sati (Chart 64), Saturn over the
  Janma Rashi (114) or the Lagna point (119), Saturn in the 2nd from the Moon at death (Chart 5).
- **Chart 35's death rule** is Saturn returning to a trine of its own natal Rasi and Navamsa sign:
  "When Maraka Dasha is operating the momentum of the last signal for death is given by AyushKaraka
  Saturn in his transits" (HTJAH:11226-11232). It is not among the transit rules he lists at
  HTJAH:10995-11033.
- **Jupiter** appears for marriage (over the sign of Lagna longitude + 7th-cusp longitude, Charts A
  and B) and for the mother's death (over the Sun-minus-Moon sign or its trines, Chart 124).
- He calls transits "accelerators" (HTJAH:4462-4463, 6718-6719). Only Chart 119 is timed by transit
  alone. He never uses vedha or ashtakavarga bindus in a worked chart.

## 7. Where his practice departs from his printed rules

1. **The four timing grades are never used.** "Par excellence", "ordinary", "limited", "feeble" are
   printed in every chapter's rule (HTJAH:1592-1596, 3531-3535, 4316-4320, 16398-16404, 17738-17746)
   and never applied to a worked chart in either volume.
2. **The influence-set timing rule does not time deaths.** Marakas from the relative's house do
   (5.2).
3. **House before lord.** Ch. II lists the lord first (HTJAH:474); every templated chart opens with
   the house.
4. **Karakas differ from the printed lists.** The 12th's stated karaka Saturn is used 0/26 times; the
   10th's karaka is never a timer; the eyes use the Sun (Chart 39).
5. **The 12th lord kills** in three death charts (51, 63, 82) though it is not in his maraka list,
   and his own maraka table fails in his Charts 5 and 6 (he admits "Instances are wanting",
   HTJAH:818-820).
6. **Stated rules never applied**: the Navamsa 6/8/12 test between lords, the drekkana tables of
   important years, the special death-period rules of Ch. XII, the Jupiter marriage transit in Vol
   II's 7th chapter.
7. **Self-contradictions**: the 3rd lord in the 11th; Venus in the 7th.
8. **Text defects found** (not corrected): Chart 58's death in "1903" for what seems to be Queen
   Victoria's birth (she died 1901); "Chart No. 212" printed in Chart 214's text (HTJAH:16712); "8th"
   for "9th" in Charts 98-99; year "7253" (HTJAH:17160); HTJAH:17336 unreadable. Several natives
   recur under new numbers (listed in the per-chapter files).

## 8. What this means for the engine (observations; nothing changed)

These are leads to check against the code, each needing its own review and pre-registration
before anything is built. None has been measured.

- **Timing by kind of event.** The engine's timer set (fixed on 2026-10-24) is Raman's influence
  set, which is correct for good events of a house. Raman times relatives' deaths by marakas
  counted from the relative's house or karaka, and raja-yoga rise and fall by the yoga's own
  planets. Worth checking how the engine times a mother's or father's death and a career rise.
- **The grade words.** The engine prints par excellence / ordinary / limited / feeble on every
  period. That is faithful to his rule, but he never uses the grades himself; the report could say
  so.
- **The 11th's gains timer** in Raman also takes the 2nd lord and the wealth-yoga planets
  (HTJAH:16795-16801).
- **The 12th's karaka.** Raman prints Saturn but never uses it; the engine follows the print.
- **Two of three.** Raman's verdict is usually a tally of three graded factors with a
  matter-specific tie-break (4.2). The engine's judge weighs pillars differently; this may bear on
  the over-calling of affliction seen in hold-out 2 (41% exact).
- **Blemish as duration or quality** (4.5) matches the yoga-blemish proposal of 2026-10-24.
- **Transits** are a minor, late trigger in Raman's practice, mostly Saturn. The engine's transit
  layer is far richer than anything he applies in a worked chart.
