## 0. Executive procedure (synthesis; every step is cited below)

1. **Lifespan window first.** Before reading any dasa, fix the longevity span and ask which
   dasas the native will actually live through. A planet whose dasa falls outside the span can act
   only as a bhukti lord (HTJAH:1578-1583, 1620-1623; HTJAH:5309-5314).
2. **Build each house's influence set** (the "timer set"): (a) the house lord, (b) planets that
   aspect the house, (c) planets in the house, (d) planets that aspect the lord, (e) planets
   conjoined with the lord. From H2 on, add (f) the lord of that house counted from the Moon, and
   for some houses (g) the karaka (HTJAH:1586-1589, 2582-2585, 4308-4311, 8270-8274, 12735-12738).
   "A planet is said to influence a house if it owns, occupies or aspects the said house or its
   lord" (HTJAH:1628-1629). A planet that combines two of these functions influences the house most
   (HTJAH:1624-1628).
3. **Grade the MD/AD pair for that house** (HTJAH:1592-1596, 1635-1640, plus the per-house
   paragraphs; see §2 for the exact wording, which varies by house):
   both lords in the set → par excellence; only the AD lord in the set → limited; only the MD lord
   in the set → feeble / nominal / small. The H1 text further splits the both-in case: if the AD
   lord is *associated* with the MD lord the result is par excellence, otherwise ordinary.
4. **Fix the quality (good or bad) separately from the intensity.** Quality comes from: the lord's
   functional status for the Lagna (benefic, malefic or neutral lord; yogakaraka; maraka); its
   dignity, sign and Navamsa; its aspects and associations; the other houses it owns, occupies or
   aspects; and its nakshatra lord (HTJAH:1642-1648; HPA-24:80-86, 102-107, 123-128;
   HTJAH:4518-4520).
5. **Compound the AD with the MD.** Read the AD lord's results through the MD, taking "the
   relation between them" into account (HPA-24:137-142). Mutual 6/8 or 2/12 → unfavourable
   (HPA-24:150-153). Kendra, trikona, 3/11 or samasaptaka → favourable (NH and HTJAH examples, §4).
   The lords of the 5th and 9th in each other's periods → good (HPA-24:155-157).
6. **Nodes:** Rahu and Ketu give the results of their sign dispositor, of the planets they
   conjoin, of their nakshatra lord, and by the dicta *Sanivad Rahu* / *Kujavad Ketu* also of
   Saturn and Mars respectively (§1.8).
7. **Sub-divide in time where the text supports it:** first half versus second half of a dasa
   (benefic from the Moon but not from the Lagna; position in the first or second half of the
   sign); the hora of the sign for the two halves of a bhukti; antara for fine timing (§1.10, §3).
8. **Transit (gochara) is a secondary trigger**, never the primary judgment (HTJAH:11038-11040;
   HPA-24:109-110).
9. **Death:** run the maraka scheme (§5) inside the lifespan window. Transits are applied last.

## 1. General rules for judging a dasa lord

### 1.1 The dasa lord gives what it signifies by ownership, association, location and aspect
- HPA-24:102-107: "5. In the course of a dasa, a planet produces such results as it indicates by
  virtue of ownership, association, location and aspect. For instance, favourable position of the
  lord of the 2nd will give wealth during his dasa."
- HPA-24:123-128: "10. If the lord is well placed ... he will do good regarding the house or houses
  with which he may have anything to do either by way of ownership or aspect or location during his
  period, while if the lord is weak and ill-placed he will inflict misery."
- HPA-24:80-86: "The lord of the dasa has a quality of his own stamped upon him. This will again be
  modified by the nature of the house, the nature of the sign, the nature of the aspecting bodies,
  the favourable and unfavourable situation of the lord of dasa in the navamsa and various other
  factors."
- HTJAH:1645-1648 (H1 "Nature of the Results" (iv)): "The major lord or minor lord (influencing the
  1st house) will produce effects, pertaining to the 1st house in its relation to the other houses
  with which the lord may have anything to do by way of ownership, aspect, location, etc. Thus, in
  case of Taurus ascending with Mars posited: Mars in his own dasha may produce effects of the 1st
  house in its relation to the 7th house."
- HTJAH:505-508: "When the Dasha or Bhukti of a house lord comes, the results of the Bhava in
  question will be realised."
- HTJAH:3665-3667: "In predicting results of Dasha or Bhuktis, due attention must be paid to all
  shades of influences covering a particular Bhava or house. A lord is capable of conferring the
  results of not only the house but those due to Karaka nature of planets joining or aspecting the
  Bhava."
- HTJAH:3521-3523 (H3): "First take the house, then the lord and finally the Karaka. Planets in
  association with or aspecting the lord and Karaka will imprint their characteristics also."

### 1.2 Functional benefic, malefic and neutral by Lagna (ownership)
- HTJAH:1642-1644 (H1 (iii)): "The good or bad nature of the results depends upon whether a
  particular lord is benefice or malefic, whether he is favourably or unfavourably disposed towards
  the particular Lagna, and whether he is a Raja-Yoga Karaka or a Maraka planet."
- HTJAH:518-520: the Lagna lists of benefics and malefics are given because "The details will be
  useful when predicting the results of Dasha." The table follows at HTJAH:522-550.
- Ownership scheme (HTJAH:556-583): the benefic lords are the 1st lord (other than the Moon), the
  5th and 9th lords, and the 4th/7th/10th lords when they are natural malefics. The 9th is more
  benefic than the 5th, the 10th more than the 7th, and the 4th least. The malefic lords are the
  lords of the 3rd, 6th and 11th (11th worst, 3rd least), and the kendra lords when they are natural
  benefics. Neutrals are the Moon as Lagna lord, and the Sun or Moon owning the 8th, 2nd or 12th. A
  kendra lord sitting in its own kendra loses the kendradhipati effect (HTJAH:584-591).
- HPA-24:94-96: "3. The dasa period of the lords of the 6th and 8th produce harmful results unless
  they acquire beneficence otherwise."
- HPA-24:130-133: "11. The periods of lords of 5 and 9 are said to be good so much so that the
  periods of planets, which are joined or otherwise related with them are also supposed to give
  rise to good."
- HPA-24:112-115: "7. When lagna (ascendant) is powerful, during the dasa of lord of lagna,
  favourable results can be expected." HPA-24:117-121 (rules 8 and 9): the 2nd lord gives wealth;
  the 3rd lord gives new friends, help to brothers, leadership, and pain if afflicted.
- HPA-17 (key planets per sign) adds per-Lagna exceptions to who kills, for example "If Venus
  becomes a Maraka he will not kill the native" for Aries (HPA-17:39-41), and "The Moon will not
  kill" for Gemini.

### 1.3 Yogakaraka and yoga results in the dasa
- HTJAH:608-612: a yogakaraka confers fame and similar results; "The extent to which he produces a
  yoga depends upon the strength he has gained by virtue of Shadbalas ... association, aspect and
  occupation."
- HTJAH:641-645: a yoga has real effect if its planets are "within 12° of each other". "In
  measuring the yogas or even the general nature of the horoscope, the starting point should be
  either the ascendant or the Moon, whichever is stronger."
- HTJAH:4324-4329 (H4; restated for H7 at HTJAH:8278-8282): "Where a lord is involved in a Raja
  yoga or Arishta yoga, the lord is capable of producing in his Dasha or Bhukti the Raja yoga or
  Arishta yoga results, so far as they relate to the fourth house. ... a Rajabhanga Neecha Yoga ...
  During his Dasha or Bhukti, he can bring about the destruction of the Raja yoga."
- **3HC quantitative yoga-dasa method** (3HC:800-905). Raman's own words (3HC:902-905) say it was
  "developed and applied by me, the germ ... taken from standard astrological works". At 3HC:11091-11093
  he states it "has no warrant in any classical works". The method:
  - Score each yoga-causing planet: +1 for a benefic lord, +1 for each association with or aspect
    from a benefic lord, +1 for exaltation or a friendly sign. Score −1 for each malefic
    counterpart (3HC:846-879). If the positives predominate, the planet confers the yoga's good
    results.
  - "The planet whose inherent strength [shadbala] is more will fulfil the larger part of the
    indications of the yoga in his Dasa while the planet whose shadbala strength is less will fulfil
    his part as a sub-lord to a lesser extent" (3HC:888-892).
  - The nature of the result follows the house where the yoga occurs. Its extent equals the
    planet's **residential (bhava) strength %** (3HC:892-896).
  - Shadbala is always preferred over the saptavargaja "approximate" method (3HC:826-830).
  - Worked case (Chart 1, 8-8-1912; 3HC:970-994). The Moon's dasa falls outside the life, so the
    Moon gives yoga results only as a sub-lord, at 40%. Jupiter gives 90%. The yoga runs "throughout
    Jupiter's Dasa subject to the influences of sub-lords". The Moon bhukti in Jupiter dasa "would
    have been excellent" but is discounted.
  - A planet causing several yogas gives a blend of them, and "the more powerful of the planet or
    planets would give the effects" (3HC:10960-10970). If the positive and negative units are
    equal, the yoga is "practically neutralised" (3HC:1148-1150).
- Udayat Indutovapi dictum (3HC:1797-1800): "any planet in the 10th from Lagna or Chandra would
  give, during his Dasa, much wealth".
- Varahamihira as quoted by Raman (HTJAH:15680-15685). A kingdom is obtained in the dasa or bhukti
  of (1) the planet in the Lagna, (2) the planet in the 10th, or (3) the most powerful planet. It is
  lost in the period of a planet in an inimical sign, or of a planet in the 7th from its own sign.
- Saturn-Venus (HTJAH:16104-16111): "by standard astrological rules the mutual periods of Saturn and
  Venus are fatal to Raja-Yogas ... However, if the Raja-Yoga is powerful enough, the bhanga (loss
  of power) will be set right in appropriate Periods."

### 1.4 Dignity, sign, Navamsa and strength (shadbala)
- HPA-24:48-52: "they should not be applied verbatim. The results do vary according to the strength
  and weakness of the dasa lord." HPA-24:67-78 gives an example: political success in the Sun dasa
  for the Sun in Aries, at maximum only if he is unaspected by malefics and equally strong in the
  Navamsa. Saturn's aspect from Aquarius prevents "complete political success".
- HPA-24 tabulates dasa results **by the sign the dasa lord occupies** (for example the Sun's dasa
  in Aries, its Moolatrikona, Taurus ... Pisces, HPA-24:161-204) and, for the Sun, **by which
  shadbala component is strong**: Sthana, Dig, Kala, Naisargika, Chesta (HPA-24:204-218). For the
  Moon it tabulates by Avaroha (descending from exaltation), Neecha, Moolatrikona and so on
  (HPA-24:353-363). Raman's general comment: "These results are merely general and are to be
  modified with reference to the planet's correct position and the exact nature of strength or
  weakness" (HPA-24:674-677).
- HTJAH:1669-1671 (after the H1 Sun results): "The Sun may be exalted, debilitated, may occupy a
  friendly sign, may be in his own sign and may form important yogas. These results should
  certainly be modified. This holds good with regard to every planet."
- Per-house dasa rules frequently invert on a **Navamsa 6/8/12 relation** between the dasa lord and
  the other lord involved. Examples: "None of the above benefits will accrue if the 2nd lord is in
  an unfavourable Navamsha disposition from the 4th lord" (HTJAH:2651-2652); "If the third lord is
  in the 6th, 8th or 12th from the 4th lord in the Navamsha, then either in the sub-period of the
  3rd lord or in that of the 4th lord (within the Dasha of the 3rd lord) brothers will die"
  (HTJAH:3592-3594). The 4th, 5th and 6th house tables (HTJAH:4344-4373, 5330-5388, 6296-6340) do
  the same thing.
- HPA-24:98-100: "4. The dasa periods of planets in Bhava sandhis (junctional points) will be unable
  to produce their full results." This matches the 3HC residential-strength percentage.
- HTJAH:14270 (H10): "[10th lord] feeble in strength but not afflicted, his Dasha fetches the native
  moderate success and wealth."

### 1.5 The dasa lord's relation to the Lagna and to the Moon (Chandra Lagna)
- Raman counts lordships and positions **from both the Lagna and the Moon** in nearly every worked
  example. Factor (f), the house lord from the Moon, is formally part of the influence set from H2
  onward (HTJAH:2584-2585).
- Halves of a dasa (Mussolini): "From Lagna, Saturn is not a benefic whereas from Chandra Lagna he
  is a benefic. Hence he produced good results in the first half of his Dasa while in the second
  half he necessarily gave rise to very unfavourable results" (NH:10759-10763; also HTJAH:16027-16030).
- HTJAH:1140-1145: when the Lagna lord is not strong and occupies the 3rd (vipat), 5th (pratyak) or
  7th (naidhana) star from the Janma nakshatra, evil is intensified. When it is strong there, the
  good is lessened. And "Even when Lagnadhipati is strong but the lord of sign occupied by
  Lagnadhipati is blemished, the benefice results decrease."

### 1.6 Nakshatra (constellation) lord of the dasa lord
- HTJAH:4512-4520 is the key statement. "In Chart No 66 ... it is reasonable to expect the death of
  the mother in Moon Dasha itself. But because the Moon in this case is in his own constellation,
  he could not cause the death of the mother in his own Dasha. In Chart No. 64 on the other hand the
  Moon is in the constellation of Mars, a Maraka ... and the mother died in the Dasha of Mars.
  **According to Satyacharya, the fact of a planet being situated in a particular constellation is
  of paramount importance especially in the matter of predicting significant events.**"
- HPA-24:144-148 (rule 13): "During the dasa period of a malefic planet when the sub-period of a
  planet ruling the 3rd, the 5th or the 7th star (reckoned from the natal star) is in progress the
  subject will have a period of trial and tribulation."
- Used constantly in the examples. Hitler: the MD lord "owns the constellation from the birth star
  going under the technical name of pratyak", and the Moon "presides over Vipat tara" (NH:12246-12257).
  Golwalkar: "The latter part of Rahu Dasa, consistent with the fact that Rahu is in the nakshatra
  of Mercury lord of the 9th, resulted in ... name and fame" (NH:14584-14586). Marx: "Venus is in the
  constellation of the Sun and hence is capable of producing some of the results due to the Sun"
  (NH:6590-6592). Farouk: Mercury's "situation in the constellation of Mars ... rendered him so
  powerful as to make the native fall from power in his sub-period despite the general strength of
  the major lord" (NH:15257-15260). Indira Gandhi: "Saturn, the Dasha lord, occupies an unfriendly
  constellation" (HTJAH:16107-16108). The longevity chapter judges strength by the nakshatra lord
  throughout (HTJAH:11340-11362, 11730-11778, 11890-11900).

### 1.7 Maraka and badhaka roles
- Maraka: see §5. A maraka lord's dasa or bhukti can kill when it falls within the span. If longevity
  is good it gives separation, illness and similar instead (HTJAH:8321-8322, "If longevity is good,
  then since the 2nd and 7th are Maraka places, the native may suffer separation from wife").
- **Badhaka: not found.** No dasa rule in these Raman texts uses badhaka or badhakadhipati (grep
  "badhak" returns nothing relevant in HTJAH, NH, HPA or 3HC). Do not encode it as Raman doctrine.
- Chidra grahas (HTJAH:10927-10939): the 8th lord, the occupant of the 8th, the planet aspecting the
  8th, the 22nd-drekkana lord, the 8th lord's associate, the lord of the 64th navamsa from the
  Moon, and the 8th lord's bitter enemy. "the strongest of them can cause death in his period".

### 1.8 Nodes (Rahu, Ketu): whose results they give
Raman applies several "results-of" channels at once:
1. **Sign dispositor**: "Ketu should give the results of sign dispositor" (HTJAH:8566); "Ketu
   being placed in Taurus must necessarily give the results of Venus" (NH:8379); "Rahu being in the
   sign of Mercury has to give the results due to the strength of Mercury" (NH:11306-11307).
2. **Conjoined planet**: "Rahu gives the results of Venus (the planet with whom he associates),
   Jupiter (his sign-dispositor) and Saturn (Sanivad Rahu)" (HTJAH:11740-11741).
3. **Nakshatra lord**: "Rahu being a shadowy planet should give the results of Mars whose star he
   occupies" (HTJAH:4477-4478).
4. **Dicta**: *Sanivad Rahu* (Rahu like Saturn) and *Kujavad Ketu* (Ketu like Mars): HTJAH:11195,
   12106-12107, 12432-12433, 12763, 12978; NH:12250-12252 ("he must give the results of Mercury and
   in a secondary capacity those of Saturn also as he is similar in nature to Rahu").
5. **Navamsa dispositor** (secondary): Thyagaraja, "In the Navamsa, Venus, whose results Rahu
   should give, owns the 2nd from Chandra Lagna" (NH:6109-6111).
6. **Maraka delegation**: a maraka whose own dasa falls outside the span "delegates his Maraka or
   killing power" to the node in his sign (HTJAH:11195-11206). A node may take over a function for
   itself when it is stronger ("Ketu should partake of the characteristics of the Sun but as the
   Sun himself has 5 units, Ketu has only 4 ... he would rather assume the responsibility himself",
   HTJAH:11213-11216). As a natural malefic, Rahu "is likely to appropriate to himself this function"
   (HTJAH:7001-7008).

### 1.9 Dasa order effects (HPA)
- HPA-24:88-92: "1. The dasa period of Saturn, if it happens to be the 4th dasa, will be
  unfavourable. 2. The dasa period of Jupiter will be unfavourable if it happens to be the 6th dasa."
  Raman lists these but never applies them in the worked examples read.

### 1.10 First half and second half of a dasa; halves of a bhukti
Raman gives no general rule stated as doctrine. Three applied criteria appear in the examples:
1. **Benefic from the Moon, malefic from the Lagna → good first half, bad second half**
   (Mussolini, NH:10759-10763; HTJAH:16027-16030). The converse is not stated.
2. **Half of the sign**: "Saturn is in the 2nd half of Leo. Consequently during the first half of
   Saturn Dasa Farouk began his reign as a popular prince ... In the second half of Saturn, his
   position became a bed of thorns" (NH:15264-15268).
3. **Hora for a bhukti**: "The Moon is in the second hora of Sagittarius. Therefore it is only the
   latter part of his Bhukti that can prove harmful" (Hitler, NH:12259-12263).
- HPA-24 also records within-bhukti phases from the classical lists, for example Mercury bhukti in
   Rahu dasa: "In the first 18 months ... very busy ... In the latter 12 months enemies increase"
   (HPA-24:714-717).
- Planets in the western half: "If the planets are in the west it indicates rise during the second
   half of one's life" (NH:10752-10753). This is a life-half rule, not a dasa-half rule.

### 1.11 Results must be possible, and consistent with the native's station
- HPA-24:54-72: "incongruous results whenever they occur, should be avoided ... The result should
  be consistent with the person for whom it is intended, under the condition of life in which he is
  brought up as well as the physical possibility at the time."
- HTJAH:5385-5387: "In Interpreting horoscopes, commonsense must be applied to the blending of
  various indications."

## 2. Judging the bhukti relative to the dasa

### 2.1 Compounding rule (HPA)
- HPA-24:137-142: "12. The general effects of the dasa (major period) get modified by the planet that
  rules the particular bhukthi (minor period). In such a case, however, the influences of both the
  planets are to be compounded and the relation between them taken into account."
- HPA-24:150-153: "14. Unfavourable results will be realised when the sub-lord and the major lord are
  situated in the 6th and 8th or 12th and 2nd from each other respectively."
- HPA-24:155-157: "15. The sub-period of the lord of the 5th in the major period of the lord of the
  9th or vice versa is supposed to produce good effects."
- The fixed per-planet bhukti lists in HPA-24 (from line 220) are classical generic results. Raman
  says they must be weighed against strength (HPA-24:48-63).

### 2.2 Mutual position of the MD and AD lords as applied in examples (favourable vs unfavourable)
| Relation (AD from MD) | Raman's verdict | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 3rd / 11th | "an ideal combination for elevation in career" | Nehru, Venus-Ketu, HTJAH:14112-14116 |
| Mutual kendra (4/10, 1/7) | favourable | Nehru, Mars-Rahu "in mutual quadrants" HTJAH:14120-14122; Pius XII NH:9704-9706; Tippu Mars bhukti NH:5711-5712; Milton Saturn bhukti NH:4436-4438; Rahu bhukti in Venus dasa HTJAH:17148 |
| Samasaptaka (7th) | favourable for succession | Tippu, Jupiter bhukti NH:5703-5705 |
| 9th / 5th (trine) | favourable | Rahu dasa Jupiter bhukti marriage, "sub-lord is in the 9th from the major lord" NH:10355; Shaw's marriage NH:7331-7333 |
| 11th | favourable (marriage) | Marx, Venus "the 11th from the major lord Mars" NH:6588-6589 |
| 10th | favourable but can be vitiated | NH:13484-13486 |
| 2nd / 12th (dwirdwadasa) | unfavourable | Milton "deepest degradation" NH:4473-4477; Narasimha Bharathi death NH:7463-7466; HTJAH:11222-11223 (maraka bhukti) |
| 6th / 8th (shashtashtaka) | unfavourable | Indira loss of power HTJAH:16104-16105; Thyagaraja death NH:6108-6109; Vivekananda NH:8140-8143 |
| Mutual aspect | favourable (association) | Indira, "both the Dasha and Bhukti lords are in mutual aspect" HTJAH:16102-16103 |

A 2nd or 7th position from the MD lord is also read as **maraka from the major lord**: "in a house
of death from the major lord" (NH:13797-13798); Hitler: "the Moon becomes a maraka, as he occupies
the 3rd from Lagna and the 7th from the major lord" (NH:12230-12232); HTJAH:12354.

Friendship is used: "Moreover he is a bitter enemy of Rahu" (HTJAH:11221). But no general
naisargika-friendship rule for bhuktis is stated.

### 2.3 Which lord gives which result
- The bhukti lord gives the results of **the houses it owns and occupies and the karakatva it
  carries**. The dasa lord sets the frame. Example: "Venus owns the 7th and 12th houses (both bad)
  and occupies the 2nd Bhava ... and is associated with two first-rate malefics ... Each planet is
  capable of a two-fold function. The results due to the ownership and malefic association
  manifested themselves" (Milton, NH:4448-4456).
- In the dasa of the 6th lord in the 6th: "the sub-period of a planet, joining the above combination,
  would be unfavourable, and the results pertain to the nature of the house owned by the conjoined
  planet ... In the sub-periods of planets, not joining the 6th lord in the 6th fairly auspicious
  results pertaining to the several Bhavas owned by the sub-lords will transpire" (HTJAH:6296-6302).
  Also: "The significations of the Bhava, whose lord joins the 6th lord in the 8th, will be destroyed
  during the sub-period of the former in the major period of the latter" (HTJAH:6325-6329).
- For the 12th house: "Note the Bhava ruled by the planet occupying the 12th, and also the Bhava
  occupied by the lord of the 12th house. During the periods of these lords, loss or trouble in
  respect of these Bhavas can be expected" (HTJAH:17748-17750).

### 2.4 "When do indications fructify": the influence-set grading, verified line by line

**Factor list (H1)**, HTJAH:1585-1589: "(a) Lord of the 1st house, (b) planet or planets aspecting
the 1st house, (c) planet or planets posited in the 1st house, (d) planets aspecting the lord of the
1st house and (e) planet or planets in association with the lord of the 1st house. The same
consideration holds good with reference to the other houses dealt with in the subsequent pages."
From H2 on, Raman adds "(f) lord of the 2nd from the Moon" (HTJAH:2584-2585). Karakas are added

## 3. Antara and pratyantara

- Computation: HPA-13:603-628. Bhuktis "are further sub-divided into still more minute divisions
  called the antarams or inter-periods; these into antarantarams and so on, till swara ... is
  reached. **Nevertheless for all practical purposes the antaram (inter-period) will be found to be
  quite sufficient.**" Raman works one example: Jupiter antara in the Saturn bhukti of Ketu dasa =
  1 month 23 days 4.8 hours.
- **Pratyantara (antarantara) is named but never used for judgment** in any worked chart read.
- Antara is used in judgment in these examples:
  - Mother's death in "Rahu Dasha, Venus Bhukti, **Venus Antara**". Rahu gives Mars's results
    (nakshatra lord), Mars is a maraka from the Matru-karaka, and Venus owns the 8th from the
    karaka (HTJAH:4476-4483).
  - "T.B. was suspected in 1947 in Rahu Dasha, Jupiter Bhukti, **Sun Antara** ... The Sun ...
    occupies the 12th from the major and minor lords" (HTJAH:6635-6640).
  - "Moon Dasha, Venus Bhukti, **Sun Antara** ... The Sun is in the 8th from Lagna having
    DwirdwaDasha position from major and sub-lords" (HTJAH:6646-6650).
  - Marie Antoinette: imprisoned "As soon as the Venus inter-period commenced", and beheaded "just a
    few days before the Antara ended" in Saturn Dasa, Saturn Bhukti, Venus Antara. Venus is in an
    Ayudha drekkana (NH:5909-5962).
  - George VI ascended "in the inter-period of Mercury in Ketu's major period" (NH:13631-13632).
    Raman then calls it "Ketu Dasa Mercury Bhukti" at NH:13637, so his loose usage of
    "inter-period" for bhukti makes this one ambiguous.
  - HPA-14:139-142 (balarishta 15): "the inter-period (antara) of the lord of the 8th in the dasa of
    the lord of the ascendant or vice versa".
- **Method at antara level**: the same logic one level down. The antara lord's own significations
  (maraka, 6th/8th lordship, karaka) are combined with its **position from the MD and AD lords**
  (12th, 2/12). No separate grading scheme is given.
- Varshaphal (annual) dasas are a different system and out of scope.

## 5. Maraka and death timing within dasas (brief)

- **Span first.** HPA-15:21-37: "Suppose, a horoscope indicates middle life ... whereas the dasa of a
  most powerful maraka comes into operation after 80 years. In such a circumstance the period of a
  planet ... even if it is less malicious ... which comes within the maximum number of years allotted
  for Madhyayu, must be determined as bringing about death, in preference to the most malicious one."
- **Primary marakas** (HTJAH:771-783; HTJAH:10903-10916): the 2nd and 7th lords; the (malefic)
  occupants of the 2nd and 7th; the (malefic) planets associated with the 2nd/7th lords. "Of these
  several determinants ..., planets in conjunction with lords of 2nd and 7th are the most powerful
  in causing death, while the lords themselves are least powerful" (HTJAH:783-785).
  - HPA-15 differs slightly: "The 2nd house is always stronger in inflicting death than the 7th.
    Planets who occupy the 2nd are stronger than the planets who own it ... and the planets who are
    in association with the lord of the 2nd are the most powerful" (HPA-15:39-52).
- **Secondary**: benefics associated with the 2nd/7th lords; the 3rd and 8th lords; a 3rd/8th lord
  associated with a 2nd/7th lord. **Tertiary**: Saturn conjoined with, associated with or aspecting a
  maraka; the 6th or 8th lord; the weakest planet (HTJAH:787-797; HTJAH:10918-10924).
- HPA-15 fallbacks: the planet conjoined with the 12th lord, killing in the bhukti of a malefic 3rd/8th
  lord; then the strongest of the 3rd, 6th and 8th lords; and "If the dasa of a death-inflicting
  planet does not at all come into operation, then the most malicious planet in the horoscope will
  cause death. Saturn, inclined to produce evil by his conjunction with the lords of death houses,
  will cause death in preference to any of those planets" (HPA-15:56-99).
- 8th-lord rules (HTJAH:10941-10953): if the 8th lord is in the 6th, 8th or 12th, death comes in the
  8th lord's MD/AD, or in the MD of Saturn's dispositor with the 8th lord's AD, or in the 8th lord's
  MD with the 9th lord's AD. Lagna-lord and Seershodaya/Prushtodaya rules: HTJAH:10953-10974.
- **Moon-based and Navamsa-based marakas** are always added (Ex. 8-12). A maraka-units table counts
  ownership, location and association from the Lagna, the Moon, and both in the amsa
  (HTJAH:11050-11185).
- **Transit last**: "these transits must be looked into only after determining the Maraka Dasha and
  Bhuktis ... Transits are always secondary in importance. They are like catalytic agents. All
  conclusions must be primarily drawn on the basis of Dasha-vichara" (HTJAH:11037-11040). Death by
  transit happens "only when the directional influences in the birth charts warrant. Otherwise
  misfortunes ... may happen" (HTJAH:10989-10993). Saturn over its natal rasi/amsa or their trines is
  the "last signal" (HTJAH:11226-11228).
- Raman's caution: "Instances are wanting to prove that the above assignment of so-called Maraka
  always holds good" (HTJAH:818-819). Certain works say the Sun, Moon and 9th lord do not kill, but
  "In actual practice, however, this is not found to be true" (HTJAH:797-800).

## 6. Precedence when rules conflict (only what Raman states or demonstrably applies)

1. **Lifespan window beats maraka severity** (HPA-15:21-37; HTJAH:1578-1583). A dasa outside life
   is replaced by bhukti-level action or by delegation (HTJAH:11195-11206; 3HC:970-972).
2. **Natal dasa beats transit.** Transit only accelerates or triggers (HTJAH:11037-11040;
   HTJAH:10989-10993; NH:12257-12259; HPA-24:109-110).
3. **Nakshatra lord is "of paramount importance" for significant events.** It can veto a dasa that
   would otherwise deliver the event (HTJAH:4512-4520), and can make a sub-lord overpower a strong
   major lord (NH:15257-15260).
4. **Within marakas**: the associate of the 2nd/7th lord ranks above the occupant, which ranks above
   the lord (HTJAH:783-785). HPA ranks the 2nd above the 7th and adds Saturn with marakas above all
   others (HPA-15:39-52, 96-99). These are two orderings; the HTJAH one is the later and fuller text.
5. **The house's good aspects can override a badly placed lord**: "evil results should not be
   predicted" (HTJAH:503-505).
6. **Strength evidence**: shadbala beats saptavargaja (3HC:826-830). Among co-causers of a yoga, the
   stronger by shadbala fulfils the larger part as MD lord, the weaker as AD lord (3HC:888-892).
   Measure from the Lagna or the Moon, whichever is stronger (HTJAH:643-645).
7. **Two functions beat one**: a planet combining two of the five factors influences the house most
   (HTJAH:1624-1628).
8. **A powerful raja yoga survives a bhanga period**: "if the Raja-Yoga is powerful enough, the
   bhanga ... will be set right in appropriate Periods" (HTJAH:16109-16111).
9. **Possibility and station override literal application** (HPA-24:54-72).
10. **Navamsa adverse relation (6/8/12) reverses or mitigates** the rasi-based dasa promise in the
    per-house rules (§1.4). Example 24 shows the Navamsa kendra relation outweighing a rasi 2/12.
- **Not stated in Raman**: any numeric weighting among house lordship, dignity, nakshatra and MD-AD
  relation. Raman repeatedly says it is a matter of judgment ("It is difficult to lay down any
  particular rules which will apply in all cases", HPA-24:58-60; HTJAH:5302-5309; 3HC:11094-11105).

## 7. Gaps and honest notes for the engine

- **No badhaka** anywhere in these Raman dasa texts.
- **No general first-half/second-half rule.** Only the three applied criteria in §1.10.
- **Pratyantara and finer levels**: named (HPA-13:603-609), never applied. The antara is "quite
  sufficient".
- HPA-24 rules 1-2 (the 4th dasa of Saturn, the 6th dasa of Jupiter) are listed but never applied in
  examples.
- The four-tier grading in CLAUDE.md is a faithful synthesis but not a single printed scheme. See
  §2.4, fidelity notes 1-2. Raman's tier 3 is "limited" in H3/H7 and "nominal/small/feeble"
  elsewhere.
- The 3HC yoga-dasa scoring is Raman's own method, which he explicitly says lacks classical warrant
  (3HC:11091-11093). It is citable as Raman, not as classical.
