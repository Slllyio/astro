## A. General MD/AD meta-rules

- **A1. Dasha tone from natal placement.** "Favourable if at the commencement of the Dasa the Dasa lord be in the Ascendant, exaltation, own or friend's sign; unfavourable if in the 6th, 8th or 12th, debilitation or inimical sign." — **BPHS 47.5–6**.
- **A2. AD lord 6/8/12 from MD lord → bad; kendra/trikona/11 → good.** Stated pair-by-pair through BPHS 52–60 (e.g., 52.13–14 Moon AD; 52.43–44 Saturn AD; positive 58.13–15½ "kingdom… if Venus be in kendra/5/9/11 **from the lord of the Dasa**"). Single-verse codification: **Uttara Kalamrita 5.23½–24½** — "Should the Bhuktinath occupy the 12th, 6th or 8th from the Dasa lord, he becomes hostile… in the 11th, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th, 9th or 10th from the Dasa-nath, greatly beneficial; the 7th and association also favourable." (Note: UK counts 2/3/11 *from the MD lord* as good.)
- **A3. Benefic AD lord unconnected with a malefic MD lord → evil anyway; connected → mixed.** — **Jataka Chandrika 36–37** (= Laghu Parashari); UK 5.24½–25½.
- **A4. Planets associated with the MD lord give the MD lord's results in their ADs.** — **Jataka Chandrika 29**.
- **A5. Carry-over: prior dasha-bhukti's effects continue at the boundary.** — **Jataka Chandrika 28**.
- **A6. Drekkana phase rule** (first/middle/third drekkana → start/middle/end of dasha; retrograde reversed). — **BPHS 47.3–4**.

## B. Raja-yoga formation & WHEN it fructifies

- **B1. Kendra×trikona sambandha = raja yoga.** "If there be an exchange between an angular lord and a trinal lord, or if these two join in an angle or trine… or full aspect between them — one born in such a yoga will become a king." — **BPHS 34.11–12**; "angles are Vishnu-sthana, trines Lakshmi-sthana…" — **BPHS 41.28**.
- **B2. Yogakaraka** = one planet owning kendra+trikona — **BPHS 34.13**; cancelled if it simultaneously owns an evil house — **34.15**; per-lagna lists 34.23–24 etc. Parallel **Jataka Chandrika 20–21**.
- **B3. Dharma-Karmadhipati (9L+10L) yoga.** — **Jataka Chandrika 14–15**; BPHS 39.37 (4L/10L joined 5L/9L → kingdom); BPHS 39.33–34 (9L & 5L mutual aspect → kingdom).
- **B4. Timing: the yoga fruits in the dashas of the Dharma-Karma lords / yoga-participants.** "Raja Yoga comes during the Dasa periods of the lords of Dharma and Karma." — **Jataka Chandrika 17**; "In the Dasas of Raja Yoga Karakas very good effects commence; continued in the Bhuktis of 3/6/11 lords" — **JC 32**; planets conjunct the yogakaraka give success in their bhuktis — **JC 33**; even 3/6/8/11 lords conjoined with powerful raja-yoga planets give its results in their ADs — **JC 18**.
- **B5. "Fructifies in the dasha of the STRONGER of the two" — ⚠ not a literal BPHS verse.** Actual sources: **Uttara Kalamrita 5.28½–29½** ("the stronger one will cause the Yoga", stated for Venus–Saturn) + **BPHS 39.5** (results full/half/quarter per strength).
- **B6. Kendra-lord MD → trikona-lord AD gives the rise (and converse).** — **Jataka Chandrika 31**; BPHS 48.9–13 ("kendra lord in trikona or trikona lord in kendra → extremely favourable dashas").
- **B7. Explicit dasha verse for 1↔10 exchange:** "There will be acquisition of a kingdom **in the Dasas of the lords of the Ascendant and 10th**, if the 10th lord be in the Ascendant and the Ascendant lord in the 10th" (*tayor daśā-vipāke tu rājya-lābho bhaved dhruvam*). — **BPHS 48.16–17½**. Likewise 1↔9 exchange.
- **B8. Raja-yoga bhanga (cancellation).** — **Saravali ch. 39** ("Obstructions to Raja Yogas"): v.2 (two+ of Mars/Sat/Jup/Sun fallen…), v.14 (weak debilitated Moon), v.15 ("Sun in the 10th degree of Libra destroys all Raja Yogas"); BPHS 34.19–22 per-lagna bhanga. Gate all raja-yoga timing on absence of bhanga.

## C. 10th house / 10th lord — profession, rise, recognition

- **C1.** Strong 10L (exalt/own rasi-navamsa) → fame, good deeds — **BPHS 21.2**; weak → obstruction — 21.3; with benefic → royal patronage gains — 21.4. **Dasha verse: BPHS 48.1** — 10L's dasha favourable/adverse per its dignity & house. **BPHS 48.6**: "recognition from and awards by Government in the Dasa of the lord of the 10th" (*daśameśa-daśākāle sammānaṁ nṛpa-saṁsadi*). Phaladeepika **20.11**: strong 10H+10L → in 10L's dasha success in every venture, name and fame.
- **C2.** 9L/10L dashas conditioned by conjunction with 5L; 10L/4L dashas favourable if associated with 9L. — **BPHS 48.9–13**.
- **C3.** 10L-in-house promise inventory: **BPHS 24.109–120** (10L in 9th → "king or equal to a king"; in 2nd → wealthy, honoured; in 6th → bereft of wealth; in 8th → devoid of acts; in 12th → fear from enemies); double-lordship conflict resolution **24.145–148**.
- **C4. The karmajiva rule.** "…acquisition of wealth through [planet in] the 10th from Lagna or Moon; or through **the lord of the navamsa occupied by the lord of the 10th from Lagna, Moon or Sun**." — **Brihat Jataka X.1** (livelihood lists X.2–3, incl. a transit echo). Phaladeepika **5.1–9** repeats it ("whoever is the strongest"); **Jataka Parijata 15.43** with Bhattotpala's dissent (consider all three). ⇒ profession-nature planet P = navamsa-dispositor of L10; P's MD/AD = natural profession-start window.
- **C5.** Planets in the 10th **from the Moon**: Karmasthana = 10th from the stronger of Lagna/Moon — **Saravali 33.2–6**; planet-wise results 33.8–13; pairs 33.14–27.
- **C6.** Fame combinations — **BPHS 21.19–21**.
- **C7.** Royal-association via **Amatyakaraka**: 10L conjunct/aspected by AmK or its dispositor → chief in the king's court — **BPHS 40.1**; AmK own/exalt → minister — 40.4; 10L↔L1 exchange → associated with the king — 40.13.
- **C8.** Rasi-dasha timing: **BPHS 50.71–72** (dasha of a rasi with L1/L9/L10/exalted/benefic yoga → kingdom, opulence); **Jataka Parijata 17.70–71** (Chakradasa of the 10th-bhava rasi → authority, honor by a king).

## E. Dasha-by-lordship rules

- **E1. The 12-lord table — BPHS 48.2–8**: L1 → wellbeing; L2 → distress/death-risk; L3 → unfavourable; L4 → house & land; L5 → education + children; L6 → enemies/ill-health; L7 → distress to wife; L8 → death-risk + **financial losses**; **L9 → unexpected gains of wealth**; **L10 → recognition & awards from government**; L11 → obstacles in gains; L12 → distress/disease. Gate (48.8): planet in trikona at dasha start → good; in dusthana → adverse. Phaladeepika strong/weak split: 20.10 (strong L9 → royal favour), 20.11 (L10), 20.12 (L11), 20.15 (weak L1 → **loss of position**), 20.19 (**weak L10 → failure, exile, loss of honour**; weak L9 → penury), 20.20 (weak L12 → wealth wanes), gate 20.14.
- **E2.** Dashas of 3/6/11 lords, planets in/with them, planets with maraka lords or in the 8th → unfavourable. — **BPHS 48.18–20**.
- **E3.** Dusthana-lord dashas redeemed by trikona-lord association — **BPHS 48.14–17½**; viparita yogas Harsha/Sarala/Vimala — **Phaladeepika 6.63/65/66**.
- **E4.** Dasha of planet **in the 10th**: Sun in 10th → ruling powers, fame in its period — **Sarvartha Chintamani dasha-phala sl. 33** (+ per-planet parallels); SC sl. 19–24: malefic 10th/12th **from the MD lord** in its AD → defame, **loss of position**; benefic → comforts.
- **E5.** Per-planet MD chapters: Sun's dasha + Sun yuti L9/L10, kendra/11, strong → wealth, honours, "high position like Army Chief" — **BPHS 47.7–11**; converse 47.12–15 (punishment, exile); same template Moon 47.16–22, etc.

## F. Wealth gain / loss

- **F1.** Dhana formation: L2 in 2/kendra/trikona → wealth; in dusthana → decline — **BPHS 13.1–2**; 2↔11 exchange or conjunction in kendra/trikona — **13.4**; L11 in 11/kendra/trikona or exalted → many gains — **BPHS 22.2**.
- **F2. Explicit dhana-dasha verse:** "The 9th lord and the 5th lord are capable of bestowing wealth; similarly planets conjunct such a lord. **There is no doubt these planets will give wealth during their Dasa periods.**" — **BPHS 41.16**.
- **F3.** Great-affluence templates (5L in 5th + 11L in 11th, planet-wise) — **BPHS 41.2–8**.
- **F4. Explicit wealth-loss timing verse:** "A planet associated with one of the lords of the 6th, 8th and 12th, bereft of a trinal lord's aspect, **will in its Dasa periods cause harm to financial aspects**." — **BPHS 42.13**; penury formation 42.2, 42.5.
- **F5.** L8 dasha → financial loss (48.5); Phaladeepika 20.24: dashas of L6/L8/L12 among the five troublesome dashas.

## G. Fall / loss-of-position (collected)

- 10L + Rahu in 8th — BPHS 21.6; 10L debilitated + malefics in 10th & 7th — 21.18; Sun+Mars+Sat+Rahu in 11th → "cessation of duties" — 21.11; weak-L10 dasha — PD 20.19; AD in 6/8/12 from MD lord "loss of position" lines — BPHS 52.60–61, 52.69–73, 53.25–28½; afflicted Sun dasha → punishment/exile — 47.12–15; Chakradasa of 12th-bhava rasi → "loss of place, royal displeasure" — JP 17.74–75; SC 20–23; raja-yoga bhanga — Saravali 39 (v.8: "relinquish the kingdom soon").

## H. Popular but NOT found in the classics (flagged)

1. **Marana-karaka-sthana** — no verse in BPHS/PD/Saravali/BJ/JP/UK/SC (full-text searched). Only *Marana Pada* (arudha of 8th) and *Maranavastha* (end-degree avastha) exist — different doctrines. Modern oral tradition (Rath school).
2. **"Raja yoga fructifies in the dasha of the stronger"** as BPHS — actually UK 5.28½–29½ + BPHS 39.5.
3. **"10L dasha gives a job/promotion" stated unconditionally** — classical form is always gated by dignity/house (BPHS 48.1).
4. **"2nd & 11th lords' dashas give wealth"** — BPHS 48.2–8 actually assigns L2's dasha *death-risk* and L11's *obstacles*; wealth requires good placement (F1) — quote the nuance, not the slogan.
5. Santhanam's bracketed glosses are modernizations of *rājya-lābha*.

## I. Fact-schema required to execute these rules

(1) lordships + functional nature (BPHS 34.2–10: trikona lords benefic, 3/6/11 lords malefic, kendradhipatya dosha, Sun/Moon exempt from 8L blemish); (2) yogakaraka per lagna; (3) sambandha detection (conjunction/mutual aspect/exchange/mutual kendra-trikona); (4) dignity incl. combust/retro/vargottama (PD 20.14, 20.22); (5) house position from Lagna, **from Moon**, and **from the MD lord**; (6) navamsa positions (karmajiva, BPHS 48.1 "own Rasi/Navamsa"); (7) chara karakas (AK/AmK); (8) Vimshottari MD/AD + dasha-commencement chart; (9) Jupiter/Saturn transit overlay (BPHS 57.24–27½ — the one place BPHS mixes transit into AD results here).
