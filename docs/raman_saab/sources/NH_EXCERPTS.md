# Notable Horoscopes — verbatim death-verdict excerpts (Run-5 golden registry)

Source: B. V. Raman, *Notable Horoscopes* (later/expanded edition), full text
at archive.org item **NotableHoroscopesBVR** ("Notable Horoscopes_djvu.txt").
One anchored section per registry case (`nh_golden_cases_v2.json` →
`quote_ref`). Quotes are the death-verdict and maraka-mechanism sentences
only (fair-use excerpts; the item id above is the re-fetch provenance).
OCR artifacts left as-is except obvious spacing. Positions are transcribed
in the registry, not repeated here.

Note: this edition contains verdicts postdating the first edition (Nehru
1964, George VI 1952, Rajendra Prasad 1963, Sivananda 1963, Golwalkar 1973)
and explicitly predictive passages for then-living subjects (Hitler, Franco,
Dalmia, Farouk) — the latter are registry-excluded.

## buddha
> "Buddha's death took place at the end of Mars Bhukti in the Sun's Dasa. …
> the Sun is a distinct maraka not only as lord of the 2nd but also as
> associating the lord of the 7th. The sub-lord Mars owns the 2nd and 7th
> from Chandra Lagna and occupies the 7th therefrom with the major lord. …
> This is explained by the fact that the 22nd drekkana has reference to a
> benefic sign."

## alexander
> "Thus his death took place in Mercury Dasa, Moon's Bhukti. … Mercury is
> lord of the 3rd and 6th and in the Navamsa has joined the 3rd in
> association with Jupiter who happens to be lord of 7th both from Lagna and
> the Chandra Lagna. The sub-lord the Moon is not only in the constellation
> of Jupiter who is in a maraka place, but himself occupies a marakasthana
> causing the death." — "it is an Alpayu horoscope."

## augustus
> "he died in A.D. 14 just at the fag end of Ketu Dasa, the sub-period being
> that of Mercury. … Ketu is in the 8th in conjunction with Mars, a
> first-rate maraka, aspecting Lagna lord."

## sankara
> "He died at the age of 32, in Saturn Bhukti in his own Dasa. … Saturn, as
> owning the 7th from Lagna, occupies the 7th from Chandra Lagna and is
> therefore a powerful maraka." — "Lord of the 8th Saturn in the
> constellation of Venus who is almost in conjunction with the Sun, lord of
> the 2nd, a maraka, conferred Alpayu."

## jesus
> "He was crucified in 30 A.D., at the end of Ketu Dasa. … Venus, whose
> results Ketu should give, owns the 2nd house from Lagna and the 3rd and
> 8th from the Moon and occupies the 3rd thus causing maraka."

## nero
> "Finally the revolt of the people and his death took place in Saturn's
> sub-period in Mars' Dasa. … Saturn owns the 2nd and 3rd from Lagna and the
> 7th from Chandra Lagna, while Mars rules the 5th and 12th. In Navamsa Mars
> rules the 3rd and 8th from Chandra Lagna and occupies the 2nd maraka." —
> "He had Alpayu or short life as could be seen from the presence of lords
> of Lagna and the 8th in Panaparas."

## ramanuja
> "He died in Rahu Dasa Mercury Bhukti, when he was 120 years old. … The
> major lord Rahu is in the house of Venus who in his turn, along with the
> sub-lord Mercury, has joined the Sun a powerful maraka planet. … in the
> Navamsa, Rahu is in the 8th while Mercury is in the 2nd aspected by the
> major lord." — "owing to the Purnayu nature of the horoscope…"

## omar
> "He died in 1123, when Venus' Dasa and Saturn's Bhukti was in operation. …
> The major lord Venus occupies marakasthanas both in Rasi and Navamsa. The
> sub-lord Saturn is with Jupiter, a maraka from Chandra Lagna. In the
> Navamsa also he is aspected both by Jupiter and Mercury who are marakas
> respectively from Lagna and Chandra Lagna."

## nanak

> sub-lord Jupiter as owning the 8th is in the 2nd another maraka place."

## chaitanya
> "The Swami died at the age of 47 in the beginning of Jupiter's Dasa. …
> Jupiter owns the 3rd and occupies the 3rd. In the Navamsa Jupiter is the
> lord of the 2nd from Lagna and lord of the 3rd from the Moon and occupies
> the 12th in conjunction with lord of the 8th."

## akbar
> "His own end came on 13-10-1605, Mercury Dasa, Saturn Bhukti. … The major
> lord Mercury is in the 7th from Chandra Lagna and in the 3rd from Lagna
> while the sub lord Saturn, though a yogakaraka, has been rendered capable
> of causing death by his being natural Ayushkaraka, by his owning the 8th
> from the Moon and by joining Jupiter lord of the 7th Chandra Lagna. He
> occupies a death-inflicting house (2nd) in the Navamsa."

## milton
> "His death took place in 1674 in Rahu Bhukti in Saturn Dasa. … The major
> lord Saturn owns the 3rd and occupies the 2nd maraka while the sub-lord
> Rahu is in the 3rd from the Moon, and the latter planet who is to give the
> results of Rahu is in a maraka house."

## aurangzeb
> "His death took place in the beginning of 1707, when he was having Mercury
> Dasa Saturn Bhukti. … The major lord Mercury is a powerful maraka, as he
> owns the 8th from Lagna, and the 2nd from the Moon and has joined the Sun,
> lord of the 7th. … the disposition of the major and sub-lords is
> shashtashtak (6th and 8th). Note also the fact that Saturn was transiting
> Taurus and thus causing sadesathi for the third time."

## shivaji
> "The great Shivaji died in Saturn Dasa Mars Bhukti. … Saturn is definitely
> a maraka both by ownership and position while Mars rules the 3rd and 8th
> from Chandra Lagna both in Rasi and Navamsa."

## hyderali
> "His death took place on 7-12-1782 in Mars Dasa Jupiter Bhukti. … Mars is
> a definite maraka as he owns the 2nd and 7th from Lagna and occupies the
> 2nd. The sub-lord Jupiter owns the 2nd from the Moon and 3rd and 6th from
> Lagna and has joined the major lord and Ayushkaraka in the 2nd."

## goethe
> "He died in 1832 in Moon Dasa Mars sub-period. … Mars is lord of the 7th
> and occupies the 2nd while in the Navamsa he is in the 3rd. The Moon owns
> the 2nd in the Navamsa. Thus Mars overpowered the major lord and inflicted
> death."

## tippu
> "In Saturn Bhukti of Jupiter Dasa, the English stormed Srirangapatna and
> Tippu died bravely as a soldier … on 4th May 1799 … Jupiter though Lagna
> lord proved a maraka by virtue of his being aspected by two powerful
> marakas Saturn and Mercury and his being disposed in the 2nd from Chandra
> Lagna." — "Lagna lord weak, Ayushkaraka afflicted and the 8th lord in a
> malefic sign conferred Madhyayu."

## marie_antoinette
> "Her death occurred in Venus Antara, Saturn Bhukti, Saturn Dasa. … Death
> takes place in captivity when the 22nd Drekkana happens to be either
> Nigala, Sarpa or Pasa. In this case the 22nd Drekkana is the first of
> Capricorn and hence a Nigala one … As Mars aspects the 8th and as
> Ayushkaraka is also in a Nigala Drekkana, the queen had to meet her end by
> being guillotined. … Saturn owns the 8th house and occupies the 8th."

## thyagaraja
> "Sri Thyagaraja died in Rahu Dasa Jupiter Bhukti at the age of 85. … Rahu
> is in the 7th both from Lagna and the Moon. Saturn whose results Rahu is
> to give is lord of the 7th and 8th. In the Navamsa he is in the 8th from
> Lagna. The sub-lord Jupiter is in the 2nd a maraka place occupying a
> shashtashtaka position from the major lord."

## lincoln
> "As soon as Saturn Dasa Mercury Bhukti started, Lincoln was assassinated.
> … Saturn … by virtue of his position in the 10th, has caused Mrityu Yoga.
> From Chandra Lagna, he owns the 2nd, a maraka place. Saturn is further
> aspected by Jupiter, lord of the 2nd, a maraka place. … Mercury is in the
> 2nd from the Moon with the 8th lord. He is the 8th lord from Lagna and
> Navamsa Lagna."

## tennyson
> "Tennyson died in 1892 at the ripe old age of 83 years at the fag end of
> Ketu Dasa. … Ketu is in the 12th and Mars whose influences Ketu is to
> partake owns the 7th. In the Navamsa again Mars is in the 8th both from
> Lagna and the Moon thus justifying maraka."

## marx

> birth constellation, he occupies Jupiter's constellation and Jupiter is

## victoria

## havelock_ellis
> "Havelock Ellis died in Mercury Dasa, Saturn Bhukti. … the major lord

## tagore
> "his death took place in Jupiter Dasa Jupiter Bhukti. … Ordinarily Jupiter
> as lord of Lagna could not confer maraka results, but here Jupiter

## vivekananda
> "his untimely death in the prime of life on 4-7-1902 in the sub-period of
> the Sun in Jupiter Dasa. … Jupiter is undoubtedly a maraka because he owns
> the 7th from Chandra Lagna, occupies the 2nd, being subjected to
> Papakarthari Yoga. In the Navamsa he owns the 7th from Chandra Lagna and
> is in the 12th from Lagna. The Sun is in the 2nd from Lagna with 7th lord
> Mercury." — "…did not give him good longevity."

> and occupies the 2nd from the Moon."

## ashutosh

## fdr
> "He died as soon as Saturn's sub-period commenced in Ketu Dasa. … Ketu
> presides over the 3rd star, from that held by Saturn, going under the name
> of vipat (danger). The sub-lord Saturn owns a maraka house and is
> associated with Jupiter, lord of the 8th from Lagna. … Added to all this,
> Roosevelt was having the Elarata [sadesathi] for the third time … and
> Saturn-Rahu conjunction was taking place in his Janma Rasi."

## mussolini
> "His death took place in Saturn Dasa Jupiter Bhukti. … Saturn owns the 3rd
> and 4th from Lagna and occupies the 7th, a maraka place. In the Navamsa
> again, Saturn owns the 7th and 8th houses. The sub-lord Jupiter is a
> maraka, as he owns the 2nd and occupies the 8th with Venus, lord of the
> 7th. The major and sub-lords are in dwirdwadasa and the former in
> association with Mars. Hence Mussolini had a violent end."

> 8th. In the Navamsa again, Mercury owns the 7th from Lagna and is aspected
> by Mars, lord of the 2nd (and 9th) and Venus (lord of the 3rd and 8th)."

> occupies the 3rd from Lagna and the 7th from Chandra Lagna in the Navamsa.
> These various ownerships and dispositions have conferred on the Sun and
> Saturn maraka power."
