बृहत्पाराशर होरा शास्त्र
Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra
Ch. LVII · Vv. 4–9
0.94 match
बुधे लाभ-स्थाने स्थिते
वाणिज्य-लाभं ददाति ॥
budhe lābha-sthāne sthite
vāṇijya-lābhaṁ dadāti
When Mercury is placed in the eleventh,
gain through commerce is given.
BPHS LVII · 6
CONTEXT
The passage forms part of the systematic exposition of planetary placement effects across the twelve bhavas. Mercury in the 11th (lābha-sthāna) is uniformly auspicious, with intensity modulated by the sign tenanted and the lagnesha's strength.

लग्नेशो लाभ-स्थाने यदि
वित्त-वाक्-लाभं ददाति सः ॥
lagneśo lābha-sthāne yadi
vitta-vāk-lābhaṁ dadāti saḥ
When the Lord of Ascendant occupies the Eleventh,
he confers gain through wealth and through speech.
Phaladeepika XI · 18
CONTEXT
Particularly relevant when Mercury, as a benefic and natural significator of speech and commerce, is also the lagnesha — as in Virgo or Gemini ascendants. The conjunction of bhāvesha-strength and 11th-house placement intensifies gain.

karke sthite budhe candra-

Mercury in Cancer (lord: Moon)
placed in the gains-house...
Saravali XXIX · 21
CONTEXT
Specific to Mercury in Cancer in the 11th. Cancer modulates the normally cold-rational Mercury with emotional intelligence; the 11th-house position aligns this with social-network-mediated gain. Saravali pairs this with a remedial recommendation for Moon-Mercury periods.

Mercury in 11th, aspected by benefics,
gives prosperity.
Hora Sara XII · 7
CONTEXT
The benefic-aspect requirement (śubha-dṛṣṭa) is critical here: without Jupiter or Venus drishti on Mercury in the 11th, the promised prosperity remains potential. The drishti requirement is uniquely emphasised by Hora Sara compared to BPHS.
