Per the chapter, the same four factors used for the 1st house govern the 2nd — *"(a) The House, (b) Its Lord, (c) Its Occupants and (d) The Karaka or Indicator"* (HTJAH-I:2318), and *"These considerations hold good in the Navamsha also"* (HTJAH-I:2320).

**MARAKA NOTE — load-bearing:** The 2nd is a death-dealing house. *"When the 7th lord has Maraka power, then death of the native or his wife may happen in this Dasha"* (HTJAH-I:2664); extreme poverty/death rules invoke a *"Maraka planet"* explicitly (HTJAH-I:2526–2529). The engine must carry a **maraka flag** on the 2nd-house lord/occupants and let it interact with longevity, not just wealth.

## Main Considerations — Raman's opening guidance (cited)

- Judge by the four factors (House / Lord / Occupants / Karaka), and the **yogas — good and bad — formed by the 2nd lord or in the 2nd house produce their own effects** (HTJAH-I:2319).
- **All considerations hold equally in the Navamsa** (HTJAH-I:2320); the body text repeatedly re-judges the 2nd lord's D9 position before concluding (HTJAH-I:2638–2639, :2704).
- A **constellation that is not the 3rd, 5th or 7th from Janma Nakshatra** for the 2nd lord is *"highly desirable, especially if he is otherwise fortified"* (HTJAH-I:2335–2338).
- **Exaltation/own-disposition of the 2nd lord in Lagna, 4th, 5th, 7th, 9th or 10th** fortifies him *"to the extent of making him a Yoga Karaka"* (HTJAH-I:2339–2340).
- Affliction to the 2nd lord expresses as loss of nutritious food, family/children's diseases, or want of cordial relations with the life-partner (HTJAH-I:2343–2345).
- Apply **Satyacharya's YUKTA VICHARYA** — *"the matter should be looked into cleverly,"* balancing every source of strength and weakness before pronouncing (HTJAH-I:2427–2429).

### Sub-block — "A New Method of Examining Financial Prospects" (HTJAH-I:3229–3318)

Raman's **Dhana Lagna (Special Chandra Lagna)** technique, culled from an ancient work:

1. **Kala (root) numbers** of the seven planets (HTJAH-I:3247–3255):
   Sun 30 · Moon 16 · Mars 6 · Mercury 8 · Jupiter 10 · Venus 12 · Saturn 1.
2. **Procedure** (HTJAH-I:3257–3261): take the **lord of the 9th from Lagna** and the **lord of the 9th from the Moon**; add their root numbers; **divide by 12**; the **remainder counted from Chandra Lagna** (radical Moon) = the **Special Dhana Lagna**.
3. **Reading the Dhana Lagna** (HTJAH-I:3262–3272):
   - Several benefics on it → **immense wealth**.
   - One benefic, un-aspected → **highly rich**.
   - Pure malefic (Sun/Saturn/Mars) on it → **moderate wealth**; if that malefic is **exalted** → moderate early, considerable later.
   - One benefic **and** one malefic → still commands a lot of wealth. *"The same rule applies in the case of aspects."*
   - Planets **on / aspecting / in angles or trines from** Dhana Lagna give wealth in their Dasha/Bhukti; planets in **3, 6, 8, 12 from it destroy wealth** and give losses.

## Timing of Fructification — governing factors + dasha rule

**Six governing factors** for timing 2nd-house events (HTJAH-I:2582–2586):
(a) lord of the 2nd · (b) planet(s) aspecting the 2nd · (c) planet(s) in the 2nd · (d) planet(s) aspecting the 2nd lord · (e) associate(s) of the 2nd lord · (f) **lord of the 2nd from the Moon**.

**Two-level MD×AD activation rule** (HTJAH-I:2588–2599) — verbatim structure. Note Raman's **two distinct strength-classes**: a planet *"capable of influencing"* the 2nd (the **stronger** class — i.e. by **lordship** of the 2nd) ranks above a planet merely *"associated with"* the 2nd (the **weaker** class — by occupation/aspect):
- **AD-lord *and* MD-lord both *capable of influencing* the 2nd** (lordship class) → results of the 2nd house **"par excellence"** (HTJAH-I:2589–2591).
- **AD *associated with* the 2nd, but MD-lord *not* associated** → results **"to a limited extent"** (HTJAH-I:2592–2597).
- **AD *not* associated, but MD-lord *associated*** → results **"only to a feeble extent"** (HTJAH-I:2597–2599).

So both the *capable-of-influencing* (lordship) tier and the *associated-with* (occupation/aspect) tier are tracked separately; the par-excellence window requires the **stronger lordship class on both levels**. Any of the six factors fructifies the matter as **either Dasa or Bhukti lord** (HTJAH-I:2588, :2607–2608).

**Drekkana / important-years overlay** (HTJAH-I:2960–3227): for each of the 12 Lagnas × 3 drekkanas, Raman lists temperament and **specific important financial years** (ages). Treat as a per-Lagna, per-drekkana lookup keyed to the **rising sign and its decanate**, applied *only with* the chart's other combinations (*"cannot be applied blindly,"* HTJAH-I:2962). The full per-decanate table is transcribed in its own section below.

**Fortified vs afflicted (2nd-lord Dasha):** well-fortified 2nd lord in the 2nd → famous & wealthy, but **no acquisition if Lagna-lord weak** (HTJAH-I:2637–2638).

**Navamsa overlays (decisive):** *"there will be fame but no wealth"* if the 2nd lord is **in the 6th, 8th or 12th in Navamsha** (HTJAH-I:2638–2639); the "with-each-house-lord" rules (#48–#58 above) each carry a Navamsa reversal clause. **Maraka interaction:** in #53, *"when the 7th lord has Maraka power, then death of the native or his wife may happen in this Dasha"* (HTJAH-I:2664) — the 2nd-lord Dasha can deliver death, not merely wealth, when maraka-power is present.
