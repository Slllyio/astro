## 0. Short answer

**In Raman's general method, the Navamsa is one input to the grade of each factor (house, lord, karaka). He weighs it together with the Rasi. It is not a separate chart that overrules the Rasi.** In his worked charts it almost always does one of three things. It raises or lowers the strength of a factor ("fairly good", "middling"). It confirms a Rasi indication ("even in the Navamsa, the 5th house is spoiled"). Or it adds a sub-matter or quality: how many wives, a temperament, the type of marriage, physical features.

In two places Raman lets the Navamsa reverse a Rasi indication:

1. **Yogas and their cancellations (3HC).** Exalted planets in evil Navamsas stop a Raja Yoga from working (Yoga 248). A Rasi-exalted Saturn in a debilitated Navamsa makes a Raja Yoga "infructuous" (Yoga 298, Chart 143, the only worked chart of its kind). A benefic Navamsa cancels a bad yoga (Yogas 158 and 244).
2. **Dasha results of "lord X with lord Y in house Y" (HTJAH Vol I).** When the Navamsa relation of the two lords is 6/8/12, he predicts "quite opposite", "contrary", or "none of the above benefits". One case also runs the other way: bad becomes "favourable results may be anticipated". Vol II gives the parallel rules for the 7th–9th lords, mostly as changes of **degree** ("results will be good but their degree will be only middling").

He also writes explicit limits that put the Rasi first: "The moderate affliction ... in Navamsha is more than counterbalanced by his strong disposition in Rashi" (HTJAH 4688–4689). "Navamsha cannot deny elder co-borns" (HTJAH 16704). A Navamsa Hamsa Yoga "could not save the native from suffering incarceration" (NH 12983–12986). Three planets are Vargottama, yet "the significance of all this is lost by the 10th lord Saturn being placed in the 8th" (NH 5879–5881).

## 1. General statements: what role does Raman give the Navamsa?

### 1.1 Equal partner, weighed together ("the pivot")

- HTJAH 469–471 (Vol I, "Considerations in Judging a House"): "In examining a house due importance should be given not only to the Rashi but also to the Navamsha and other appropriate diagrams." Factor 6 of his 8-factor list (486–487) is: "The situation—favourable or adverse—of the house-lord or the lord in whose house the particular house- lord is situated, in the Navamsha." He closes the list (495): "All these must be properly weighed before any result can be deduced."
- HTJAH 974–980 (Vol I, "Concerning the First House"): "The Navamsha is also equally important. The effects of the Navamsha during which a person happens to he born are supposed to influence all his activities throughout life, subject to modifications according to the aspects of planets situated at different places with reference to the Navamsha. It is the pivot on which the horoscope revolves. Consequently Hindu Astrology attaches the greatest importance to the Navamsha chart. Every combination mentioned should be applied to the Rashi, Bhava and Navamsha charts and then a conclusion drawn."
- HTJAH 990–991: after (a) the position, (b) the aspects and associations and (c) the yogas of the house lord: "This holds good in the Navamsha also."
- HTJAH 1148–1149: "In analysing Lagna Bhava, due attention must be paid to the dispositions of Chandra Lagna and Navamsha Lagna also."
- HTJAH 2319–2320 (2nd house): "These considerations hold good in the Navamsha also."
- HTJAH 2703–2705 (2nd-lord Dasha results): "one should carefully consider the position of the 2nd lord and the planet in question, not only in Rashi but in the Navamsha also. There should be a balanced weighing of the favourable influences."
- HTJAH 1780–1782 (Lagna lord with the 11th lord, adverse in Navamsa): "The exact nature of the strength and weakness of the two lords must be cleverly balanced before coming to a decision."
- HPA ch.11 (`chapter_011_the-shadvargas.md`) 28–33: "most of the astrologers mark the Rasi and the Navamsa diagrams and base their predictions on these two. Navamsa system is the most important division." Lines 77–78: "The most important sub-division of a zodiacal sign is the navamsa."
- AfB 1129–1132: the Navamsa diagram "plays a unique part in Hindu Astrology. It has a great bearing on the interpretation of horoscopes." AfB 1148–1151: "In Hindu Astrology constant reference is made to Navamsa positions of planets. In a way Navamsa is much more important than the usual Rasi or zodiacal diagram." This is his strongest wording for the Navamsa. It is hedged ("In a way") and sits in a construction chapter, not a judgement chapter.
- Manual of Hindu Astrology (1935), ch.12 ~918–921: "As navamsa combinations are too often referred to in Hindu predictive astrology, it would be better to mark the Grahas in a navamsa diagram".
- GBB: the Navamsa enters Shadbala only as a numeric component, as one of the Saptavarga (`chapter_003_sthana-bala.md` ~475–548, Saptavargaja and Oja-yugma-rasyamsa bala). It gets no override role there.

**Reading:** "Equally important", "pivot", "applied to Rashi, Bhava and Navamsha ... and then a conclusion drawn", "balanced weighing". This describes **integration**: the Navamsa is weighed alongside the Rasi, and neither overrules the other.

### 1.2 Vargottama

- NH 2699–2702 (No. 7, Nero): "Vargottama is a highly beneficial combination — in fact more beneficial than exaltation and when Vargottama occurs for exalted planets, the resultant would, of course, be powerful Rajayogas".
- HTJAH 1859–1860 (Chart 14): "Because Lagna is the same both in Rashi and Navamsha, it goes under the name of Vargottamamsa. Hence the Lagna is very powerful."
- HTJAH 1119–1120: "He will be happy all his life if lord of Lagna is in Vargottama, in exaltation, in a friendly Drekkana, and joined or aspected by good planets."
- HTJAH 5376–5377 (5th–10th lord Raja Yoga in Dasha): "When the lords have attained Vargottamamsa, the position acquired will be that of a king or head of a State." Vargottama raises the **degree** of the result.
- NH 15426–15428 (quoting Brihat Jataka XXII.4): "A person born when the rising Navamsa is on Vargottama or when the Moon occupies a Vargottama position will be happy and prosperous."
- HPA ch.20 (`chapter_020_special-yogas.md`) 246–249: "When Jupiter is powerful, well aspected and occupies a Vargottama ... the person becomes a great lecturer and orator."
- **Limit:** NH 5879–5881 (No. 22, Marie Antoinette): "Three planets are Vargottama. The significance of all this is lost by the 10th lord Saturn being placed in the 8th powerfully aspected by Mars lord of the 6th." A Rasi affliction voids the benefit of Vargottama.
- **Limit:** HTJAH 3779–3787 (Chart 59, brothers): Venus as 3rd lord "is Vargottama in the chart ... This is good. But in the Rashi he is with an inimical planet Sun and has become combust. This makes him weak". Conclusion: "the fact of the ruler of the third becoming combust and hence powerless, renders the third house weak. This stands against his having any brothers." Vargottama does not rescue a combust lord.

### 1.3 Exalted in Rasi, debilitated in Navamsa, and the reverse

Raman nowhere states a general rule ("a planet exalted in Rasi but debilitated in Navamsa gives X"). His applications are:

- **Rasi exalted, Navamsa debilitated:**
  - **3HC Yoga 298 (Raja Yoga Bhanga).** 3HC 10246–10248 gives the definition: "The ascendant being Leo, Saturn must be in exaltation occupying a debilitated Navamsa or aspected by benefics" [OCR; the sense is malefics]. The result is "bereft of fortune and social position" (10250–10251). In Chart 143 (10270–10274) Raman applies it: "Saturn is exalted in Rasi, debilitated in Navamsa and aspected by Mars. The Raja Yoga formed in the 10th house has been rendered infructuous." **Reversal.**
  - **NH No. 28, Ramakrishna (6907–6914).** "Venus is not subject to any malefic combinations or aspects both in Rasi and Navamsa. He is of course debilitated in Navamsa." Raman then reads Venus as pure ("complete absence of passions"). The Navamsa debility is **noted and does not change the verdict**.
- **Rasi debilitated, Navamsa exalted:**
  - **NH No. 54, M. N. Tantri (11469–11475).** "Lord of the 10th Mars is neecha in Lagna, uchcha in Navamsa, but afflicted ... If at all there was any financial prosperity, it must have been only during Mars Dasa. As yogakaraka and feebly strong, Mars will have contributed his humble mite." **Degree only:** the Navamsa exaltation lifts Mars from bad to "feebly strong".
  - **HTJAH 3846–3854 (Chart 62).** "The only relieving feature is Mars exalted in Navamsha, here again aspected by the Sun. These are decidedly adverse indications." Conclusion: "the subject has no brothers." The Navamsa exaltation **does not rescue** the matter.
  - **HTJAH 3806–3816 (Chart 60).** The 3rd lord is "exalted in Navamsha. This is good. But in Bhava there is Jupiter-Ketu association ... afflicted by the powerful aspect of Mars in Navamsha." Conclusion: "The native has no brothers." **Does not rescue.**
  - **HTJAH 5814–5822 (Chart 100, children).** "In the Navamsha Jupiter is no doubt exalted but his association with the Sun and his being aspected by Mars are not good features." The karaka is "powerless": a son died and two abortions followed.
- **Navamsa debility inside an otherwise strong yoga:**
  - **NH No. 75, Farouk (15201–15203).** "Hamsa Yoga has lost some of its strength by virtue of Jupiter being Neecha in Navamsa." **Degree only:** the yoga still works, with less strength.
  - **NH No. 41, Gandhi (9134–9139).** "Jupiter ... is neecha in Navamsa and has not obtained neechabhanga. This accounts for his tirade against the orthodox Hindus". The Navamsa colours the **quality** of the result. It does not reverse Jupiter's spiritual promise.
  - **NH No. 63, Meher Baba (13124–13128).** "Jupiter in the Navamsa no doubt gave him an element of Gnana but as Jupiter is neecha and neechabhanga is caused by a malefic planet Saturn, Meher Baba could not have been an infallible authority." **Quality.**
- **Treated as equal to a Rasi debility:** 3HC 7985–7987 (Yoga 240, remark): "The same result may also be anticipated in case Lagna lord is debilitated in the Rasi or in the Navamsa."

### 1.4 "The Navamsa is the fruit"

**Not found.** No sentence in the Raman texts calls the Navamsa the "fruit" of the Rasi. I searched every Raman book in the folder for "fruit", "tree" and "seed" near "Navamsa" and found nothing. The doctrine should not be attributed to Raman from this corpus.

### 1.5 The Navamsa of the lord, and the Navamsa dispositor

- HTJAH 486–487 (factor 6, quoted in 1.1): Raman judges both the house lord's Navamsa situation and that of its sign-dispositor.
- Many 3HC yogas are built on the Navamsa dispositor ("lord of the Navamsa occupied by ..."): Gauri, Parijatha, Vishnu, and the yogas at 2583, 2636, 2830, 3309, 3495, 3730, 5247, 6222, 6437, 6538, 8743. These are **separate yoga conditions**, not overrides of the Rasi.
- HTJAH 1787–1789: Lagna lord with the 12th lord in the 12th (loss and exile): "If, however, Lagnadhipati is in his own Navamsha, he will slightly benefit in foreign countries ... When the lord of the Navamsha sign held by Lagnadhipati is exalted in a Chara Rashi, there will be considerable amassing of fortune." This is a Navamsa-based rescue from a bad Rasi conjunction. The first clause is "slightly"; the second is "considerable".

### 1.6 Navamsa and marriage (the 7th)

- HTJAH 8392–8397 (Vol II, timing marriage): "The lord of the sign occupied by the 7th lord or that of the sign occupied by the 7th lord in Navamsha are capable of giving marriage in their Dasha ... The 2nd lord or the ruler of the sign occupied by the 2nd lord in Navamsha is also capable". Lines 8484–8486: "the periods of the planets related to the 7th house in Rashi or Navamsha ... are capable of giving marriage." The Navamsa is used for **timing**.
- HTJAH 8080–8087 and 8134–8151 (Vol II, classical rules): a malefic Navamsa for the 7th lord makes the wife "wicked". Common-sign Navamsas give plural marriages. These are **additive conditions**.
- HPA ch.16 (`chapter_016_judgment-of-a-horoscope.md`) 123–131: "When Venus occupies the house of Jupiter or that of the lord of the 1st or 9th either in the rasi or navamsa, the native will have a chaste, loving and happy wife." Rasi "or" Navamsa: either chart can satisfy the condition.
- HTJAH 8322–8324 (Vol II; HTJAH2 746): the 7th lord with the 2nd lord in the 2nd, afflicted, may cause the death of the native or his wife. "If the 7th lord is in the 8th from Navamsha Lagna, the native's wife may not die but he may instead marry a second wife in some distant place." The Navamsa **changes which event occurs** (death becomes remarriage).
- Raman does **not** single out the "7th of the Navamsa" as the decider for marriage. In NH and in the Vol II 7th-house charts it always appears as one more corroborating item (section 2.4).

### 1.7 The dasha "lord-in-house-with-lord" rules: where the Navamsa reverses in doctrine

These are Raman's statements of results in the periods of the Lagna lord, 2nd, 4th, 5th and 6th lords (Vol I), and of the 7th, 8th and 9th lords (Vol II). In each, a 6/8/12 Navamsa relation between the two lords modifies the Rasi result.

**Reversals (Vol I):**
- HTJAH 1738–1741 (Lagna lord with the 5th lord in the 5th: ambassador, minister, birth of a son): "Quite opposite results may be anticipated if the lord of Lagna occupies the 6th, 8th or 12th from lord of the 5th in the Navamsha. There will be reverses in the political or official career".
- HTJAH 1753–1757 (Lagna lord with the 7th lord): "When the lord of Lagna is in the 6th, 8th or 12th from the 7th lord in the Navamsha, the person will be deprived of means of livelihood, defamed ... failure, loss of business".
- HTJAH 1765–1766 (with the 9th lord): "Contrary results may be anticipated if the Navamsha positions are adverse." Lines 1772–1774 (with the 10th lord): "If the dispositions are adverse in Navamsha, he will lose respect and will be a victim of slander".
- HTJAH 2644–2646 (2nd lord with the 3rd lord): "If the second lord is in the 6th, 8th or 12th from the 5th lord [sic] in Navamsha, quite the opposite results will happen."
- HTJAH 2651–2652 (2nd lord with the 4th lord): "None of the above benefits will accrue if the 2nd lord is in an unfavourable Navamsha disposition from the 4th lord."
- HTJAH 4362–4369 (4th lord with the 9th lord: sudden fortune): "In the Navamsha, if the fourth lord is in the sixth, eighth or twelfth from the ninth lord, contrary results will happen." Line 4372: with the 11th lord, "there will be losses".
- HTJAH 5344–5347 (5th lord with the 6th lord: political power): "but in the Navamsha is in the 6th, 8th or 12th from the place occupied by the 6th lord, there will be destruction of prosperity ... When the influences are mixed, the results will also be mixed." Lines 5398–5400 (5th lord with the 11th lord): "When the 5th lord is in adverse amsas contrary results may be expected." Lines 5411–5412 (5th lord with the Lagna lord): "contrary results should be expected."

**Bad becomes good, or evil is mitigated (Vol I):**
- HTJAH 1743–1750 (Lagna lord with the 6th lord in the 6th: ailments, enmity, litigation): "If, however, in the Navamsha, the 6th lord is debilitated and lord of Lagna is exalted, favourable results may be anticipated. The person may enter the Army. He will vanquish his enemies ... When, however, the lord of Lagna is in the 6th, 8th or 12th in the Navamsha from the 6th lord, then favourable results will be minimised and the unfavourable results will not predominate."
- HTJAH 4357–4359 (4th lord with the 8th lord): "The evil gets mitigated if in Navamsha, the fourth lord occupies the sixth, eighth or twelfth from the eighth lord." Similar wording at 5357–5359 (5th lord with the 8th lord) and 6342–6345 (10th lord, evil "lessened").

**Degree only (Vol I and Vol II):**
- HTJAH 1716–1718: "if he occupies 6th, 8th or 12th in the Navamsha from the sign held by the 2nd lord, there will be a lessening of the above effects."
- HTJAH 1777–1780: "When the dispositions are adverse in Navamsha, the gains will be less."
- HTJAH 2638–2640: "When the 2nd lord is in the 6th, 8th or 12th in Navamsha, there will be fame but no wealth." This one is a **sub-matter split**: fame stays, wealth goes.
- HTJAH 8314–8316 (Vol II): Lagna lord in 6/8/12 from Navamsa Lagna makes travel "wearisome and profitless".
- HTJAH 8335–8338 and 8365–8366 (Vol II, 7th lord): evil is "specially so" or "greatly intensified".
- HTJAH 9576–9577, 9586–9587, 9612–9614, 9650, 9658 (Vol II, 8th lord): "the intensity of misfortune is reduced", "redueed in degree" [reduced], "greatly mitigated".
- HTJAH 12828–12831 (Vol II, 9th lord with the 3rd lord): "If, however, the 9th lord occupies the 6th, 8th or 12th Navamsha, the native's fortune will be only mediocre." Lines 12821–12822: "the degree of beneficeial results will be greatly modified."
- **HTJAH 12894–12896 (Vol II, 9th lord in the 10th): "If the 9th lord in the 10th house is well placed but the 9th lord occupies the 6th, 8th or 12th Navamsha, results will be good but their degree will be only middling."** This is the clearest single sentence of the degree-only reading.

**Reading:** Vol I's wording ("quite opposite", "contrary") allows a full flip, but only for the Dasha result of a specific two-lord conjunction. Vol II's parallel rules are mostly degree ("reduced in degree", "only middling", "mediocre"). The one event change in Vol II is death becoming remarriage (1.6). Raman gives no worked chart in either volume where he applies these flips.

### 1.8 Yoga conditions where the Navamsa acts as a switch (3HC)

- 3HC 8308–8338 (Yoga 248): the yoga requires "four planets ... exalted without occupying evil navamsas or shastiamsas". Raman's remark: "If the exalted planets are in evil navamsas or shashtiamsas, the yoga does not function and the native may become a tyrant or malevolent dictator." **Good becomes nullified, even inverted.**
- 3HC 5664–5672 (Yoga 158, nervous in public assemblies): "Even when the above combination is present, if the 2nd lord is in a benefic Navamsa, the Yoga is found to lose its significance." **Bad becomes cancelled.** "Is found" signals Raman's own experience.
- 3HC 8143–8148 (Apakeerti, Yoga 244): "the mere presence of these two planets in the 10th unaspected by other malefics cannot constitute Apakeerti yoga, if benefic navamsas are held." **Bad becomes not formed.**
- 3HC 5950–5961 (Yoga 172): a debilitated or inimical Navamsa of the 2nd lord **is itself the condition** for a bad result.
- 3HC 8195–8200 (Yoga 246): "It is held by the framers of this yoga, that the Raja Yoga gets fructified by the debilitated planet occupying auspicious navamsas also." Raman **reports** this rather than endorsing it.
- 3HC 3216–3240 (Kurma Yoga): Raman is unsure whether "mitramsa" means a friendly Navamsa or a friendly sign. "Readers must accept my remarks with a certain amount of reservation."
- 3HC 1361–1366 (Kemadruma): "There are yet other authors who say that these yogas arise from kendras and navamsas but these observations are not generally acceptable." He rejects a Navamsa extension of the Kemadruma rules. The OCR context is brief, so the exact referent is slightly ambiguous.

## 2. Worked charts in which the conclusion turns on the Navamsa

Each case lists the matter, the Rasi indication, the Navamsa factor, Raman's verdict, and an outcome class:
**REV** = the Navamsa reverses the matter verdict. **DEG** = it strengthens or weakens the degree only (verdict side unchanged). **TIE** = the Rasi is mixed and the Navamsa tips it. **CONF** = it confirms only. **SUB** = it adds a sub-matter, quality, number or timing without touching the verdict. **LIM** = the Navamsa is explicitly over-ruled by the Rasi, a subclass of DEG.

## 4. Explicit precedence rules and limits

1. **No general rule says "the Navamsa overrides the Rasi"**, or the reverse, in any Raman book in the corpus. The stated method is joint weighing (HTJAH 495, 974–980, 2703–2705, 1780–1782).
2. **Explicit Rasi-over-Navamsa sentences:**
   - "The moderate affliction to which Jupiter is subjected to in Navamsha is more than counterbalanced by his strong disposition in Rashi." (HTJAH 4688–4689)
   - "[The Moon's influence on Karaka Mars in] Navamsha cannot deny elder co-borns." (HTJAH 16703–16704; HTJAH2 14996)
   - "results will be good but their degree will be only middling" (HTJAH 12894–12896): a 6/8/12 Navamsa lowers a well-placed Rasi result in degree only.
   - "Though he is exalted in the 10th in Navamsa, he could not save the native from suffering incarceration" (NH 12983–12986).
   - "Three planets are Vargottama. The significance of all this is lost by the 10th lord Saturn being placed in the 8th" (NH 5879–5881).
3. **Explicit statements that let the Navamsa reverse:**
   - Dasha two-lord rules in Vol I: "Quite opposite results may be anticipated if the lord of Lagna occupies the 6th, 8th or 12th from lord of the 5th in the Navamsha" (HTJAH 1739–1740), and the parallel lines in 1.7.
   - 3HC Yoga 248: "If the exalted planets are in evil navamsas or shashtiamsas, the yoga does not function" (3HC 8335–8338).
   - 3HC Yoga 158: "if the 2nd lord is in a benefic Navamsa, the Yoga is found to lose its significance" (3HC 5671–5672).
   - 3HC Chart 143: "The Raja Yoga formed in the 10th house has been rendered infructuous" (3HC 10272–10274).
4. **Raman's own brake on single-factor cancellation** (same 3HC passage, Yoga 299): "I am afraid, in the absence of other powerful arishtas, the mere debilitation of the Sun cannot destroy all the Raja Yogas" (3HC 10265–10268). He applies the same reasoning in his Rajayoga-bhanga remarks: "When Raja Yogas are really more powerful than Rajabhanga Yogas, then alone the former could function" (3HC 10227–10229). Cancellation is a contest of strengths, not an automatic switch.

## 5. Implications for a Raman-fidelity engine (for the caller; not doctrine)

- **The faithful default:** treat the Navamsa as a **modifier of each factor's grade** (house, lord, karaka), weighed alongside the Rasi. It may move a factor about one step, for example bad to "fairly good" (D13) or good to "moderately strong" (D17). It should **not** by itself invert a house verdict that the Rasi gives clearly.
- **Explicit Rasi-wins guard:** a strong Rasi disposition outweighs a moderate Navamsa affliction (D1). Navamsa exaltation or Vargottama does not rescue a combust or debilitated-and-afflicted Rasi lord or karaka (D5–D8, D4).
- **Where reversal is doctrinal and citable:** (a) the 3HC yoga clauses that name a Navamsa condition (248, 298, 158, 244), each within its own yoga; (b) Dasha-period results for the specific two-lord conjunction rules of HTJAH Vol I, which are timing and period outputs, not natal house verdicts.
- **As a tie-breaker:** there is no printed rule. The worked evidence is thin and two-sided (T1–T2 for, C1–C4 against). Encoding a Navamsa tie-break would be an inference, not Raman's stated method. If used at all, it should be named as such and gated.
- **Sub-matters** (number of spouses or children, spouse's nature, temperament, physical features, timing) are where Raman most often draws something from the Navamsa that the Rasi does not show.
