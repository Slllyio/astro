## What changed vs v1 (and vs Finding 13's six "families")

1. **Verse-level provenance** — every rule now carries text+chapter+verse, or an explicit flag that it has none.
2. **Two streams disentangled**: BPHS times events through *conditional benefic/maraka antardashas + the AD-from-MD-lord position*; the *house-lord-dasha* dictums (7L→marriage etc.) are the later Phaladeepika/JP stream. These are different, separately testable hypotheses — Finding 13's families blended them.
3. **The folklore list** (test nothing here as scripture): Mangal-dosha verse, marana-karaka-sthana, graha maturity ages, Sade-sati-as-death-timer, dasha-chhidra doctrine, "stronger of the two" slogan, 64th-navamsa-from-Lagna variant, Ashtakavarga marriage timing, Rao's double transit.
4. **Newly encodable, previously missed, high-value rules**: PD 20.29 (AD delivers the bhāva of its house **from the MD lord** — we never tested the FROM-MD-LORD frame); BPHS 44.8 (benefic-bhukti immunity); BPHS 47.3–4 (drekkana phase within MD — we have longitudes now); JP 14.29 (planet **conjunct** 7L); Saravali 46.27 (widowhood in D9-dispositor-of-8L dasha); BPHS 48.16–17½ (1↔10 exchange dasha rule); BPHS 18.22–34 + 16.18–23 (fixed-age tables → direct age-regression tests).
