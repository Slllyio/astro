# House 10 — Karma / Rajya Bhava

> Faithful extraction of B.V. Raman's *How to Judge a Horoscope* (Vol. II), the
> "Concerning the Tenth House" chapter. Every rule is cited as `HTJAH-II:<line>`.
> Quotations are kept to ≤1 sentence. Nothing here is invented; gaps are marked.

Raman opens by stressing that determining **occupation/profession is the most
difficult and most important** part of horoscope analysis, because modern
avocations are "so vast and varied" that exact profession can rarely be fixed
with certainty (`HTJAH-II:9429-9433`). Astrology gives **natural aptitudes and
leanings**, not deterministic vocation labels (`HTJAH-II:9579-9583`).

## Three Pillars — House / Lord / Karaka / Secondary

Before analysing the 10th Bhava, study the general strength of: **(a) the house,
(b) its lord, (c) its occupants, (d) the Karaka** — plus relevant yogas and the
**Navamsa chart** (`HTJAH-II:9446-9452`).

### The FOUR karma-karakas (multi-karaka house)

This is a **multi-karaka house**. Raman invokes four natural significators, each
governing a different *matter* of the 10th, and reads the **kind** of livelihood
from whichever dominates:

| Karaka | Matter it governs for the 10th | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| **Sun** | Authority, rulers, dignitaries, government, kingship; political planet | HTJAH-II:9620-9622, 9928-9930, 13491-13493 |
| **Mercury** | Intelligence (buddhi), trade/commerce, creative & fictional writing, documentation | HTJAH-II:9589-9593, 9671-9674, 9685-9690 |
| **Jupiter** | Intellectual/learned avocations, religious knowledge, counsel, law | HTJAH-II:9671-9672, 9860-9861, 10261-10262 |
| **Saturn** | Hard-working / humble professions, labour, masses, industry | HTJAH-II:9673-9674, 10267-10274, 12132-12135 |

Note Raman's **deviation from classical doctrine on the mind**: classical
astrology assigns mental outlook to the Moon, but "as the planet Mercury rules
buddhi or intelligence, his auspicious disposition is equally important"
(`HTJAH-II:9589-9593`). So before judging profession, assess **Sun (will),
Moon (mind), Lagna (body), and Mercury (intellect)** (`HTJAH-II:9585-9593`).

### Reference frames (secondary)

The 10th is reckoned **not only from Lagna but also from the Moon and the Sun**;
the reckoning from the **strongest of these three centres** gives best results
(`HTJAH-II:9747-9751`). Source-of-income (2nd), luck/fame/sudden-gain (9th), and
gains/trade (11th) houses are auxiliary to the professional judgement
(`HTJAH-II:10226-10236`).

### Three determinant cases (how "kind of profession" is derived)

A core procedural rule (`HTJAH-II:9718-9746`):

1. **No planet in the 10th** → take the **10th lord** as primary determinant and
   the **lord of the Navamsa occupied by the 10th lord** (i.e. the navamsa of the
   10th lord) as secondary; the **stronger of the two** indicates the profession
   (`HTJAH-II:9729-9733`). [If the 10th is *vacant*, the lord of the navamsa
   occupied by the 10th lord gives the clue — `HTJAH-II:10243-10247`.]
2. **One planet in the 10th** → among {10th lord, the occupant, navamsa-lord of
   the 10th lord}, the **strongest is the primary determinant**
   (`HTJAH-II:9735-9738`).
3. **More than one planet in the 10th** → the **most powerful** is prime
   determinant *if there is no conjunction*; if conjunct, use the **stronger of
   the navamsa-lords** of the two; if of equal strength, **blend** (more than one
   occupation) (`HTJAH-II:9740-9746`).

Additional tie-breaker: the planet whose **shadvargas predominate** in regard to
the 10th lord/house also decides the vocational determinant (a planet in
**bhava-sandhi** is incapable of giving 10th-house results)
(`HTJAH-II:9797-9811`).

## Timing of Fructification — cited

**Governing factors** for timing 10th-house events (`HTJAH-II:9913-9920`):
(a) lord of 10th, (b) planet(s) aspecting 10th, (c) planet(s) in 10th,
(d) planet(s) aspecting the 10th lord, (e) planet(s) associated with 10th lord,
(f) the **10th lord from the Moon**.

**Two-level activation rule** (Dasa × Bhukti) — explicitly graded
(`HTJAH-II:9922-9933`):

1. Sub-period of a 10th-connected planet **in** the major period of a
   10th-connected planet → **results par excellence**.
2. Sub-period of a 10th-associated planet in a major period of an *unrelated*
   planet → 10th results **only to a limited extent**.
3. Sub-period of an unrelated planet in a major period of a 10th-associated
   planet → 10th results **only to a small extent**.

So: **both Dasa-lord and Bhukti-lord must touch the 10th** for full
fructification; one-sided linkage gives partial results.

**Kingdom-accession timing** (Varahamihira, *Brihat Jataka*): obtained in the
Dasa/Bhukti of (1) planet in Lagna, (2) planet in 10th, or (3) strongest planet;
**lost** in period of (1) planet in inimical sign, or (2) planet in 7th from the
sign it rules (`HTJAH-II:13313-13322`).

### Navamsa / D10 overlays

- Navamsa is mandatory in the primary analysis (`HTJAH-II:9452`).
- **6/8/12 from Navamsa Lagna** repeatedly downgrades results (routine/mediocre
  career, reduced rank) (`HTJAH-II:9505-9506, 9551-9552, 10052-10054,
  10082-10083, 10113-10115, 10177-10179`).
- **Vargottama** (same sign Rasi & Navamsa) strongly fortifies the 10th lord /
  determinant (`HTJAH-II:9778-9779`, chart 115).
- The **navamsa-lord of the 10th lord** is the secondary/clue determinant when
  the 10th is vacant (`HTJAH-II:9729-9733, 10243-10247`).
- The special career varga is the **D10 Dasamsa** (project convention); Raman's
  text leans on D9 navamsa but the D10 is the dedicated career divisional.

## Engine Notes (for `house_10.py`)

- **Three functions of a planet (underpins conjunction yoga-transfer).** Raman:
  a planet has **3 main functions** — (a) due to **ownership** (lordship), (b) due
  to **karakatwa** (natural significatorship), (c) due to **generation of yogas** —
  plus **subsidiary functions** arising "by virtue of association, aspect, etc."
  (`HTJAH-II:13770-13778`). When two planets conjoin, each contributes *all three*
  innate functions PLUS the subsidiary function of the combination; e.g. a Sun-Saturn
  conjunction blends Sun's karakatwa + the combination's main function + Saturn's
  karakatwa. **Engine impact:** model conjunction as a *transfer/blend* of all four
  function-classes, not just a sign-membership tag — this is why a benefic-aspected
  10th planet lends *capacity* (the aspecting planet's karakatwa) without owning the
  vocation (rule #3a, `HTJAH-II:9616-9625`).
- **Multi-karaka house (4 karakas).** Unlike single-karaka houses, the 10th has
  **Sun, Mercury, Jupiter, Saturn**. Implement a per-karaka strength assessment;
  do NOT collapse to one. Route the *matter* per karaka: Sun→authority/govt,
  Mercury→intellect/trade/writing, Jupiter→learned/religious/law,
  Saturn→labour/masses/industry (`HTJAH-II:9671-9674, 10927-10963`).
- **"Kind of profession" derivation pipeline** — implement the three-case
  determinant logic exactly (`HTJAH-II:9718-9746`):
  1. Collect candidates: {10th lord, 10th occupant(s), navamsa-lord of 10th lord}.

  3. If 1 occupant → strongest of the three.

     navamsa-lord; if equal → blend (multiple occupations).
