## A. The Maraka doctrine — BPHS Ch. 44 (death timing)

1. **Maraka sthanas defined.** "O Brahmin! The 3rd and the 8th Bhava are the two Bhavas of longevity. The Bhavas related to death are the 12th from each of these, i.e. Dhana (2nd) and Yuvati (7th) Bhava are Maraka Bhavas." — **BPHS 44.1–2**. ⇒ `maraka_houses = {2,7}`.
2. **2nd outranks 7th.** "Out of the two Maraka Bhavas, Dhana Bhava is a powerful Maraka Bhava." — **BPHS 44.2**; echoed Laghu Parashari sutra 24. ⇒ weight(2) > weight(7).
3. **Primary maraka set.** Lords of 2nd & 7th; malefics occupying 2nd/7th; malefics conjoined with those lords — their MD/AD bring death "depending on whether he is a short-, medium-, or long-life person." — **BPHS 44.3–5**. ⇒ death window = MD/AD of set members, gated by ayus band.
4. **Priority ladder (occupant-of-2nd > lord-of-2nd > occupant-of-7th > lord-of-7th > conjunct)** — ⚠ NOT a verbatim BPHS verse; Santhanam commentary + Laghu Parashari tradition. Tag rule-source = commentary.
5. **Fallback ladder when no primary maraka dasha runs** (decreasing power): 12th lord's dasha → dasha of benefic related to 12th lord → 8th lord's dasha → dasha of a pure malefic. — **BPHS 44.6–7**; parallel Laghu Parashari 25–26.
6. **Saturn prime killer.** "If Saturn is ill-disposed and be related to a Maraka Graha, he will be the first to kill in preference to other Grahas." — **BPHS 44.9**.
7. **Jupiter/Venus as kendra-lords become powerful marakas** if owning/occupying maraka sthana — ⚠ source is **Laghu Parashari** (~sutras 13/23 region), not BPHS Ch.44.
8. **Venus–Saturn exchange.** In Venus MD · Saturn AD, Saturn gives Venus's results, and vice versa — **Laghu Parashari** (~sutra 33–34). Makes Ven/Sat & Sat/Ven ADs maraka-critical when either is a maraka lord.
9. **Rahu/Ketu acquire killing power** if in Lagna/7/8/12, or in 7th from a maraka lord, or conjoined with one — kill in MD/AD. — **BPHS 44.16–17** (mirror text; "in 2nd or 7th" variant is edition-dependent).
10. **Maraka dasha when longevity band not exhausted → disease and misery, not death.** "Should there be many marakas and if these marakas are strong, there will be diseases and miseries in major and sub-periods." — BPHS Ch.44 latter verses / commentary.
11. **6th-lord dasha kills; ADs of 6/8/12 lords.** "Death may come to pass in the Dasha of the 6th lord and in the sub-periods of the 6th, 8th and/or 12th lords." — BPHS Ch.44 text/commentary (⚠ single-mirror).
12. **AD gate inside a maraka MD:** death in the AD of a graha related to the maraka or itself a maraka; unrelated AD lord defers danger. — BPHS Ch.44 + restated per planet in Ch.52–60.

## B. Ayurdaya — longevity bands (BPHS Ch. 43; Jataka Parijata Ch. 5)

13. **Three life-spans:** short <32, medium 32–64, long 64–100. — BPHS longevity chapter.
14. **Three pairs:** (i) Lagna lord & 8th lord, (ii) Saturn & Moon, (iii) Lagna & Hora Lagna — band from the sign-quality each pair occupies; majority vote. — **BPHS Ch. 43**.
15. **Sign-quality table:** both movable → long; fixed+dual → long; movable+fixed → medium; both dual → medium; movable+dual → short; both fixed → short. — **BPHS Ch. 43**.
16. **Tie-breakers:** Moon in Lagna/7th → Saturn–Moon pair prevails; "Should Saturn be a contributor, the class of longevity declines. If Saturn is in own Rasi or exaltation, change will not occur." — **BPHS ~43.14–19**.
17. **Year-caps by agreeing pairs:** long×3 = 120, ×2 = 108, ×1 = 91; medium 80/72/64; short 32/36/40. — **BPHS Ch. 43**.
18. **Pindayu haranas:** combustion −½ (except Venus/Saturn), inimical sign −⅓, chakrapata graded, malefic-in-Lagna reduction. — BPHS Ayurdaya computation chapter (⚠ summarised via secondary).
19. **Kakshya hrasa/vriddhi** (band drop/raise) — ⚠ **Jaimini** ayurdaya tradition (Upadesa Sutras Adhyaya 2 + Vriddha-Karikas), not a BPHS verse.
20. **JP multipliers:** "The Ayurdaya of a planet is to be tripled when in exaltation or retrograde; doubled when in Vargottamamsa, own Navamsa, own house, or own Drekkana." — **Jataka Parijata 5.19** (Sastri).
21. **JP alpa/madhya/purna combinations** — JP Adhyaya 5 (124 vv.), combination section (verse numbers not triangulated).

## C. Sensitive killers — 22nd drekkana, 64th navamsa, chhidra grahas

22. **The seven chhidra grahas:** 8th lord; occupant of 8th; aspector of 8th; lord of the **22nd drekkana from Lagna**; planet conjoined with 8th lord; lord of the **64th navamsa from the Moon's navamsa**; bitter enemy (atisatru) of the 8th lord — "whichever of them is strongest, in the dasa of that planet should an astrologer predict death." — **Jataka Parijata** (~slokas 52–53 of the longevity exposition; numbering varies). ⇒ killer = argmax-strength of chhidra set.
23. **22nd drekkana (Khara).** "The cause of death … shall be determined from the 22nd Drekkana counted from the rising Drekkana; death will be caused by the lord of such Drekkana or by the lord of the sign of which the Drekkana is a division, whichever is powerful." — **Brihat Jataka Ch. 25** (Aiyar). BPHS names it Khara / lord Kharesha.
24. **64th navamsa from Moon** — killer candidate per JP chhidra list; Prasna Marga/Devakeralam add **Saturn's transit over the 64th-navamsa / 22nd-drekkana degrees** as a death trigger. ("From Lagna too" = commentarial extension.)

## D. BPHS antardasha chapters (Ch. 52–60) — per-planet maraka clauses

Template: danger fires iff the AD lord owns 2nd/7th (nodes: is joined with their lords); severity per planet:

25. **Sun AD:** "danger of premature death, or death-like sufferings" if Sun owns 2/7.
26. **Moon AD:** "lethargy and physical distress" if Moon owns 2/7 (illness-grade).
27. **Mars AD:** "diseases of the mind and body" if Mars owns 2/7.
28. **Mercury AD:** "premature death may be expected" if Mercury owns 2/7.
29. **Jupiter AD:** "physical distress, agony, death of the native, **or of any member of the family**" if Jupiter owns 2/7 (hook for death-of-relatives).
30. **Venus AD:** "physical distress" if Venus owns 2/7; "premature death if Venus is associated with the lord of the 6th or 8th."
31. **Saturn AD:** "fear of premature death" if Saturn owns 2/7.
32. **Rahu AD:** "physical distress if Rahu is associated with the lord of the 2nd or the 7th."

## E. Illness timing & other classical timers

33. **8th-lord dasha:** weak 8L → "epilepsy, poverty, fruitless wanderings, sickness, humiliation and even death"; strong 8L → "freedom from debts, personal elevation." — Phaladeepika Ch. 19 region (⚠ sloka not triangulated).
34. **Malefics in 6/8/12 from any bhava destroy it** ("…counted from the Lagna or other Bhava under consideration") — Phaladeepika bhava-judgment (~15–16). Affliction operator for derived houses.
35. **Disease signatures of 6th/8th occupants:** Sun → fever; Mars/Ketu → ulcer; Venus → disease of private parts; Jupiter → consumption. — **Phaladeepika Ch. 14**.
36. **Death agent from the 8th:** strongest planet in/aspecting the 8th, else 8th lord; malefic-owned & occupied 8th → death by weapon/fire/tiger/snake. — Phaladeepika Ch. 14.
37. **Prasna Marga Ch. 9–10:** Yogayus vs Dasayus; explicit two-stage selection of the death-dealing MD then AD from candidate list. (Chapter-level cite.)
38. **Marakas of a bhava → death of relatives:** "lords of the 2nd and 7th with respect to any Bhava are Marakas of that Bhava" — father from 9th (its 2/7 = radical 10th & 3rd), mother from 4th, spouse from 7th. — Uttara Kalamrita kanda IV–V (⚠ verse not triangulated).
39. **Dasha-sandhi / chhidra danger** at MD junctions bordering maraka or 6/8/12-lord dashas. — Phaladeepika dasha chapters; Prasna Marga (chapter-level).

## F. Popular-but-unsourced (flagged — do not test as scripture)

40. **Sade-sati as death timer** — not in jataka classics. Sourceable core: Saturn adverse transiting 12/1/2 from Moon (Phaladeepika Ch. 26 gochara; Brihat Samhita: Saturn over natal Moon → "loss of honor and wealth, and danger to life"). The 7½-year package + phases = later Samhita/folk elaboration. (Claim "BPHS formalized Sade-sati" — rejected on verification.)
41. **Crisp 5-rung maraka ladder as a Parashara verse** — commentarial (see #4).
42. **Marana-karaka-sthana** (Sun in 12th, Moon in 8th, …) — modern/Jaimini-revival teaching; no BPHS/JP/Phaladeepika verse found.
43. **"64th navamsa from Lagna" / "22nd drekkana from Moon" variants** — commentarial extensions of #22–24.
