प्रथमः Sec. I

Of the Ascendant and its Lord

Virgo rises at the eastern horizon, its tenant the cool-rayed Moon-house occupying the longitude of 173.99 degrees of ecliptic. The lord of the lagna is Budha — Mercury, the swift-footed messenger and natural significator of intellect, commerce, and the spoken word.1

Mercury hath placed himself in the eleventh bhava, the sthana of gains and aspirations, in the watery sign of Karka (Cancer). The Crab is the natural seat of the Moon — a cardinal kendra of the zodiac and the abode of nourishment. For the lord of the lagna to dwell in the eleventh from his own house is a most fortunate disposition, conferring upon the native gain through intellectual labour, the written word, and trade in matters of communication.2

लग्नेशो लाभ-स्थाने यदि
वित्त-वाक्-लाभं ददाति सः ॥
lagneśo lābha-sthāne yadi
vitta-vāk-lābhaṁ dadāti saḥ
When the Lord of the Ascendant occupies the Eleventh,
He confers gain through wealth and through speech.
Phaladeepika, Chapter XI, Verse 18

द्वितीयः Sec. II

Of the Currently Active Period

The native presently dwelleth in the Mahadasha of Budha — the great period of Mercury — which extends to the full measure of seventeen years according to the Vimshottari reckoning. Of this, some fifteen years and seven months yet remain.3

Budha Mahadasha · महादशा 15 yr 7 mo remain
Shani Antardasha · अन्तर्दशा 2 yr 1 mo remain

Within this period unfoldeth the antardasha of Shani — Saturn, the slow-moving lord of discipline and karmic recompense. Saturn, posited in his own sign of Makara (Capricorn) in the chart, bringeth to bear his full strength upon the matters of the eleventh house through the operative period.4
