## Summary of core House-1 lessons

### 1. Three-pillar integration
- Every example weighs:
  - the first house itself (Lagna/Bhava)
  - the Lagna lord
  - the lagna karaka (Sun) plus the Moon for mind-related outcomes
- When two of {Lagna, Sun, Moon} are well disposed, Raman explicitly calls that "steady fortune".
- Example 8 vs Chart 9 shows that the same movable Lagna can produce greatness or ordinariness depending on the lord and hemmed-malefic pattern.

### 2. Navamsa as a failure/mitigation layer
- Repeated examples show: a strong Rashi Lagna is downgraded by adverse Navamsa conditions.
- Chart 11 demonstrates the five-factor strength test in both Rashi and Navamsa, with Saturn dominating the 1st-house influence.
- Chart 13 and Chart 30 both use proximity/orb to decide whether a planet is effectively conjunct — a narrow-gap model is required.

### 3. Karaka-specific physical signs
- The Sun is the first-house body karaka.
- Chart 12 and Chart 13 show blended planetary physiognomy: Sun+Jupiter+Saturn produce broad head/tall/lean/fair; Mars aspect yields martial features.
- Chart 18 is a canonical health flag: the 1st-lord aspected by the 6th-lord Mars produces illness/defamation.

### 4. Papakartari is a powerful disqualifier
- Chart 26 is the strongest example: Lagna appears strong, but Papakartari plus malefic Navamsa aspects leave the native unfortified and afflicted.
- Chart 33 is a deliberate cautionary example: a six-planet 10th stellium cannot rescue an afflicted first house.

### 5. Specific timing lessons
- Chart 11 uses the five-factor timing worksheet: the planet that both rules/aspects and associates the first house is the strongest timer.
- Chart 13 and Chart 18 show illness or fever in Venus Dasha/Saturn Bhukti; the examples are concrete confirmations that Dasha+Bhukti both matter.
- Chart 36 emphasizes the Moon frame for mental abnormality: a sound first house with an afflicted Moon still yields mental trouble.

## Expert synthesis
- Raman's House 1 is a masterclass in layered reading: the body-lens is the Lagna and the Sun, while the mind-lens is the Moon. A healthy body is necessary but not sufficient for overall first-house wellbeing.
- The examples deliberately contrast similarly shaped charts to show that the timing, mental, and bodily readings can diverge. Chart 31 vs Chart 32 are a classic pair: changing only the Sun/Moon shifts build from thin to corpulent and life-station from tutor to barrister.
- The strongest lessons are not the generic 'good sign/bad sign' rules but the conditional qualifiers: Subhakartari versus Papakartari, neechabhanga versus permanent debility, and Navamsha reversal of lord-with-lord yogas.
- As an analyst, I would treat House 1 as a decision tree of three principal buckets:
  1. Strength of Lagna by Bhava, Rashi and Navamsha.
  2. Strength of the 1st lord by position, aspect, and Navamsha modifier.
  3. Strength of the Sun as body Karaka, especially in Rashi and Navamsha and in relation to malefics.
- The examples teach a second-level rule: when two of {Lagna, Sun, Moon} are strong, expect sustained fortune; when only one is strong, the outcome will be mixed and the chart will show the nature of the obstruction.
- The chapter also repeatedly teaches that cross-house power (10th stellium, 9th lord strength) can only magnify, not rescue, a physically weak or mentally weak first house.

## House 1 engine blueprint
- Score each first-house factor on a three-layer scale: Rashi/Bhava, Navamsha, and Moon-frame.
- Apply a multiplier for Papakartari/Subhakartari and for functional malefic/functional benefic status.
- Evaluate first-house health separately from first-house fame/fortune. Health uses the 1st lord + 6th lord contact rule and Moon afflictions; fame uses Lagna+Sun+Moon harmony and 10th/11th amplifiers.

## Implementation implications for House 1

1. **Use both Lagna and Moon frames** for every first-house judgment.
   - Body/appearance/constitution = Lagna + Sun.
   - Mental health and liability = Moon + Karaka/Moon afflictions.

2. **Sun must be treated as the body Karaka** and weighted with the Lagna in both Rashi and Navamsha.
   - A weak Sun can spoil an otherwise good Lagna even when the Moon is benign.
   - A strong Sun with a weak Lagna can still produce respectable physical presence if the lord and Navamsha cooperate.

3. **Navamsa mitigation is mandatory**: any strong Lagna-lord or strong lord-with-lord yoga can be reduced by adverse Navamsa placement.
   - Especially watch for the lord of Lagna in 6th/8th/12th from the relevant partner in Navamsha.
   - Treat Navamsa Lagna and Navamsa Lagna-lord as second-order first-house indicators.

4. **Hemming asymmetry matters**: Rahu in 2nd is worse than Rahu in 12th; Saturn in 12th is worse than Saturn in 2nd.
   - Use positional severity rather than simple counts.
   - Consider Hemming by Rahu/Saturn/Ketu separately from Hemming by Mars.

5. **Orb-aware conjunction rules are required** for near-misses like Chart 30 and Chart 13.
   - Declare conjunction only when the orb is effective by Raman's standards.
   - Allow "free of conjunction" when a planet is separated by about 18–20 degrees and no close aspect is implied.

6. **The five-factor timing method should be encoded as candidate influencers** in the engine.
   - A planet with two of the five functions dominates.
   - A major/minor lord both influencing the 1st yields par excellence results.

7. **Papakartari must be evaluated before scoring any positive first-house strength.**
   - Papakartari on Lagna or Lagna-lord can negate otherwise strong placements.
   - Subhakartari can elevate a weaker first house if the hemming planets are benefic.

8. **First-house health is a separate diagnostic dimension.**
   - Use the no-contact 1st-6th rule to flag illness/defamation.
   - Use Moon affliction, Sun/Moon/Karaka malefic combinations, and Sushka rules to diagnose constitution.

9. **Do not permit 10th-house strength to rescue a weak first house automatically.**
   - Chart 33 proves this: a powerful 10th stellium does not override an afflicted Lagna.
   - Respect the chapter's explicit decoupling of first-house results from 10th-house power.

10. **Validate against House 1 example clusters.**
   - Cancer charts 20/21/22 are a micro-dataset for how similar Ascendants diverge by lord disposition.
   - Leo charts 23/24/25 validate the two-of-three {Lagna, Sun, Moon} rule.
   - Chart pairs 31/32 and 8/9 are training cases for subtle Sun/Moon/hemmed differences.

## House 1 decision-rule checklist
- Score the Lagna, the Lagna lord, and the Sun independently in Rashi, Bhava, and Navamsha.
- If two of {Lagna, Sun, Moon} are strong, mark the result as "steady fortune" unless Papakartari/Papakartari overrides.
- Treat Navamsa placement of the Lagna lord as a corrective factor, not a secondary afterthought.
- Apply the 1st-6th contact rule to produce a health/defamation warning flag.
- Separate bodily constitution from mental liability: body = Lagna+Sun, mind = Moon.
- Explicitly evaluate the quality of hemmed positions:
  - Rahu in 2nd worse than Rahu in 12th
  - Saturn in 12th worse than Saturn in 2nd
  - Mars as a hemming planet is not the same as Saturn or Rahu
- Allow a debilitated planet with neechabhanga to contribute positively in the first house when other supporting factors exist.
- Treat Subhakartari as an uplift and Papakartari as a penalty on first-house fortification.

## Suggested House 1 feature model for the engine
- `lagna_strength`: composite score from Lagna Bhava, Lagna Rashi, Navamsa Lagna, and hemmed/aspected condition.
- `lagna_lord_strength`: score from lord house, sign dignity, occupations, aspects, Navamsha placement, and neechabhanga.
- `sun_karaka_strength`: score from Sun sign, Sun house, aspects, Navamsha status, and malefic contact.
- `moon_mental_strength`: score from Moon position, aspects, Navamsha, and afflicted combinations.
- `papakartari_penalty` / `subhakartari_bonus`: boolean flags that alter the first-house score.
- `first_six_contact_flag`: binary diagnostic output for health/defamation.
- `steady_fortune_flag`: true when two or more of {lagna_strength, sun_karaka_strength, moon_mental_strength} are strong.
