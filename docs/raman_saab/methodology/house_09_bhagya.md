## Three Pillars — House / Lord / Karaka / Secondary

Raman's stated judging factors `HTJAH-II:7289-7294`: "(a) The House, (b) Its Lord, (c) Its Occupants and (d) The Karaka or signifi-cator," and **"These considerations must be judged from the Navamsa chart also."**

**Dual-karaka routing rule:** Sun governs the *father* sub-matter; Jupiter (with Venus as co-benefic) governs the *fortune / dharma / religion / learning* sub-matter. Both are weighed alongside the 9th lord — never in isolation `HTJAH-II:7906-7912`.

## Main Considerations — cited

- Judge House + Lord + Occupants + Karaka, **always cross-checked in Navamsa** `HTJAH-II:7289-7294`.
- The 9th primarily = father + long-distance travel `HTJAH-II:7295-7296`.
- Combinations are "very general" and must not be applied literally; the karaka and other dispositions must be taken in `HTJAH-II:7396-7404`.
- A powerful 9th lord confers fortune even in a malefic house; a beneficially-disposed 9th house greatly reduces malefic results of an afflicted lord `HTJAH-II:7398-7402`.
- "Great skill is required in interpreting the bhava and in no case is a literal application of the results... desirable" `HTJAH-II:7402-7404`.
- Final synthesis: weigh strengths of karaka Sun, 9th lord, planets influencing it, and running Dasas/Bhuktis; conclude only after the **entire** chart is analysed `HTJAH-II:7906-7912`.

## Timing of Fructification

**Governing factors** `HTJAH-II:7608-7615`:
(a) 9th lord; (b) planets aspecting the 9th house; (c) planets occupying the 9th house; (d) planets aspecting the 9th lord; (e) planets associated with the 9th lord; (f) the 9th lord **from the Moon**; (g) the Sun (karaka for father).

**Dasha rule** (three graded tiers) `HTJAH-II:7617-7628`:
1. Sub-period of a 9th-influencing planet within the Dasa of another 9th-influencing planet → 9th-house results **par excellence**.
2. Bhukti of an associated planet in the Dasa of a non-associated planet → limited 9th-house results.
3. Bhukti of a non-associated planet in the Dasa of an associated planet → very small 9th-house results.

Primary delivery: results manifest in the **Dasa of the 9th lord**; if that Dasa does not run in the native's lifetime, planets influencing the 9th by aspect/occupation/association deliver them `HTJAH-II:7678-7687`. `Kujavad Ketu` (Ketu acts like Mars) and `Sanivad Rahu` (Rahu acts like Saturn) are explicitly used to qualify nodes as 9th-house influencers `HTJAH-II:7669, 9224`.

**Father longevity / maraka** `HTJAH-II:7916-7925`: judged from strength of 9th house + Sun; 7th & 8th **from the 9th** and other maraka houses/planets from it estimate father's span; maraka planets reckoned from karaka Sun also used; afflictions to Sun or his malefic-house occupation → early death of father; 9th lord in kendra/11th or aspecting/fortifying the 9th → father lives long.

## Engine Notes (for `house_09.py`)

- **Dual karaka, routed by sub-matter.** Sun = pitrukaraka for *all father* questions; Jupiter (with Venus as co-benefic) = karaka for *fortune / dharma / religion / higher learning / pilgrimage*. Never collapse to one karaka. `HTJAH-II:7291-7292, 7481-7482, 7916-7917`.
- **From-Sun frame for father.** Beyond from-Lagna and from-Moon, the father sub-engine must read houses **from the Sun**: positions 9/8/11/12-from-Lagna for the Sun, malefic-sign occupation by Sun, and maraka houses (2nd, 7th, 8th, 12th) counted **from the Sun** and **from the 9th** drive father longevity. `HTJAH-II:7411-7456, 7916-7925`.
- **Triple-frame requirement.** Every reading evaluates the 9th house/lord/karaka three ways: from Lagna, from Moon (Chandra Lagna), and the karaka's own condition. All worked charts (86–113) execute this triad explicitly.
- **Navamsa (D9) routing.** A 9th lord in the **6th/8th/12th from Navamsa Lagna** is the single most repeated downgrade in the chapter — it converts "fortified" Dasa-phala to "middling." Implement as a multiplier on every house-placement result. Also check 9th-lord debility/exaltation in Navamsa and Vargottama status. `HTJAH-II:7369-7372, 7463-7465, 7738-7740`.
- **Dwadasamsa (D12).** NOTE: Despite D12 being the canonical father-varga, Raman does **not** invoke D12 anywhere in this chapter — he routes father entirely through Sun + 9th + Navamsa. Do not fabricate D12 logic from this source; if house_09.py uses D12 for father it is sourced elsewhere, not HTJAH-II.
- **Nodal substitution dictums.** `Kujavad Ketu` (Ketu = Mars-like) `HTJAH-II:7669, 7995` and `Sanivad Rahu` (Rahu = Saturn-like) `HTJAH-II:9224` must be applied when deciding whether a node can act as a 9th-house influencer or maraka. A node in a planet's sign/constellation gives that planet's results.
- **Constellation (nakshatra) lord matters.** Repeatedly decisive: 9th lord or karaka in the constellation of a maraka-from-9th planet shortens father's life; in a benefic/own constellation it protects (Chart 90 vs 91; 96/97 Visakha counterfactuals). The engine must resolve nakshatra-dispositor and check its house from the 9th. `HTJAH-II:8230-8284, 8627-8638`.
- **Kartari yogas.** papakartari (malefic on both sides) afflicts; subhakartari (benefic on both sides) protects — both applied to the 9th house and to the Sun specifically (Chart 86, 89, 91, 94, 97). `HTJAH-II:7984-7986, 8409-8412`.
- **Neechabhanga.** Cancellation-of-debility for Sun (Chart 94) and Moon flips a deprivation verdict to protection; "no cancellation" (Chart 90) is fatal-early. Engine must compute neechabhanga before scoring the karaka. `HTJAH-II:8157-8158, 8394-8396`.
- **Timing tiers.** Encode the three graded Dasa/Bhukti tiers `HTJAH-II:7617-7628`; default delivery is the 9th-lord's Dasa, else any 9th-influencer. For father-death timing, score maraka strength from BOTH the 9th house and the Sun (2/7/8/12 lords therefrom, and constellation maraka-dispositors).
- **Foreign-travel sub-engine.** No classical karaka for travel `HTJAH-II:7658-7659`. Use: movable/common + watery signs on 9th; 12th-from-Lagna-lord disposition (well-placed = home prosperity, weak/inimical = abroad); reason-routing by the involved lord/planet (`HTJAH-II:7714-7754`); and optionally Paradesha Saham (= 9th house − 9th lord + Lagna lord) and Jalapathana Saham (= Cancer 15° − Saturn + Lagna), adding 30° if Lagna does not fall between the two components. `HTJAH-II:8640-8712, 9109-9365`.
  - **House-division triad for travel-distance** `HTJAH-II:8709-8712`: the **3rd** house governs short journeys; the **7th** rules foreign travel; the **12th** rules long journeys and residence in distant lands (the 9th remains the all-important long-journeys house). Route travel-distance/duration questions through this triad, not the 9th alone.
  - **12th-from-Lagna-lord prosperity nuance** `HTJAH-II:8699-8702`: when the 12th-from-Lagna-lord is weak/inimical/debilitated the native simply "goes abroad", but when that lord is strong (in a kendra/trikona/Lagna and in a friendly, own, or exaltation sign, well placed) the native goes abroad **to prosperous countries**. Grade the destination's prosperity by the strength of this lord.
- **Gotchas:**
  - Karaka-in-own-house penalty: the Sun **in the 9th** is repeatedly flagged as "not desirable / not very welcome" for father longevity even when exalted (Charts 86, 88, 94). Do not treat exalted-Sun-in-9th as purely positive. `HTJAH-II:7966-7967, 8088, 8422-8423`.
  - A debilitated planet aspecting the 9th lord (e.g. Chart 90's debilitated Sun afflicting 9th lord Mercury) is an affliction even from a kendra. `HTJAH-II:8176-8177`.
  - Lagna lord (e.g. Saturn) aspecting the Sun is "not an affliction in the strict sense" — exempt own-house/Lagna-lord aspects from the malefic-aspect penalty (Chart 93). `HTJAH-II:8367-8369`.
  - Mars aspect on its own depositee (9th lord in Mars' sign) **strengthens** rather than afflicts (Chart 94). Sign-lord aspect ≠ malefic aspect. `HTJAH-II:8419-8421`.
  - "These combinations are very general" — never emit a literal single-rule verdict; require multi-factor synthesis before a conclusion. `HTJAH-II:7396-7404, 7906-7912`.
